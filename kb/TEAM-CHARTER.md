# Project Odisha charter

**POD-CHR-001 · Candidate v0.4 · For final review**

## Purpose and audience

Build a credible, growing encyclopedia of Utkala · Odisha that serves residents, the diaspora, learners, researchers and curious visitors. Connect people with the region’s history, places, languages, cultural practices and contemporary opportunities through traceable knowledge and meaningful contribution.

## Scope and outcomes

The initial scope is public knowledge, credited photographs where rights permit, structured indexes, source trails and a readable website. Over time, investigate migration, language, trade, cultural connections and global footprints without assuming a particular ancestry or influence in advance. Distinguish evidence, testimony, interpretation and uncertainty.

Success begins with useful, well-supported entries that people can find, understand, correct and build upon. This first phase establishes the operating records and one locally reviewed entry preview; it does not claim a launched site or working public contribution system.

## Human authority and team

Ahimanikya Satapathy is the Founder & Editor-in-Chief, the only initial human and final internal authority. He decides appointments, access, commitments, publication and adoption. Future human delegation is explicit and bounded. Humans report only to humans; each AI assignment has a named human supervisor and no final approval authority.

## Relationships and independence

Project Odisha (POD) is the master coordination project and has its own knowledge mission. Odisha Collective and Odisha Unbound have independent lifecycles; precise legal structures are not established here. BAC and BAL remain deferred. Blueprint stewardship and encyclopedia delivery are distinct workstreams.

Editorial judgment is independent of commercial membership, payment or property ownership. Disclose relevant interests. Association with another organization does not confer authority over its people or resources.

## Baseline and publication

Adopt the shared operating model through the version-specific adoption record. Keep project-specific audience, scope, assignments and work here. Public knowledge and project records are separated from private personal information. Human approval does not substitute for a contributor’s rights or consent.
