# Chilika — free-footage film reviews

## Current candidate · water journey v2

[Play the 42.4-second film with sound](chilika-water-journey-review-v2.mp4).

Three actual recordings take us through reed-lined Mangalajodi channels, onto open water from a boat, and back to returning boats at sunset. Slow dissolves connect separate moments; this is an editorial sequence assembled from different recordings, not a continuous trip or a promise of current conditions. A newly composed synthetic ambient score accompanies the film. It contains no sampled recordings, imitated performer or claim of traditional Odia music.

Footage credits, including the creator’s original video titles, source links, licence and editing changes, appear in the ten-second closing card and [provenance record](provenance-v2.json). All three sources carry the Creative Commons Attribution reuse label. Source publication dates are 2016 and 2019; precise filming dates have not been independently verified.

- [Mangalajodi, Chilika Lake, Odisha](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCIHglqlJMc) — Pratap Padhi, published 7 June 2016. The 720p source is resized to the 1080p film canvas.
- [ferry ride @ Chilika lake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCnSeJtCOqw) — Ajit Satam, published 16 January 2019; 1080p source.
- [sunset at chilika lake.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EMHBwT5_8hc) — Ajit Satam, published 16 January 2019; 1080p source.

[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) applies to the source footage. Changes: selected excerpts, resizing where needed, opening and place titles, dissolves/fades, end credits and replacement of original audio with the new score. No AI-generated scenery, purchase or deployment. Founder direction: Ahimanikya Satapathy; editorial assembly and score preparation: AI assistant for Project Odisha.

An initially selected bird compilation was excluded because its description stated that some material was used under fair use; its underlying rights and filming locations could not be established. It does not appear in this film.

[Timed text](description-v2.vtt) supplies the visible headings, sound description and scene descriptions. Visual/audio descriptions are not spoken narration. Human listening and full-speed aesthetic review remain pending; technical validation is recorded in provenance.
