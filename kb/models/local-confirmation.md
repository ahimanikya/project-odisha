# Dated local confirmation

Use this alongside the [seven-guide queue](../research/destinations/coastal-verification-queue.json). A blank template is not a confirmation. One record covers one clearly defined claim; independent entry, access, event and transport claims may need different sources.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Guide and work | Exact page route; POD-WORK-097 or a linked specific work record. |
| Claim | Exact proposed visitor-facing wording and the site, room, dish, service or dated programme it describes. |
| Date and validity | Observation/contact date, date of the visit or event, and when the information must be checked again. |
| Source and role | Public source URL, or the consenting person's public name and relevant role. Do not place private contact details in Git. |
| Basis | Official published notice, direct statement, firsthand observation, or secondhand account. Record which. |
| Evidence | Public link or public-safe excerpt; separate approved permission reference for any restricted evidence. If no approved private evidence store exists, leave private material out of this repository. |
| Limits | What was not checked; conflicts, seasonal limitations, the particular entrance/room/category and any commercial interest. |
| Permission | Whether the person permits publication of the statement and their name/credit. A private conversation is not blanket reuse permission. |
| Editorial result | Proposed wording, retain uncertainty, needs corroboration, defer, or reject—with reason. |
| Human decision | Actual Founder decision and date; leave blank until received. |
| Publication | Actual approved change/PR and released page after publication; leave blank until it happens. |

Before describing a place as accessible, record the entire visitor approach and facilities actually checked. Before recommending a stay or kitchen, distinguish a provider's claim from an inspected experience. A dated online directory supplies a possible contact channel, not proof of a visit or service.

No outreach is authorised by this template. When the Founder supplies evidence or authorises a particular contact, use the existing public contribution and work records. Resolve contradictions visibly; do not overwrite the older source trail.
