- [ ] **Revise and independently review drafts.** Work one article at a time; integrate verified findings, improve story and citations, and obtain separate review. Fix blockers then rebuild affected preview.
- [ ] **Assemble user review package.** Verify local links, image-credit integrity, metadata and source hashes; record desktop/mobile checks as performed or outstanding; deliver remaining gaps and publication decision to user.

## Each cycle

Research → record evidence and omissions → revise one article → independent review → validate → update the queue.

The queue tracks source limits, attempted searches and unresolved dependencies. It does not promise that every perspective is accessible or equally supported.

## When we stop

The current draft scope is ready when material claims are supported or qualified, omissions are explicit, independent review finds no critical issue, and two final passes uncover no new material issue. We then return to you for editorial review. If only access, rights or human-input dependencies remain after three distinct attempts, we stop automatic retries and report those gaps.
