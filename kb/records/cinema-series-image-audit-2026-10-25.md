# Cinema series image audit · 25 October 2026

All eleven drafts have a designated, visible key image connected to the article, collection card and social-preview metadata. The collection contains 22 image placements: eleven generated title illustrations and eleven documentary placements using nine unique assets. Source photographs and artwork remain unchanged. This is an image audit, not publication approval.

## Corrections and outstanding work

- Replaced eight generic key-image descriptions with descriptions of what is visible. Clarified the Nandita montage caption.
- Refreshed DAMaN’s two stale copied provenance records through rebuilding; the underlying photograph was already correct.
- Preserve original media. Existing Commons crops, rendered book pages and commemorative artwork have distinct alteration/context notes; no artistic wash replaces an original photograph.
- **Performance remains open:** original files amount to approximately 58.3 MB when counted across article placements, including repeats. This is not a measured network transfer. AIR’s photograph is about 10.8 MB; all eleven title PNGs exceed 2 MB. Create smaller delivery versions and responsive sizes before publication, retaining originals and provenance.
- **Human Natural workflow evidence is incomplete:** seven early title-illustration records and DAMaN’s record lack an explicit skill/mode entry. Current visual review does not prove the skill was applied when they were generated. Do not backfill that history or claim blanket workflow compliance. AIR, television and OTT record their mode.
- **Art-direction priorities:** simplify Nandita’s overloaded floating-page/map montage; make Sadhu’s generic theatre-chair cover more specific; reconsider the cinema overview’s picturesque settlement/temple imagery. DAMaN’s polished path is a secondary concern because it can romanticise access difficulties. These are improvements to consider, not documentary errors or instructions to replace original photographs.
- The series shares warm painted interiors, empty chairs, paper and film props. This gives continuity but limits visual variety. Future replacements should respond to a specific story need, not regenerate photographs to match a style.
- No reusable actual film still is currently embedded. Manmohan’s portrait and documentary television/OTT imagery remain acquisition gaps. Public accessibility is not a public-domain licence.
- Browser desktop/mobile layout and live social-card rendering remain untested. The audit checks image pixels, files and generated markup; it does not substitute for those tests.
