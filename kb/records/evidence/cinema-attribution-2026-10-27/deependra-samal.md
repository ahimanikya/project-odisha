# Deependra Samal: making room for a different film

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

A producer’s contribution can begin before there is a screenplay to finance. In Vishal Mourya’s account of how *ଦମନ (DAMaN)* came together, Deependra Samal asked the directors to make something distinctive. They returned to an experience that had stayed with them: a documentary assignment in Malkangiri, where they had encountered the daily work of taking healthcare to distant villages.[^making]

That request became part of the beginning of an Odia film that would receive national recognition. Samal produced *DAMaN* under JP Motion Pictures; the CBFC’s certification record identifies him by name.[^certificate] His place in the story is worth understanding alongside the directors, performers and technicians whose work reached the screen.

## A producer in the conversation

The directors’ public recollections describe more than financial backing. In their interview with *The Federal*, they credited Samal with constructive feedback and an understanding of what the project needed.[^conversation] This gives us a small but meaningful view of the collaboration: producing could involve listening to an idea and helping the filmmakers develop it.

It also connects Samal’s work to a larger question for Odia cinema. How can a producer make room for a story rooted in a particular place while giving it a chance to find an audience? *DAMaN* provides one concrete example around which to ask that question. It does not supply a formula that every subsequent film must follow.

## Recognition for a shared work

On 16 August 2024, *DAMaN* was announced as Best Odia Film at the 70th National Film Awards, honouring films of 2022. The official award entry names JP Motion Pictures as producer and Vishal Mourya and Debi Prasad Lenka as directors.[^award]

The distinction belongs to a film made through many contributions. Naming its producer helps complete that record. Readers can follow the connected [DAMaN story](../culture/cinema-series/daman.md) for the actors, local performers, healthcare context and questions of representation that also belong to its history.

## The work continues

Samal’s public production credits extend to *ପରବ (Parab)*. Its colourist Sriprasad Sahoo’s project announcement names him as producer, Chinmay Das as director and Anu Choudhury as co-producer, again under JP Motion Pictures.[^parab] This brings another creative team into view rather than treating one successful collaboration as the whole of his career.

There is more to explore about how Samal chooses projects, builds teams and balances a film’s ambitions with its resources. His documented work already gives this series a useful starting point: the person who helps a story become a production, and then has to consider what can be made next.
