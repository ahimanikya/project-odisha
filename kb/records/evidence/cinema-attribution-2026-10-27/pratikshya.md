# ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା (Pratikshya): a family waiting for security

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

A secure job can become more than employment. It can stand for a family’s hopes, the ability to repay a loan and relief from being dependent on someone else. **ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା (Pratikshya)** places that desire beside a father’s illness, until the son’s hope for security becomes entangled with the possibility of loss.

The Film Bazaar viewing-room synopsis locates the story in a middle-class Bhubaneswar household. Sanjay wants a government job; his father Bipin approaches retirement while the family carries wedding debt. Illness changes the meaning of their waiting.[^bazaar]

## A writer, a director and a leading actor

The official IFFI account identifies a short story by **Gourahari Das** as the film’s inspiration. **Anupam Patnaik** directed it; **Dipanwit Dashmohapatra**, playing Sanjay, described this as his first lead role.[^iffi] These connections make the film a useful meeting point between contemporary Odia writing and performance.

Our [Gourahari Das profile](../../people/gourahari-das.md) follows his fiction and translations beyond this adaptation. The adaptation offers a way back to the writer’s other fictional worlds.

## The pressure inside a household

The Film Bazaar synopsis describes Sanjay learning of compassionate appointment after an employee’s death. The prospect distorts his hopes for his father.[^bazaar]

The discomfort of that premise gives viewers room to think about the relationship between affection and economic pressure. A household can contain care, resentment and dependence at the same time. The film’s premise holds those feelings together, making security a question about relationships as well as income.

## A film’s development and its recognition

The development-stage Film Bazaar entry credits **Roshan Bisoi** as writer and gives April 2021 as a tentative completion date, rather than a release date.[^bazaar] The credit places screenwriting beside the source story in the film’s creative history.

*Pratikshya* later received **Best Odia Film at the 69th National Film Awards**, announced in August 2023 for the **2021 award year**. The result names **Amiya Patnaik Productions** and **Anupam Patnaik** as producer and director recipients.[^award]
