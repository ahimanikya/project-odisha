Looking again

I began with a shawl. The reading brought me to people whose names I now want to remember.

Site Jakesika’s embroidery, Mukteswar Wadaka’s teaching and the work of keeping seeds in circulation belong to different lives. Bringing them into one book does not make those lives the same. Each asks for its own attention.

The cloth that first delighted me still does. But I look at it differently now. Alongside colour and pattern, I think about time: the hours spent making, the work fitted around other work, and the knowledge passed between people. Beauty has become a reason to pay closer attention.

There is much more to learn. I hope to keep asking, listening and revising what I think I know.

If this book leaves you curious, I hope you follow that curiosity towards the people and their work. Remember a name. Look again at a familiar object. Ask where it came from and how it was made.

I bought a shawl that evening. I am still learning how much there is to see in it.
