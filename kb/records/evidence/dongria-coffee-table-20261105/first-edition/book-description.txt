Dongria Kondh: Life in Niyamgiri
Ahimanikya Satapathy

A shawl encountered at a dinner table led Ahimanikya Satapathy into weeks of reading about the people and place behind it. This illustrated introduction follows life in Niyamgiri through twelve connected chapters: hills and homes, seed keeping, food and markets, cloth and ornaments, education, language and song.

Published research and attributed accounts bring individual makers, farmers, learners and singers into view. Photographs, illustrations, a locator and a short glossary accompany the stories, while compact source notes retain the dates and contexts of the evidence.

Written before the author’s first visit, the book offers a starting point for looking more closely at the relationships between people, knowledge and place.
