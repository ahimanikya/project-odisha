PHOTO DELIVERY NOTES
Dongria Kondh: Life in Niyamgiri |6 November2026

Please send camera-original files in a Drive folder or as uncompressed documents, not compressed chat images. Keep original filenames. JPEG plus RAW if available; no filters or generated details. Photograph both the wider setting and useful close details, with vertical and horizontal options.

For each selected image, supply:
- Original filename and photographer name/credit.
- Date and actual village/town; exact coordinates are not needed for publication.
- Names as the people wish to be identified, with spellings checked; do not guess identities or relationships.
- A plain description of what is happening, naming materials or processes only when confirmed.
- Agreement to photograph and to use the image in this book and its digital edition; record any restrictions or wishes about names/captions. For children, obtain appropriate guardian agreement and the child's assent.
- Photographer's permission/licence for the proposed uses, any agreed fee and required credit. Record payment separately from permission.

New photographs may replace a visual subject, but do not become photographs of the named people or historical events in the old captions. We will write new captions. Photograph festival or ritual activity only when it actually occurs and photography is welcomed; do not stage it to match the book.

Keep Kotpad dyeing/weaving separate from Dongria embroidery. Do not ask someone in Niyamgiri to stand in for a Kotpad artisan. Primer cover reproduction remains a publisher-permission question; taking a new picture of a book cover does not settle that question.
