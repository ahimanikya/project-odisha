# Companion social post

Before a Maniabandha sari reaches the loom, its pattern has already begun—in carefully tied and dyed yarn.

In Odisha’s Cuttack district, Maniabandha’s weavers carry forward the bandha tradition alongside a cultural heritage shaped by Buddhist and Jagannath influences. The village was recognised in the crafts category of India’s Best Tourism Villages Competition in 2024. [Odisha Tourism](https://odishatourism.gov.in/en/places/maniabandha-handloom-village).

Its next chapter deserves our attention: textile tourism that gives visitors time to understand the work and gives artisans a stronger voice in how their village is experienced.

Plan a visit with a local weaver or group. Ask how the yarn becomes a pattern. Respect working time, seek permission before taking photographs, and buy with an understanding of the skill involved.

When you choose a handwoven textile, ask who made it. Remembering the maker is a good place to begin.
