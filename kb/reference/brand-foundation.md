# Earth, sea and a shared story

Version 0.1 · Founder review candidate · 28 September 2026 UTC

**Project Odisha** · **Discover Odisha. Imagine the possibilities.**

The name and tagline are settled. The colour families and Mankada's dominant role follow the Founder's directions. The precise digital colours, refined symbol, typography pairing and newly generated illustration remain working design proposals.

| Material name | Odia | Working colours | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mankada Pathara | ମାଙ୍କଡ଼ା ପଥର | `#91462F` | Dominant identity, headings and primary actions |

Paper `#F2E6D0`, a lighter reading surface `#FBF6EC` and ink `#302E28` support the three colour families. Straw is an accent, not a small-text colour on pale paper. Live labels use the corrected Mankada name; older records retain the original discussion.

## Logo recommendation

Keep the Founder's preferred open-book-becoming-a-sailboat idea. The book suggests collected knowledge; the boat suggests journeys and connection. This is a contemporary design interpretation, not an assertion that the symbol is a historic emblem.

**A · Bilingual literary — recommended:** live `ଉତ୍କଳ` in Noto Serif Odia, with the full English name in a serif face. This connects the Odia identity to a readable international name.

**B · Bilingual contemporary:** Noto Sans Odia with the same English serif. Compare the shape and weight of the Odia letters directly.

**C · English compact:** the full English name with the symbol, useful in compact or international contexts. It need not replace the bilingual masthead.

The local studio is at `/brand-review/`; the homepage demonstrates A. These are real font specimens, not generated image text. English currently uses system Georgia, so rendering can vary between operating systems. Noto fonts are bundled unchanged with their OFL notices.

The raster symbol is a concept. Its dark-background proof reveals that the sea-coloured hull loses contrast; a dedicated one-colour/reversed master and optical small-size correction are required before production. Do not treat this as a finished trademark or final asset pack.

## The story to keep

The palette emerged through conversation, not a palette generator alone. Ahimanikya supplied stone, sea, dry straw and muted olive as the material references, connected Sukha Ghasi with cow dung and paddy straw used as cooking fuel, and insisted that the stone colour define the brand. The correction from Kankada to Mankada made the name more precise while preserving the path we took to it.

That is the memory worth keeping: the Founder recognizing the feel of Odisha in ordinary materials, and asking that the website carry it forward. We do not add an invented childhood scene, ancestral anecdote or historic colour claim. See the preserved [colour origin story](../history/stories/001-earth-sea-and-sukha-ghasi.md).

The Founder also reported many discussions with retired IAS officer Pradip Biswal and his keenness to help. The [relationship story](../history/stories/002-early-conversations.md) records that interest separately; it is not a formal appointment or endorsement.

## Decision to record next

Choose A, B or C as the default; confirm or refine the book/boat proportions; and confirm the balance of illustrated welcome and quiet reading. Final vector cleanup, a reverse version, favicon optical adjustment and share-card exports follow that choice. These later assets are not yet delivered.
