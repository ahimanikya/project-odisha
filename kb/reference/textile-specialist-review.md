## A conversation with a maker

Use the existing [dated local confirmation form](../models/local-confirmation.md) for each claim that might enter a story. Begin with the maker’s preferred public name, work and locality. Ask which stages they perform, who else contributes, where materials come from, and what permission they give for credit, quotations and photographs.

For a visit, record the precise public address, who can welcome a visitor, the date checked, appointment requirements, fees, photography rules and what can actually be seen. For a purchase, ask about the specific piece’s fibre or blend, dye and finish, care, price and receipt. A tradition’s reputation does not certify an individual product. No visit, sale, interview or outreach is authorised by this sheet.
