# Made in Odisha

Discover the things Odisha grows, weaves and makes—and follow the people and places behind them. This collection joins craft, food and enterprise research. It is an editorial collection, not a certification label or a verified supplier directory.

## The people behind the cloth

**53,472 workers** — Handloom weavers. 2019–20 Handloom Census; Odisha.[^connect-handloom2019]

The same 2019–20 census records **64,364 allied workers**, a separate category. These historical counts make the scale of work visible; they do not establish today’s workforce, wages or growth.[^connect-handloom2019]

## Food enterprises deserve their own evidence

A named dish and a named business are different records. Expand the collection with consenting cooks, processors, cooperatives and entrepreneurs. Establish who makes the product, where production occurs, how ingredients are sourced and which claims the business can substantiate. Track dated sales or production only when the units and geographic scope are clear. No verified food-enterprise sales directory is established yet.

## Connect making with the wider economy

Handlooms and food belong alongside manufacturing, logistics, finance and skills. The [industrial production](../statistics/industrial-production.md) and [exports](../trade/merchandise-exports.md) pages provide wider context; company-wide results must not be relabelled as Odisha-only production. [Progress cards](progress.md) separate proposed industrial capacity from realised output.

## The next evidence to seek

Maker income and working conditions, producer ownership, buyer access, production volumes, provenance and a comparable time series. A GI registration history does not certify every seller or establish current registry validity; check the named product and relevant registry before publishing a certification claim.
