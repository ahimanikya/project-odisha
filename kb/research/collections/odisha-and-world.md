### Case 1 · A gateway measured in cargo

**12,711 TEUs of EXIM container traffic in 2024–25.** Paradip’s 2 April 2025 release supplies this distinct measure alongside its cargo totals. TEUs are container-size equivalents, not tonnes or export value. The release also notes weak export demand for iron ore and coking coal. [Original release](https://paradipport.gov.in/all_news/paradip-port-cements-its-position-as-numero-uno-among-indian-major-ports-second-consecutive-time-in-a-row/).

Reuse the [ports evidence](../statistics/ports.md) for total and coastal cargo and the later FY2025–26 position. A dated ranking should not become a permanent national-superiority claim. Our inference is that Paradip provides a logistics platform; these totals alone cannot establish which Odisha producer sold to which foreign buyer.

### Case 2 · A processing base within an international policy relationship

The **16 November 2012 memorandum** names IREL and Toyota Tsusho and expresses stable-supply and Indian value-addition aims. Commercial terms and a joint venture were envisaged, not demonstrated as completed by that document. [Signed memorandum, clauses 1–5](https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/LegalTreatiesDoc/JP12B0296.pdf).

A **31 March 2022 parliamentary answer** distinguishes its wording “no pact” from a company agreement to supply mixed rare-earth chloride. Its ore-mining table must not be read as rare-earth output or Japan exports. [Question 3202, answer (c)–(d)](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s35b8e4fd39d9786228649a8a8bec4e008/uploads/2023/02/2023020889-1.pdf).

The [existing Odisha plant record](../journeys/rare-earths.md) supplies the location and capacity link. Capacity in mixed chloride is different from refined oxides, magnets and exports. The saved 2026 corridor discussions remain proposals. This is an institutional connection, not proof that every processing stage or a Japan-bound shipment took place in Odisha.

Current continuity is **unverified**. A June 2025 [IBEF secondary report](https://www.ibef.org/news/india-moves-to-conserve-rare-earths-seeks-halt-to-exports-to-japan) describes a request to suspend exports; its original directive and later status remain unresolved. We therefore do not describe the earlier agreement as operating today or adopt that report’s shipment totals.
