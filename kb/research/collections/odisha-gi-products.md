# Odisha’s GI products: the complete dated registry list

Odisha’s official list brings together cloth, paintings, metalwork, stone carving, foods, crops and fragrance. **26 registry entries represent 25 named non-logo entries and one separate Pattachitra logo entry.** This is a count of the Odisha rows in IP India’s list **as of 31 December 2025**, not a claim that every entry’s present legal validity has been independently certified.[^gi-state-register2025]

The list is dated even though its download page was published later. Its dates are **filing dates**, not the dates GI certificates were issued. We checked all **28 application numbers** behind these 26 rows on **4 October 2026**. Pipli and Konark each group two application numbers; their merged applications do not add two more products. “Orissa” is retained where it is part of the registered name.[^gi-live-register2026]

## From recognition to livelihoods

GI registration alone does not measure sales, exports, maker income or demand. Product-specific follow-up should establish the specification and production area, registered proprietor and authorized users, credited communities and processes, then dated production, prices, domestic sales and export evidence. Unknown values remain blank. Existing profiles are enhanced rather than recreated.
