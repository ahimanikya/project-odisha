# Tourism experience desk shortlist — 22 leads

This is a desk shortlist of **22 leads**, drawn from existing project research: seven in Bhubaneswar, six in Cuttack and nine across Puri district. We stopped before 30 because these provide enough variety for a first selection. Every proposed experience still needs a willing operator or institution and current operating checks. **No host is confirmed; no experience is bookable through this pack.**

The selection favours a cultural story, an existing evidence trail, a manageable visit and potential benefit to local makers. It is editorial judgment, not a scored quality ranking. Seasonal fairs and performances are held separately. Puri district includes Pipili, Raghurajpur, Nimapada and Konark; these are not all Puri city or one walking route. Food and craft ideas can overlap with place visits and must not be counted as distinct businesses.

## Proposed first six

Kala Bhoomi and Dhauli; Cuttack Tarakasi and the Maritime Museum; Raghurajpur and Pipili. Together they test institutional access, heritage interpretation and paid maker time. Three field days with two appointments each is a capacity assumption, not a verified itinerary. Confirm appointments and travel before pairing stops. If the museum is unavailable, investigate the Netaji Birthplace Museum; if a workshop declines, use another willing maker or reduce scope.

## What the coordinator must return

For each candidate: a consenting contact and role, proposed visit format, date and capacity, total price and who receives it, entry/photography rules, language support, access and toilets, water/dietary needs where relevant, cancellations/weather fallback, photo rights and the date checked. Record refusals and “not known” without filling gaps with assumptions. Do not put private contact details, identity or bank documents in the public repository.

The [structured shortlist](../references/data/tourism-experience-shortlist.json) retains original source IDs, URLs, source-check metadata and KB file hashes. Reusing those records does not refresh their original source checks. The [experience record](../../models/tourism-experience-record.md) is the fuller confirmation template.
