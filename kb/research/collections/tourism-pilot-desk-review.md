# Tourism pilot — first research batch

The first desk batch turns the approved idea into a selection and funding decision: **22 experience leads, six recommended first visits and two cost scenarios**. The new official-document checks distinguish event incentives from capital investment support. No fieldwork, host confirmation, appointment, payment, application or external outreach has occurred.

## Read the pack

1. [Official tourism evidence](tourism-policy-opportunity.md): dated notifications and clause/page references, a government-linked OTIIMS address and remaining application-workflow questions.
2. [Twenty-two candidate experiences](tourism-experience-shortlist.md): every lead traces to the existing KB, retains its evidence limits and names its missing operating information.
3. [People and budget](../../specs/tourism-pilot-capacity.md): a six-experience start at ₹59,340 or twenty at ₹157,090, based on explicit planning allowances rather than quotations.

## Recommended starting choice

Investigate **Kala Bhoomi, Dhauli, Cuttack Tarakasi, the Maritime Museum, Raghurajpur and Pipili** first. They test three different needs: institutional permission, interpretation of a place, and compensation/consent for a maker’s time. The selection is a proposal, not evidence that any organisation has agreed to participate.

Start with six and learn before aiming for twenty. The three-day field assumption needs appointment and route checks. A smaller result with honest refusals and gaps is more useful than a full list of nominal partners.
