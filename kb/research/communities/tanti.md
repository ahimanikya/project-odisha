# Tanti: weaving and community histories

The [canonical narrative](tanti.json) compares a dated nineteenth-century name/occupation table, the Bhadrak Gazetteer’s regional Aswini Tanti account and Gunjan Jain’s named Bomkai Patra Tanti weaver account from 2015. The [source map](../sources/tanti-research.json) records exact locators, preview access and claim limits. Existing textile research was searched first; technique and surname do not establish community identity.

No universal founder, earliest attestation, exhaustive branch map, contemporary ritual survey or equivalence between Tanti, Pantanti and all weaving communities is established. The sources’ caste-ranking assumptions are not adopted. No Odia spelling or additional alias is asserted without verification.

Text-first: existing Khandua imagery has rights, but no verified Tanti-specific maker connection. No photograph is assigned a caste identity. No fieldwork or human community review is claimed.
