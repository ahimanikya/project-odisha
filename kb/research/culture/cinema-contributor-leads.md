# Odia cinema: six contributor research leads

These are gaps in our current series, not a ranking of artists or a claim that their work is generally overlooked. The selection favours distinct crafts and sources we could inspect. It is a research shortlist, not six finished biographies.

| Research lead | What it brings into the series | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lipika Singh Darai | Documentary filmmaking, editing and sound; a connection to puppetry through Backstage | Start with her own credits, then compare film-end credits and festival records where they disagree. |
| Bijaya Jena | Producing, directing, performing and shared screenwriting on Tara | Build a credit-led account that keeps collaborators visible; verify award and release chronology separately. |
| A. K. Bir / Apurba Kishore Bir | Cinematography and work across different screen forms | Recover the full early catalogue page supporting his Odisha connection before a definitive biography. |
| Pramod Pati | Experimental shorts, animation and work within public film institutions | Examine selected works and institutional context without adopting “father” or “first” labels. |
| Prafulla Kar | Film music and recording-level distinctions between composer, lyricist and singer | Enhance the existing profile and Mamata/song records; do not create another profile. |
| Bijay Mishra | Screenwriting and dialogue, theatre connections and collaborative direction | Begin with his published testimony and check original credits before writing a full account. |

## Sources and reuse

- Lipika: [filmmaker’s own filmography](https://www.lipikasinghdarai.com/films) and [IFFR profile](https://iffr.com/en/person/lipika-singh-darai). Some dates and credits disagree; do not silently choose the more impressive credit.
- Bijaya and Bir: [39th National Film Awards catalogue](https://nfaindia.org/images/pdf/39th%20National%20Film%20Award%20Catalogue.pdf), inspected pages 149 and 25. Bir’s earlier [21st catalogue](https://dff.nic.in/images/Documents/95_21stNfacatalogue.pdf) remains indexed-text evidence in this pass.
- Pramod: [Films Division retrospective announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1690373&lang=2&reg=48) and [Golaka Bihari Singh’s Odisha Review essay](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/2021/Feb-Mar/engpdf/64-67.pdf). A retrospective is not a contemporaneous personnel file.
- Prafulla: reuse [existing profile](../people/prafulla-kar.md), [Mamata record](../works/mamata-film-music.md) and Kapilas Bhuyan’s [film-history retrospective](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=67399).
- Bijay: [Bijay Mandal’s 2019 interview](https://www.orissapost.com/i-celebrate-failures-too-says-thespian-bijay-mishra/). His recollections and opinions remain attributed testimony.

## What this selection still misses

Exhibition, distribution, less-documented crews, wider popular cinema and regional language ecosystems need more work. Two women in a shortlist do not establish representative coverage. No complete film viewing, award census, photograph-reuse permission or community consultation is claimed.

The next article-development priority is **Lipika Singh Darai**, followed by **Bijaya Jena**. This recommends future work; the current fairness loop still needs to finish regional framing, reception and representation checks on the eight existing drafts.
