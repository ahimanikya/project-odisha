# ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା (Pratikshya): a family waiting for security

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Four applicants sit shoulder to shoulder, books and papers on their laps. In the official trailer for **ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା (Pratikshya)**, Sanjay turns towards the woman beside him while the two men on his other side keep looking down at their reading material. The row fills the frame. His search for a job already has company: other people, other pages, another turn to wait for.[^ensemble]

The Film Bazaar viewing-room synopsis locates the story in a middle-class Bhubaneswar household. Sanjay wants a government job; his father Bipin approaches retirement while the family carries wedding debt. Illness changes the meaning of their waiting.[^bazaar]

## A writer, a director and a leading actor

The official IFFI account identifies a short story by **Gourahari Das** as the film’s inspiration. [Anupam Patnaik](../../people/anupam-patnaik.md) directed it; **Dipanwit Dashmohapatra**, playing Sanjay, described this as his first lead role.[^iffi]

Patnaik’s [profile](../../people/anupam-patnaik.md) follows the work that led to the film. The [Gourahari Das profile](../../people/gourahari-das.md) opens a path to the writer’s fiction beyond this adaptation.

Alongside Dipanwit Dashmohapatra, the ensemble includes **Choudhury Jayprakash Das, Barsha Patnaik, Sreela Tripathy and Roopambika Nayak**.[^ensemble]

## The pressure inside a household

The Film Bazaar synopsis describes Sanjay learning of compassionate appointment after an employee’s death. The prospect distorts his hopes for his father.[^bazaar]

The discomfort of that premise gives viewers room to think about the relationship between affection and economic pressure. A household can contain care, resentment and dependence at the same time. The film’s premise holds those feelings together, making security a question about relationships as well as income.

## A film’s development and its recognition

The Film Bazaar entry credits **Roshan Bisoi** as writer, placing screenwriting beside the source story in the film’s creative history.[^bazaar]

**Deepak Kumar’s cinematography** and **Deven Mishra’s editing** helped bring that writing to the screen. **Barsha Patnaik** contributed production and costume design; **Ankesh Anand and Ashish Pradhan** composed the music and background score, with **Bivash Rath** composing one song.[^crew]

*Pratikshya* later received **Best Odia Film at the 69th National Film Awards**, announced in August 2023 for the **2021 award year**. The result names **Amiya Patnaik Productions** and **Anupam Patnaik** as producer and director recipients.[^award]
