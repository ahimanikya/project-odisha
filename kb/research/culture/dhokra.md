# Dhokra: a wax pattern becomes metal

The Dhenkanal process documented by NID builds a clay core, adds a wax pattern and encloses it in a mould. Heat removes the wax; poured metal takes its place. Removing the mould and finishing reveals the object. This explains the recorded process without prescribing an alloy formula or inferring an uninterrupted link with Indus-period objects.

## A named maker

[Sansari Gadatia](../people/sansari-gadatia.md) is credited in the NID documentation and appears in the state award register for **2006**. This is state recognition, not a national award. The documents do not establish current earnings or availability.

## From craft to transaction: an unfinished link

A [SIDAC proceedings lead](../sources/arts-sidac-selection2017-lead.md) points to a2017 artefact-selection process. Its indexed table distinguishes an owner’s asking price, negotiations and committee decisions, but columns appear displaced and the original could not be retrieved. No numbers are adopted. Selection, acquisition, payment and maker authorship must be established separately.
