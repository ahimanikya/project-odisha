# People and teams behind Odisha’s storytelling institutions

This register credits identifiable work that enabled cinema, radio, television and streaming. It is neither a ranking nor a claim that everyone here is unknown. A famous person can have a less-discussed contribution; an unnamed crew should not disappear behind its leader. These are research entries, not finished biographies or a representative workforce census.

## Production and community support

See [Parbati Ghose’s article](cinema-series/parbati-ghose.md) for production credits and the [cinema overview](odia-cinema.md) for the crew and location-community acknowledgements. The [evidence dossier](../sources/storytelling-builders-evidence-2026-10-25.json) retains source-access limits.

This example warrants a closer history of how a film unit worked with a location community. It does not yet establish each resident’s role, remuneration or experience. Future work should seek production records and participant accounts, with consent for any interviews.

## Radio and television

**P.V. Krishnamoorthy:** Shyamanuja Das’s 2020 retrospective credits the AIR Cuttack station director with encouraging lighter music and bringing musicians and lyricists together. Das draws on published recollections and conversations with artists. This supports an attributed account of programme leadership, not the claim that one administrator created modern Odia song. [Source](https://odishabytes.com/pv-krishnamoorthy-the-unsung-force-behind-modern-odia-songs/)

**Biren Das:** Mrinal Chatterjee credits him with producing the 1995 news magazine *Ajira Odisha*. That is a specific independent-production contribution worth following, rather than a basis for an unsupported “first” label. [Source](https://www.osou.ac.in/eresources/television-in-odisha.pdf), p.2.

**Sanjay Pathak, Satya Rath and the initial ETV programming team:** Participant Fakira Mohan Nahak describes the preparation of a visual library and programmes before launch, with guidance from colleagues in other language channels. His account identifies Pathak and Rath with the Bhubaneswar team. [Source](https://odisha.plus/2022/02/rome-wasnt-built-in-a-day-by-fakira-mohan-nahakthe-journey-of-television-in-odisha-part-2/)

**Akshay Mohanty, broadcast engineer, and the live-production team:** Nahak’s account of the 12 July 2002 Rath Yatra telecast names Pathak leading the Puri field team, Mohanty handling the technical system, Amitabh Bhattashali leading the Hyderabad team and Vijay Krishna handling panel production. Nahak also recalls coordination with graphics staff. Credit belongs to that distributed operation, including workers the account does not name. The engineer must not be conflated with composer Akshaya Mohanty. [Source](https://odisha.plus/2022/02/live-broadcast-rathyatra-and-etv/)

These participant recollections are valuable but are not contemporaneous personnel or transmission logs. No complete programme viewing or project interview is claimed.

## Audiences and preservation

**Nirad Mohapatra:** His memorial biography records Cinexstasy screenings and film-appreciation teaching alongside filmmaking. [Source](https://niradmohapatra.com/about-me/). The [cinema article](odia-cinema.md) now credits this educational work.

**Film Heritage Foundation and its partners:** The foundation’s restoration account names its director Shivendra Singh Dungarpur and credits the laboratories, filmmaker’s family and archival materials involved in *Maya Miriga*. The detailed [evidence record](../sources/storytelling-builders-evidence-2026-10-25.json) preserves their distinct roles. No single-person rescue claim is made.

**Film Society Bhubaneswar:** Its January 2025 festival closed with the restored *Maya Miriga*, with FHF support. Presenting restored cinema to an audience is another contribution, distinct from performing the restoration. [Report](https://www.orissapost.com/7-day-iffb-concludes-with-screening-of-restored-odia-classic-maya-miriga/), 29 January 2025.

## Public investment needs a chronology

The flagship now distinguishes Naveen Patnaik’s 2021 rebuilding foundation ceremony, IDCO’s assigned implementation and the R&B Department’s renovation work from Mohan Charan Majhi’s 2026 inauguration. See the [evidence and official records](../sources/storytelling-builders-evidence-2026-10-25.json). Ceremonial, policy, administrative and technical responsibilities are different contributions. Announced jobs, budgets or completion deadlines are not proof of outcomes.

Earlier Kalinga Studio foundation and policy histories contain conflicting retrospective dates. Attribution to an individual political founder remains open pending contemporary administrative evidence. Publicly prominent politicians are included as accountable office-holders, not relabelled as forgotten workers.

## OTT and the next gaps

The [OTT article](odia-ott.md) already credits Kaushik Das, Bibhu Prasad Rath, Akshay Parija and CR Nayak for the platform work supported by their interviews. Susant Mani, Tamanna Vyas and Divyadisha Mohanty appear through a concrete creative project. Founder testimony remains distinct from independently verified business outcomes.

The next gap is specific non-founder work: commissioning editors, subtitlers, developers, sound and post-production teams, and distribution staff. Unverified staff affiliations are retained only as unresolved leads in the evidence dossier; no role is assigned without support.

## Research priorities

1. Recover original screen credits and programme logs for the named examples.
2. Identify projectionists, transmission engineers and exhibition workers through credited records.
3. Extend women’s and community participants’ accounts beyond the small selection available here.
4. Verify early studio financing and policy decisions through dated files, not commemorative praise.
5. Seek OTT subtitle and technical credits before building further profiles.
