# What the Osha collection still needs

The 39 profiles are a selected collection, not a complete calendar. Ten have narrative checkpoints; 29 remain early research notes. This audit checks which named entries already have a reading route and which still need research. It does not supply new origins, local menus or visitor offers.

## What was checked

Complete transcription of named entries in the opening Osha/Brata/Mela inventory of Dash2006, plus six selected gaps in Mahanti printed43–53. Not an exhaustive inventory of either all book chapters or Odisha practice.
Dash says at least18 Oshas and32 Bratas. This register counts printed name entries under a documented segmentation rule, not unique observances; introductory estimates are not used as an exhaustive denominator.
Each separately introduced inventory name is one row; explicit or-aliases stay in one row; Dutiya/Puajiuntia stays one source row with two profile pointers; Sumbadasami/Sambara and Rai Damodara/Kartika remain separate source entries without asserting separate festivals.

The opening inventory yields 54 source entries: 16 under Osha, 31 under Brata and 7 under Mela. These are text-entry counts, not statewide festival totals. Eight Brata entries lack dedicated profiles; six additional Mahanti headings are retained as leads.

## Next: Sankata Chaturthi

Dash’s food paragraph prints “Bundi”, while its note17 prints “Bindi”. This is a transcription and identity question, not permission to substitute a familiar sweet recipe. The food must also remain separate from Nagala Chaturthi.

Begin with the saved original pages, then seek independently attributable Odisha textual or institutional evidence for missing fields.
