# Which Osha? Names, full moons and food traditions

A full-moon name is a starting point, not a complete identification. For a seasonal food guide, the month, locality and particular observance matter: an offering at Kumar Purnima cannot simply become the menu for Pandu Purnima.

## Two inventory names still need an identity

Pratap Kumar Dash’s 2006 list names Benatia Osha and Purnima Osha without separate descriptions. The original page does not supply their stories, months or foods. A parenthesis mentioning Kartika and Shiva follows Bada Osha; it is not used here to fill the preceding entries. Benatia’s Odia spelling, locality and possible aliases remain unverified.

Source: [The Perishing Tradition of Osha, Brata and Mela in Orissa: An Overview](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/April2006/engpdf/The_perishing_tradition_of_osha.pdf). Printed15 / PDF1, right column.

## Why the full-moon label is not enough

Mahanti separately names Aswina’s Kumara Purnima and Margasira’s Pandu Purnima. The saved collection also covers the Kartika/Rai Damodara vow. These give readers distinct routes into seasonal practice, but do not identify which observance Dash intended by the unqualified Purnima Osha entry.

## Stories and histories stay with their named observance

Follow the linked profiles for their attributed narratives and dating limits. Shared moon worship, a similar offering shape or a repeated word does not establish a common origin. This guide does not create a combined Purnima story or assign a history to Benatia.

## What to ask before looking for seasonal food

Record the local name and month first, then distinguish an offering from a fast-breaking meal, a game material or ritual drawing paste. A participant’s account should retain its locality and permission to share. Current annual dates, public access, host consent and serving venues need separate evidence; none is established by this guide.

## Research still open

Benatia needs an identifiable Odia textual or local account. The intended identity of the unqualified Purnima entry also remains open. A June 2013 Odisha Review search result repeats both names, but the issue could not be retrieved in this pass. Its independence from the earlier list is unestablished.
