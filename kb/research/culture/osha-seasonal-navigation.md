# Reading the observance year without inventing dates

A month helps a reader find a story. It does not by itself tell a visitor when a household will observe a vow, what will be offered, or where food will be served. This guide reorganises the existing 53 profiles using their saved source accounts; it adds no observance and no annual date. The [structured navigation record](../references/data/osha-seasonal-navigation.json) retains the source wording, section locators, unresolved alternatives and food-role notes.

## Browse the saved month labels

These are editorial reading groups. Bhadrava and Bhadrab are grouped under Bhadraba; Aswina under Ashwina. The original labels remain in each profile. No fixed Gregorian range or weather season is assigned to the lunar months. A month can be usable for browsing even when the lunar day or fast-breaking rule is unresolved.

A recurring condition can also have conflicting published descriptions. Sudasa and Rabinarayana therefore keep both the recurrence condition and the disagreement. Gouri, Guru Panchami and Sankata need identity work before their alternative labels become ordinary month filters. Bengali Bipattarini and South Indian Skanda comparisons do not supply Odisha calendar fields. Raja is kept under its solar transition; its separately saved June association is an enquiry cue, not a date for every year.

## Ask what role the food has

A food name is only the beginning. [Dutiya](osha/dutiya.md) describes vegetables offered and later cooked into ghanta; [Sathi](osha/sathi.md) documents an offering eaten by one family. [Bali Trutiya](osha/bali-trutiya.md) distinguishes fruit offered in instructions from forest fruit eaten by Parvati within its story. [Kanji Anla](osha/kanji-anla.md) separates prepared offerings from fish arranged as ritual figures. Those roles should stay beside the food names when readers search.

Some apparent menus belong elsewhere. [Kukkuti](osha/kukkuti-brata.md) and [Shiva Pradosha](osha/shiva-pradosha-brata.md) have textual food comparisons without an established Odisha household menu. [Tarini](osha/tarini-brata.md) has shrine food evidence that cannot fill the household vow’s blank. [Guru Purnima](osha/guru-purnima-brata.md) includes reported prasad and a dated announcement, which do not verify present service. An empty food field means more evidence is needed.

The navigation data supplies one food-role note for each profile and retains its original food terms. These notes are editorial summaries of the linked evidence, not recipes, universal menus or proof of current availability.
