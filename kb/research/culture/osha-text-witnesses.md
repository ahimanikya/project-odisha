# Osha texts and manuscript references

Sudasa, Budhei, Dutiya, Nagala, Rabinarayana and Somnath have identifiable catalogue references at the Odisha State Museum. The selected register now contains thirty-two catalogue records, including four comparative Varanasi records, and five recovered digital texts: two Sudasa, one Somnath, one Kedar and one Pusa Rabibara. Some catalogue rows combine several works; none establishes an earliest date for an observance. The original twelve-record table below is followed by the Nagala, Rabinarayana and Somnath extensions.

## Recovered Sudasa texts

The eleven-page scanned booklet names Damayanti on its cover and opening; she is the story’s queen, not an established author. Printed p.2, verse 17, names ten manda with chhena and coconut filling. Printed pp.2–3 link the offering to the knotting and loss of a ritual thread. The four-page typed file has now been rendered and visually read; its legacy-font extraction remains unsuitable for quotation. Both arrived through [OdiaBooks’ public links](https://www.odiabooks.com/sudasha-brata-katha-pdf-ebook.html); the shared host is not independent corroboration. They tell different stories; their historical relationship and relative dates remain unestablished.

No edition date was established in the inspected scan pages. File creation timestamps describe digital files, not composition dates. A retailer’s 2007 date for another listing is not assigned to either recovered copy. Source files and page images stay in research working storage; no republication rights are claimed.
