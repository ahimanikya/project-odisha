# Bhai Jiuntia

Food and a returning sister’s place in the family are central questions for reading this western Odisha vow.

**Research note.** Practice and food accounts are documented; full ritual stories and earliest histories remain open.

## Sisters, brothers and the autumn calendar

Mahanti describes sisters invoking Durga on Ashwina’s bright-fortnight eighth day, returning to parental homes and exchanging gifts. He names jugara and tikhiri as offerings shared by sisters and brothers.

## A recent ethnographic window

Indhubala Kesavan reports eleven months of fieldwork during May2022–December2023 in Bargarh district. The village name is a pseudonym. Her account connects married sisters’ homecoming, gifts and ritual duties with rice cultivation.

Sources: [Rice culture and sacred siblingship: Nua Khai and Bhai Jiuntia celebrations in Western Odisha, India](https://journals.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/paideuma/index.php/paideuma/article/download/2046/2237/4542). Printed pp.15,31–35 / PDF1,17–21.

## Malathi and Mathana Kumar: a story in recitation

Kesavan records an elderly participant’s story of Malathi, whose prayers to Durga save her brother Mathana Kumar from death. The participant associates it with a Bhai Jiuntia osa-katha booklet; the researcher also observed married sisters gathering to recite the story on the festival evening. This is an attributed narrative synopsis and witnessed recitation, not a complete recovered text or historical event.

## From paddy to lia jugar

Kesavan describes an older woman collecting household paddy, roasting and redistributing it. Stored parched rice is mixed with melted jaggery into conical lia jugar for worship, then shared. This documents collective processing and skilled labour; temperatures and proportions are not supplied.

Sources: [Rice culture and sacred siblingship: Nua Khai and Bhai Jiuntia celebrations in Western Odisha, India](https://journals.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/paideuma/index.php/paideuma/article/download/2046/2237/4542). Printed p.33 / PDF19; preparation and distribution paragraphs.
