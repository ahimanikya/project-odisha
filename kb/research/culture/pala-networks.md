# Pala networks: Sachidananda Dash, UKS TV and organisations

The clearest documented connection is to **UKS TV / Utkaliya Kala Sanskruti**, a cultural media publisher carrying Pala performances and training material. A shared public booking contact creates a more specific lead to **Sri Satyanarayana Pala Parisada**, Kantapara, Cuttack. The evidence does not yet establish Sachidananda Dash as the owner, founder or elected officer of either body.

This is a research follow-up to the [Pala article](pala.md), checked on 19 October 2026. It uses public professional material and original institutional publishers. No contact, interview, membership request or message was sent. [Structured identities, recordings, relationships and open questions](../references/data/pala-networks.json).

## The person and the media publisher

The [supplied Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/sachidash.ukstv) identifies Sachidananda Dash as a Pala performer and a speaker on the Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavata, Malika and Jagannath culture. Its Communities section includes **ଉତ୍କଳୀୟ କଳା ସଂସ୍କୃତି ପାଲା**. That is evidence of a named social channel, rather than proof of an incorporated association.

The [UKS TV channel](https://www.youtube.com/@odiapala) calls itself **ଉତ୍କଳୀୟ କଳା ସଂସ୍କୃତି TV**. Its catalogue includes a performance explicitly credited to Sachidananda Dash and team, and a playlist bearing his full name. That supports a performer–publisher connection. The About panel does not identify a legal proprietor or governing committee. The channel’s displayed joining date, 5 June 2017, must not become an organisation founding date.

UKS links both an older [Utkaliya Kala Sanskruti Facebook identity](https://www.facebook.com/utkaliyakalasanskruti/) and, in the Guru Dakhina description, a [UKS TV Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/UtkaliyaKalaSanskrutii). The older profile’s bio identifies UKS TV Odia and a cultural dissemination purpose. These are linked publisher identities, not several independent witnesses or several organisations. Mutable platform counters have not been adopted as measures of audience, reach or livelihood.

One public contact in the UKS TV About booking information exactly matches this row’s published contact. The row was retrieved over HTTPS and checked directly. This is a useful organisational lead. A shared contact might belong to a performer, booking coordinator or someone serving more than one group; it cannot by itself establish common ownership or Dash’s office.

The portal dates describe registration in that system and a profile update. They do not establish the troupe’s founding date, registration under a particular legal Act, present personnel or present booking availability. Other rows on the same page contain Pala in their names but carry different art categories. Those inconsistencies should be retained, rather than repaired by assumption. A bounded page sample is not a statewide census.

## Other bodies belong in a separate network map

**Sri Nilachala Nikhilotkala Pala Gayaka Parisad Odisha** appears in the art-institution field of serial 298 in the government’s [Keonjhar–Jhumpura artist database](https://culture.odisha.gov.in/en/artist-database/odisha-folk-artist/kendujarh/Keonjhar-Jhumpura?page=9). A [17 July 2021 report](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/odisha/2021/Jul/17/pandemic-hit-pala-artistes-seek-odisha-government-aid-2331463.html) names a similarly rendered association in the context of an appeal for pandemic assistance. This supports research into collective organisation and welfare. Old office titles and reported hardship are historical evidence, not current officers or current income measurements. Its statewide count is not adopted without the underlying register.

**Shree Jagannath Pala Research Foundation** is another kind of institution. Its [own website](https://www.sjprf.in/) describes research and dissemination, reports registration on 27 August 2015, and lists Pala Kabita and documentary notices for Jagannath Behera and Markandeya Bahinipati. A registry certificate was not examined; linked documentary playback and permissions were not established. Its undated team page should not be treated as a verified current officer list. No affiliation between Dash and the foundation was established.

These records distinguish a publisher, a registered troupe, an association network and a research foundation. Similar cultural purposes do not make them the same institution.

## What the second article audit found

The maintained reader article keeps its ensemble description local, its award and festival examples dated, and its origin account qualified. Its source mapping contains seven sources and thirteen footnote markers. The present follow-up reused those stable original-paper inspections; it did not freshly reread every historical publication or supply independent specialist review.

The largest coverage gap is the social infrastructure around performance: apprenticeship, circulating recordings, hosting, associations and payment. The newly found catalogue and register offer concrete entrances. They can support a bounded future section without asserting ownership, present earnings or universal practice.

Recent photographs inspected on the supplied Facebook profile include AI-content labels and devotional-event banners. They were not adopted as proof of association office, identity of pictured participants or institutional history. Likewise, do not merge this performer with Sachi Pati, an Odissi Mardala exponent named Sachidananda Das, or unrelated namesakes found by general search.

There is also a capture-history difference to preserve. The separate research workspace records a 12 October recovery of Dash’s 2011 PDF after disabling TLS verification; the project dossier records failed secure retrievals. The former is not fresh secure authentication or independent corroboration. The public article’s form explanation already uses Patra’s recovered 2017 study, so this follow-up does not silently replace its evidence.
