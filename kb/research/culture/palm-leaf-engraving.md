# Palm-leaf engraving: stories cut into a leaf

A leaf can carry a story through an incised line. Talapatra Chitra combines prepared palm strips, drawing and engraving; the NID documentation connects the practice with Raghurajpur and Bhubaneswar.

## How the image appears

The documented process joins prepared strips, cuts fine lines with a pointed tool and rubs colour into the incisions before wiping the surface. Some examples add colour or cutwork. This is one institutional account of practice, not a conservation prescription or a rule followed by every maker. The PDF’s western-India comparison image is not catalogued here as an Odisha work.
