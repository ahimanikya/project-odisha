# Rhythm and transmission: a research guide

This guide links the illustrated tala article with remaining questions about particular recordings and oral transmission. Original posts are credited beside the relevant question.

## Ada Tali and changing cycles

Read [Odissi tala: why the grouping matters](odissi-tala-and-changing-cycles.md), which verifies Adatali 14 = 4+3+4+3, distinguishes Aditala 16 and documents three changing fourteen-matra groupings in Sabda Swara Pallavi. See [the original post](https://www.instagram.com/odissiandbeyond/reel/DNLMAFUTQny/).

**Next evidence needed:** Analyse the original Ga Champu and changing-cycle recordings, with timestamps and teaching/notation context. Jhula’s precise structure still needs an identified teaching source.

The changing-tala post explicitly says the song is not primarily a Jhulana song. The Ada Tali count comes from [the collaborative Ga Champu caption](https://www.instagram.com/tulika_tripathy/reel/DXt09PvjD-I/), now independently corroborated by practitioner documentation, the ABGMVM syllabus and Patnaik’s 2019 article. The original rendition itself has not been analysed. Begin with [Mardala](mardala.md) and [abhinaya and teaching](odissi-abhinaya.md). [Soumya’s oral-versus-printed example](https://www.instagram.com/odissiandbeyond/reel/DQPHk_JjDpt/) provides a separate question about word variants: record the teacher-taught and printed readings independently.
