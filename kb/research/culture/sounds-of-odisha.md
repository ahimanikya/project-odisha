# Sounds of Odisha

A sound can open a story before a photograph does. Follow voices, instruments and everyday rhythms into the people who make them, the occasions that give them meaning and the places where they belong.

## Eight published stories and their KB records

- [Ghanta and ghanti](ghanta-ghanti.md) — ritual ringing, group playing and the metalworkers behind the sound.
- [Pala](pala.md) — an illustrated invitation to follow poetry, accompanying artists and a listening evening.

## How each story should work

Begin with a clearly imagined scene or an evidenced encounter. Explain the sound, meet its people and connect it with a specific place and occasion. Keep devotional meaning distinct from historical origin. Add a short real recording and an image when rights, context and permission are established; preserve sources at the end.
