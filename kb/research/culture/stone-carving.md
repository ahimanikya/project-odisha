# Stone carving: an image revealed by removal

Stone carving begins with a block and removes material to reveal a form. The Bhubaneswar account documented by NID describes rough shaping, finer chiselling and polishing, with tools and pressure chosen for the stone. Its broad national history is not evidence of an unbroken Odisha lineage.

## People and recognition

[Ramakanta Mahapatra](../people/ramakanta-mahapatra.md) is the named documentation contact. [Sudarshan Sahoo](../people/sudarshan-sahoo.md) provides a separate national-recognition and institution-building route: the 2021 Padma Vibhushan announcement lists Odisha, while Incredible India attributes Puri’s craft museum foundation in1977 to him. These sources do not credit him with every sculpture shown in the NID account.

## What to catalogue next

Individual sculptures need titles or clear descriptions, maker credits, material, creation/installation date and a holding institution. A museum founder, workshop contact and object creator are different roles. Current commissions, quarry provenance, earnings and shipping remain unknown.
