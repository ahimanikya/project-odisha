# Bargarh: a textile photography and catalogue service

**Desk-research starter dossier · 3 October 2026.** Opportunity OPP-BAR-001. Local demand, client permissions, quotations and profit remain unverified.

## The service to test

Create product photographs and accurate catalogue records for Bargarh weavers or cooperatives. The client keeps stock, pricing, payment, sale and fulfilment responsibility. Start with an agreed batch photographed on the client's premises; agree editing, revisions, file delivery and usage rights before accepting work. Order management is a separate possible service, outside this first model.

This is an editorial business hypothesis. It connects [Bargarh's existing weaving research](../handlooms/bargarh.md) and [Sambalpuri Bandha](../handlooms/sambalpuri-bandha.md) to a defined service, without treating craft heritage as proof of a profitable business.

## Evidence of a purchasing category

Boyanika's May 2026 agency EOI requested **ten photographs per product** for e-commerce, including garment detail views, within a broader video/design/social-media engagement. This is a documented buyer specification, not ten paid jobs or an open contract. Its deadline was 22 June 2026. The [original record](../sources/enterprise-boyanika-agency2026.md) includes eligibility and location restrictions.

A [second thematic EOI](../sources/enterprise-boyanika-thematic2026.md), issued 25 June 2026 and due 15 July 2026, sought celebrity/model campaigns. It required average annual turnover of at least ₹4 crore across three stated financial years. The earlier instrument required ₹25 lakh in FY2024–25. These different scopes cannot supply a beginner's revenue target. Neither establishes an award, payment or Bargarh cooperative willingness to purchase a smaller service. Both belong to the same buyer, as the [tender index](../sources/enterprise-boyanika-tenders.md) confirms.

## A useful first deliverable

Proposed pilot specification, to agree with a willing client:

- Product ID and approved maker/cooperative credit; retain permission for identifiable people and workspaces.
- Consistent full-product, border, pallu and texture photographs, as appropriate. Ten views is the published institutional example, not an imposed minimum for every local item.
- Verified form, dimensions and fibre; technique/tradition labelled separately. Record who supplied each claim. Leave unknowns blank.
- Client-approved descriptions, filenames, image rights and handover copies. Do not invent GI authorization or product certifications from the place name.
- Agreed batch size, fee, travel, turnaround, revision allowance, acceptance and payment schedule. Record actual hours and rework in a permitted paid trial.

An existing [Boyanika SKU](../handlooms/sambalpuri-bandha.md) can inform field structure; it does not grant image reuse or prove a photographed local item has that provenance. No existing photograph or maker identity has been licensed for this pilot.

## Premises, people and permissions
