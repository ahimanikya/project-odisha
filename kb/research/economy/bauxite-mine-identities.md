# Kodingamali and Baphlimali: output, processing and accountability

**Two bauxite mines, two different stories.** OMC’s Kodingamali adds a regulator-recorded output series. Baphlimali connects a mine to the Utkal Alumina refinery and an international certification record. Neither capacity nor certification measures mineral revenue or household prosperity.

IBM’s September 2024 inspection records **3 million tonnes of ROM bauxite in FY2023–24**, against a 6 million-tonne proposal. It explicitly identifies a 3 MTPA environmental-clearance constraint. This is a historical inspection, not confirmation of today’s permission.
