# Cement and fertilisers: what the plant actually made

**A small total increase can conceal a large change in product mix.** IFFCO’s Paradeep unit reported **22.31 lakh tonnes of NP/DAP in FY2025–26**, about **0.8% above** the preceding year. The component table shows DAP rising while its NPK-labelled row fell. This is one unit’s output, not Odisha’s fertiliser production or farmers’ consumption.

## A company name is not a geographical boundary

Paradeep Phosphates is distinct from IFFCO. Its 2025–26 report maps facilities in Paradeep, Goa, Mangalore and Mahad. On printed p.108, its narrative gives 3.36 million tonnes while the table gives 3,666,516 tonnes. That discrepancy is held for clarification; neither company figure is accepted as the Odisha plant’s output. ## Questions worth turning into district business research

For **Jagatsinghpur**, investigate plant-qualified bagging, maintenance, testing and freight services. Product output is a starting signal; actual tenders, buyer specifications, vendor qualifications and payment terms are still required.

For **Sundargarh**, investigate maintenance, material testing and equipment support around the defined cement lines. Verify contracts and input specifications before estimating a market. A factory’s material needs do not prove that a new local supplier can win orders.

Land availability, permissions for a defined business, local hiring, margins and current orders remain unknown. These are research hypotheses, not ready investment recommendations.
