Its annexure lists the proposed 33-MVA furnace and 67.5-MW captive power plant as **not implemented**. The transfer covers the implemented furnaces only. This concerns KNR2, not the separate KNR1 greenfield project; it does not establish October operating status. [Letter pp.2–4](https://www.imfa.in/api/pdf/IA_OR_IND1_04092026.pdf/IA_OR_IND_1_04092026_a1432f31b5.pdf).
