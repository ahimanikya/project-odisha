# Food processing: follow the milk through the business

**More turnover does not tell us how much more food a processor made.** OMFED’s dated November 2025 account reports ₹1,028 crore turnover in FY2024–25, up from ₹855 crore: about **20.2% nominal growth**. These are rounded federation figures, not audited product volumes or farmer profit. [Original account](https://omfed.com/media/latest-events/national-milk-day-celebrated-by-omfed/).

## Different stages, different numbers

| Measure | FY2023–24 | FY2024–25 | What it measures |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| OMFED average daily procurement | 4.91 | 5.14 | Lakh litres/day collected, not processed output |
| OMFED reported turnover | 855 | 1,028 | Nominal ₹ crore, not profit |
| Dated product-level processing output | Unknown | Unknown | Milk, powder, ghee and other products need their own quantities |

The procurement pair comes from the [Economic Survey, section 3.10.10, printed p.88](https://assembly.odisha.gov.in/WriteReadData/News/OES%202026%20WEB%20UPLOAD.pdf). Its indexed publisher paragraph implies roughly 4.7% growth; the full PDF download failed, so facsimile review is still pending. The comparison does not explain turnover growth. Price changes, product mix, stocks and business scope need separate evidence. The existing [state milk-production figure](../statistics/livestock.md) is a different measure and is reused without making a tonnes-to-litres market-share calculation.

## An actual business mechanism: handling seasonal surplus

OMFED’s **May 2024 conversion tender** sought processing services for surplus milk into powder, white butter and ghee. Its draft agreement ties supply to the FY2024–25 flush season and availability of surplus milk. Requirements include experienced technical staff, testing facilities, storage and insulated tankers. [Original tender, PDF pp.7 and 20](https://old.omfed.com/tenders2024/tender%20skimmed%20milk%20040524.pdf).

This identifies a historical procurement mechanism and the capabilities a buyer sought. It does not establish a winning supplier, completed conversion volume, current open contract or conversion inside Odisha. Product yields and service prices remain unknown.

## District leads that can become business studies

The [publisher’s directory](https://omfed.com/dairy-plants/) names Arilo in Cuttack, Berhampur in Ganjam, Rourkela in Sundargarh, Sambalpur and other dairies. This supports place-specific research into cold-chain services, testing, maintenance and distribution. It does not verify spare capacity, vendor access, land or a viable customer contract.

For each proposed enterprise, obtain dated buyer requirements, monthly volumes, quality/rejection records, transport costs and payment terms. These are editorial research hypotheses; no forecast revenue or investment recommendation is supplied.
