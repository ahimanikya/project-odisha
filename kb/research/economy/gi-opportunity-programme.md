## How the think-tank work becomes a filing

1. **Identify:** record local/Odia names, makers, exact goods and place; screen registered and pending applications.
2. **Substantiate:** assemble dated name-use and history, a mapped production area, reproducible characteristics and the link between reputation/quality and place. Test competing explanations and shared traditions.
3. **Organize:** establish the legitimate applicant, producer membership and mandate, including artisans and temple custodians where relevant. Utkal can prepare research; it cannot invent representation.
4. **Specify and govern:** agree production standards, permitted variation, inspection, traceability and a fair route for qualifying producers to participate.
5. **Prepare and review:** assemble the statement of case, annexures and current forms. Confirm current fees, rules, representation and authorization before submission. Seek an Indian GI practitioner’s review of the exact dossier.
6. **Follow through:** monitor examination, publication/opposition and registration; then authorized-user uptake, quality control, distribution and measurable producer returns.

The [preparation pack](../product/gi-application-preparation.md) starts four evidence dossiers and names the missing annexures. The statutory producer-representation requirement is in [section 11](../sources/gi-act-wipolex.md); the Registry’s [introductory checklist](../sources/gi-filing-booklet.md) identifies practical evidence categories. No forms have been submitted, fees paid, representatives appointed or external messages sent.

NABARD-supported [EDII facilitation work](../sources/gi-edii-facilitation.md) is a useful institutional model: identification, producer organization and post-tag support belong together. No affiliation or support award to Utkal is implied.
