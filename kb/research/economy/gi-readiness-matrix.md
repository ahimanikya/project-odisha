These are editorial work routes, not eligibility scores, profitability rankings or a decision to prioritise one community over another. Every route still needs producer participation.

## Shared gaps that a web search cannot fill

The pilot has not verified an actual mandate for Utkal to represent any candidate’s producers. It also lacks an accepted local specification, certified boundary, verified inspection authority and operation, and a comparable product-level economic baseline. Submitted claims and proposed institutions remain separately recorded; an absent verification field does not mean no institution or document exists.

A participant-supported record should establish whose practice and goods are represented, how variants are included, who has agreed, and how dissent is retained. Consent to an interview is not authority to file. A historical artisan roster is not present membership or collective consent. The [preparation pack](../product/gi-application-preparation.md) provides the annexure structure; no appointment, outreach or signature is implied by this matrix.

Product costs, sales, export values and income effects remain unknown. Research priorities cannot be ranked by an invented GI premium. If a future participating group wishes to test an enterprise idea, record actual batch quantities, costs, net realisation, defects/spoilage and repeat orders, with dates and sample coverage. This is a proposed measurement plan, not a forecast or a mandated filing requirement.
