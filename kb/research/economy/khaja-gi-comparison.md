# Khaja GI specifications and evidence needed for Puri

**The original specifications describe two different sweets:** Silao Khaja is a layered wheat preparation, while Goan Khaje is made as chickpea sticks with a ginger-jaggery coating. Their similar names are a reason to compare defined goods carefully. They do not establish Puri Khaja’s specification or decide its GI eligibility.

[Odisha GI opportunity programme](gi-opportunity-programme.md) — This study supports the Odisha GI opportunity programme. It reads the original advertised specifications for applications 584 and 655, recovered from the Registry on 4 October 2026. These are historical application publications, not fresh production surveys. The [existing status checks](../references/data/gi-opportunities.json) separately record both as registered.

## Conflicts and claims kept out of the recipe record

**Silao’s ingredient tables conflict.** Page 15 gives ghee as 20%; page 19 gives 30% with the other proportions unchanged. The latter listed shares total 110%. We retain both readings and do not silently choose or normalize a recipe. The source’s ancient-origin narratives also distinguish legend from documentary references; the age of a settlement does not date a sweet’s invention.

**Goa’s text needs technical review before recipe reuse.** Page 53 mentions lead-coated copper utensils; we have quarantined that wording and do not carry it into preparation advice. Health claims in pp.53–55 are not adopted as medical facts. Shelf-life statements in both applications remain historical claims, not tested storage instructions for products sold today. These cautions arise from the actual documents, not a general assumption that a GI specification is a food-safety manual.

The Goa application also includes production and turnover claims on p.55 without an adequate shared observation period or survey basis. Those figures are excluded from the economic comparison. A recipe batch size elsewhere in the document is not annual market output. Current Puri sales, exports and producer counts remain unknown.

## The Puri dossier now needs its own technical evidence

The [preparation pack](../product/gi-application-preparation.md) can use this comparison as a background annexure. It still needs original Puri records, documented name use, a reasoned production boundary and a legitimate producer representative. Existing indexed cluster and temple-food descriptions support an attributed association; they do not supply these missing fields.

For later authorized maker participation, record the following without presuming one universal recipe:

1. Local name and variants; the goods included and excluded; actual making locations and channels of sale.
2. Flour and other inputs; folding or layering method; frying medium; syrup treatment; measured dimensions and permitted variation.
3. Batch traceability, packaging, breakage, seasonal changes and product-specific shelf-life testing. Document practice separately from validated food-safety advice.
4. Dated evidence linking the name and reputation to Puri, including evidence that disagrees with the proposed account.
5. Producer membership, agreed controls and who could inspect and authorize use. No consent or appointment is assumed.
