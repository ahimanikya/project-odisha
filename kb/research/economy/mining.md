This is a research selection, not a verified deposit or active-mine map. Preserve historical and administrative aliases.

## What readers should see

Short mineral explainers, district portraits, credited worker and scientist profiles, five- or ten-year charts, and plant-stage timelines. A public card should carry one takeaway, its year/unit and a source reveal. A mine profile should state whether it is proposed, auctioned, leased, operating, suspended or closed, with an observation date.

Keep useful positive achievements alongside setbacks and unresolved questions. More ore extracted does not on its own establish higher local incomes or better services. Green-mining claims need measured outcomes.
