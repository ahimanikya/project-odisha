## A supply chain we can actually trace · 2 October 2026

**Niali’s chhena connects dairy work with Pahala’s sweet market.** A December 2023 case study interviewed 10 retailers and 200 consumers. It describes supply from Niali and sales reaching other Odisha markets and West Bengal. These are the authors’ study findings; fieldwork dates and shipment quantities are not given. [Original study, pp.2306–2307](https://ndpublisher.in/admin/issues/EAv68n5z11.pdf).

The study’s ingredient table totals ₹2,000 for a described 20 kg batch. This is an ingredient bill, not the full cost of production or profit. Its consumer table ranks short shelf life as the leading concern. That gives us a grounded research direction: packaging, product consistency and distribution, alongside the sweet’s cultural appeal. ### Numbers that need correction or more evidence

The paper says 10 kg yields ₹3,000–₹4,000 at a stated ₹150–₹200/kg. Those inputs produce ₹1,500–₹2,000. We preserve the discrepancy and withhold its extrapolated cluster revenue. Retailer percentages also lack a denominator consistent with its ten-person sample.
