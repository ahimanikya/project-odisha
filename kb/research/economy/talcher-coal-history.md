# Talcher: town, coalfield and the dates that differ

**Talcher’s coal story has several starting points.** The 1961 Census handbook records a Handidhua lease beginning on **22 April 1921**, while its historical narrative places production in **1924**. These describe different events. [Table39, printed p.342; narrative p.4](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29109/download/32290/21930_1961_DHE.pdf).

| Place or process | Date | Meaning in the source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handidhua | 22 April 1921 / 1924 | Lease commencement / production start |
| Deulbera (spelled Dewlbera in narrative) | 22 April 1921 / 1932 | Lease commencement / production start |
| Dera | 1930 | Production start; no lease date supplied in Table39 |
| Talcher town | 1954 | Notified Area status, as described in the handbook |

The handbook was published in **1966**, although its Census reference year is **1961**. It describes Talcher town and the collieries as distinct places. Its district geography is historical Dhenkanal; this cannot be used as a present-day district inventory. [Printed pp.4,53,342](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29109/download/32290/21930_1961_DHE.pdf).

## Exploration continued beyond the first mines

CMPDI’s later report describes detailed IBM/NCDC exploration in the late 1950s, GSI exploration in 1963–65 and work in the Bharatpur/Kalinga blocks in 1971–75. These are exploration periods, not production-start dates. [Historical introduction, printed p.1](https://www.mahanadicoal.in/Environment/pdf/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202018-19%20Talcher.pdf).

Its attribution of an 1837 search to “Blandford Brothers” is held for original archival verification. The Census account records an 1837 examination without that personal attribution. No named discoverer is established by this checkpoint. The inaccessible district-hosted 2019 report remains a discovery lead, not an independently verified third account.
