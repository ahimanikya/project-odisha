# Forest foods: knowledge, seasons and everyday life

**Build this collection around ingredients, the people who know them and the seasons in which they are used.** Household food, local exchange and commercial trade deserve separate evidence.

## Reuse the food identities already documented

[Koli fruits](koli-fruits.md) already records botanical and local-name distinctions, including chara, jammu and several kinds of bara. That collection is a starting point for location-specific forest-food research, not proof that every market fruit is wild harvested. [Ou khatta](ou-khatta.md) and [bela pana](bela-pana.md) preserve attributed preparations; record cultivated, garden and wild origins separately when known. [Chatu patrapoda](chatu-patrapoda.md) connects food with leaf cooking, but a recipe does not establish a mushroom's wild origin or edible species identity.

## What households keep matters too

Mishra and Pradhan's 2018 publication describes 120 households in six villages of Patana and Bansapal, Kendujhar. Its abstract reports that **38.09% of collected non-timber forest products were consumed and 61.91% sold**. This includes edible and non-edible materials; it is not a diet share. The fieldwork year is unspecified in the abstract, and the sample cannot represent all Odisha. [Study abstract](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ags/injagm/399613.html).

## Product stories to build

Document fruits and kernels, flowers, honey, edible leaves and shoots, tubers and identified edible fungi. These are research groups, not a verified list of edible Odisha species. Each entry needs the local name and language, identified species and plant part, place, season, community-attributed uses, preservation or preparation account, and whether supply is wild, cultivated or mixed. Do not collapse one community's knowledge into a single statewide tradition.

[Preparation and variation](preparation-and-variation.md) remains the recipe home. Add reliable, attributed versions there when available; do not publish identification-dependent wild-food instructions from a common name alone. Monetary sales cannot capture every food's household or cultural value.

[Siali products](../economy/siali-products.md) connects plant identity with distinct leaf, fibre and seed uses. The mahua profile now includes attributed food-development research, while the honey profile contains a source-specific historical seasonal account.

[Mahua’s local seasonal and cake account](mahua-mohula.md) now distinguishes a documented food preparation from later university product-development work. Historical collection includes both private and forest land.

[Sal](../economy/sal-products.md) and [siali](../economy/siali-products.md) extend the food story to the materials used for wrapping and serving it. Named historical testimony and a local working plan document different practices and seasons; neither establishes present availability.
