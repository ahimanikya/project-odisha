## Preparation knowledge and its limits

The hospitality chapter supplies short ingredient and process descriptions. It does not give these sweets a complete reproducible formula with quantities, yield, temperature, time or measured storage conditions. We have not tested a recipe. The authors’ use of “ricotta cheese” is retained as their explanatory terminology, not treated as proof that all chhena products use an identical cheese or process.

Its discussion describes a literature-review approach and references earlier books and articles. It is not a new census of Puri makers or a producer-approved GI specification. The NCHM-hosted book and the IHM-hosted chapter are the same publication lineage, not two independent confirmations. A 2022 or 2025 URL folder is not the chapter’s publication date; the proceedings identify the March 2018 conference.

For a usable preparation entry, document each named variant separately: local spelling and meaning; ingredient identities and proportions; mixing or resting; shaping and dimensions; cooking medium, time and temperature; syrup or coating stage; yield; and storage evidence. Attribute whose practice is being described. Record variation rather than silently selecting one account as the universal recipe.
