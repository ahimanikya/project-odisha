# Ghanta tarkari

Ghanta tarkari is a useful food story because its role changes with the occasion. In the saved Dutiya accounts, produce offered in worship becomes a curry for eating and exchange. The food name alone does not identify that ritual sequence.

## Offering, meal and exchange

Dash2006 records offered fruits and vegetables cooked into ghanta the following day (printed17/PDF3). Mahanti’s coastal account describes21 seasonal vegetables under an aparamanga leaf, later cooked and exchanged (printed58/PDF74). These are source-specific descriptions. Neither gives a complete measured recipe or establishes the botanical identity of the named branch/leaf.

## A general cuisine listing has a different scope

Odisha Tourism’s Jajpur page describes Desi Ghanta Tarkari as a home-style mixed vegetable curry, alongside Macha Besara as a separate mustard fish preparation. This is an undated promotional food association, not a Dutiya offering, menu, serving venue or current food offer. We do not transfer the fish description to vegetable ghanta.

## Related preparations need separate accounts

[Pua Jiuntia](../culture/osha/pua-jiuntia.md) preserves western accounts and their differing vegetables and leaf/thread descriptions. [Samba Dashami](../culture/osha/samba-dashami.md) has its own food context. This page does not combine every ghanta variant into one recipe or claim a common origin.

## What a future preparation record should contain

Name the cook, locality, occasion, vegetables and pulses, substitutions, cooking sequence and recipients. Record whether ingredients were offerings or ordinary cooking ingredients. Quantities, allergens, botanical identities and a tested recipe remain unknown here. Cultural association does not establish a food-sale venue.
