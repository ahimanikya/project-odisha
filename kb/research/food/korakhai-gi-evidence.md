## A working goods description, with questions left open

The2016 report describes khai, jaggery or sugar, coconut and cardamom. It attributes a distinct preparation tradition for Lingaraj offerings to temple sevak Trinath Badu. These are reported descriptions, not a recipe tested by Utkal or proof that all retail batches are ritual offerings. Its general/special labels lack measurable specifications.

For the dossier, document the following with participating makers:

| Field | Needed evidence |
|---|---|
| Grain and raw materials | Local names for khai/lia, paddy or rice input, grain preparation, variety and permitted substitutions; do not assume every product called puffed rice is equivalent |
| Sweetener and flavour | Whether sugar and jaggery are alternatives, proportions, coconut form, spices and permitted variation |
| Process | A witnessed, attributed sequence with batch weights, heat control, mixing and cooling; the published word caramelisation is not a measured chemical finding |
| Finished goods | Shape, size, texture, moisture and packaging; distinguish retail variants from any temple-specific specification |
| Shelf life | Storage conditions and testing; a dry appearance or a travel anecdote does not establish a safe shelf-life claim |
| People and place | Actual preparation sites, producer membership, service roles and consent; retail location and temple association do not define a certified boundary |

No time-temperature recipe or compulsory formula is inferred from general descriptions. Existing maker names remain in the sweets register; they are not newly appointed representatives. A temple-service account is not a signed mandate from all confectioners.

## Livelihood possibilities to investigate

A maker-approved demonstration, a food-history walk and improved packaging are plausible project ideas. The BDA and college records help explain why these are relevant locally; neither proves demand, profitability or a ready business opportunity. A useful pilot would record maker payment, batch costs, sales, packaging loss and repeat orders, with a clear agreement about access and credit. No partners have been contacted or committed.

The saved2016 shop, quantity and price estimates remain unverified historical leads. They must not be annualised into turnover or used to forecast a GI premium. Current staffing, rent, permits, supplier costs, market size and operating availability are unknown.
