Its preparation section (PDF51–53, printed54–56) describes household production, heat treatment and overnight setting; it also acknowledges occasional clay vessels. These are applicant descriptions. ## Geography, oversight and livelihood gaps

The submitted text claims Manikapatna and adjoining Chilika-area villages; the map identifies a Puri/Bramhagiri context. Neither has been converted here to an agreed or certified boundary. The Registry explicitly asks for clarification.

The case proposes an eight-seat inspection body led by the Puri Collector or nominee, with technical and administrative representatives and Sampark as convener (PDF62–64, printed65–67). It says the body would become functional upon registration. This supports a **proposed structure**, not evidence that its members accepted appointments or that inspections occur.

The local livelihood story could connect buffalo keepers, household curd makers, bamboo-vessel makers and buyers. These are research directions, not a verified producer census or investment forecast. Current prices, sales, margins, export values, producer mandate, accepted boundary and agreed specification remain unknown. GI-C009 stays an existing-application lead; no duplicate proposal or filing-readiness claim is created.
