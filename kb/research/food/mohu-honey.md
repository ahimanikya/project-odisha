# Mohu: honey, collectors and processing

**Mohu (ମହୁ) means honey here.** The user explicitly confirmed that meaning on 1 October 2026. This records the intended name; it does not constitute a linguistic survey or identify the source of a particular honey.

Honey needs its own entry, distinct from [mahua/mohula](mahua-mohula.md). For each producer or lot, establish whether it is forest-collected, from managed hives, mixed, or unknown. Keep floral-origin labels as claims until supported by suitable traceability or analysis.

## What is already documented

The 2025 Odisha forestry report describes OFDC honey processing at Jashipur, Mayurbhanj. The [existing capacity observation](../statistics/forest-products.md) is reused: rated capacity is not actual output, and the report does not establish that all input honey is wild collected. Vasundhara lists honey among products in its producer-group livelihood work; this is the organisation's account, not measured statewide sales.

## Follow the value and the knowledge

Document named collectors or beekeepers, seasons and places, attributed collection history, aggregation, processing, testing, packaging and sales. Household uses and preparations require credited accounts. Beeswax should be a related non-food product record when Odisha-specific evidence is obtained, rather than being counted as honey.

The next market fields are dated collector prices, quantities purchased, processing throughput, quality tests, realised sales and Odisha-origin exports. Product listings and the presence of a processing plant cannot establish these outcomes. No current statewide honey market value or named producer certification has been verified here.
