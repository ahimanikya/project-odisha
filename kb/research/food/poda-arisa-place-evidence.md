# Poda pitha and arisa: Puri associations and wider Odisha traditions

**A Puri association is documented, but the foods still need a locally agreed definition before a place-specific GI proposal can be assessed.** Existing leads GI-C011 and GI-C012 remain research candidates. The evidence does not establish exclusive invention at the Jagannath temple or a single recipe used by all makers.

## What is connected to Puri

The Ministry of Tourism’s Utsav listing describes a poda pitha offering near Mausi Maa Temple on the return journey from Gundicha. The listing concerns the 1–9 July 2022 event, not the current festival calendar. Its indexed text is available, but direct retrieval timed out. This establishes a documented ritual association; it gives no measured recipe or producer mandate. [Utsav source record](../sources/gi-utsav-ratha2022.md).

The saved 2019 Odisha Review temple-food account lists Poda Pitha and Arisa on printed p.58. That is attributed cultural description, currently indexed-only, not an accepted specification or an origin date. [Existing source and access limits](../sources/gi-temple-food2019.md).

## What the preparation science enquiry can establish next

The different ingredient lists and methods are reasons to document variants separately. For arisa, the saved method uses syrup thickening and dough consistency to judge when shaping is possible. For poda, the named versions use different mixtures and cooking arrangements. These are documented practical process cues. They do not establish a validated commercial process, a historical invention date, or the chemistry of a particular temple preparation.

A future producer-supported study could measure flour particle size, ingredient ratios, moisture, resting conditions, cooking temperature and time, product dimensions and batch yield. Compare the named versions before choosing which differences matter. Record whose knowledge is being described and obtain consent for attribution. Any storage or packaging claim requires product-specific evidence; the book’s storage statement is not adopted as consumer shelf-life advice.

## Decision for the GI programme

The missing fields are a producer-supported product identity, a documented relationship between the goods and proposed place, an agreed boundary and specification, representation and inspection arrangements. Broader use of a food name does not by itself decide whether a distinct local product can qualify; this study has not decided that question.

For enterprise research, a useful next deliverable would be an attributed variant sheet with measured trials, packaging tests, actual costs and consented maker records. No prices, sales, exports or forecast demand are established here.
