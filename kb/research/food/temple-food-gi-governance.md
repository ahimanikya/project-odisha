## Product, place, people and process

In Journal28 pp.38–42, TTD appears as applicant and as the listed authority/organization. The production-area text on p.42 names the Sri Vari temple at Tirumala, while the live summary’s geographical-area field says Andhra Pradesh. The broad state field should not substitute for the specification’s production-place description or map.

The goods description identifies round boondi-based laddus, Bengal gram flour, ghee, syrup and adjuncts, and distinguishes small and large forms. Pages50–52 describe staged preparation; p.64 emphasizes cooks’ skills and ritual practice. These are an applicant’s historical account, not a tested home recipe. The p.66 inspection flow follows purchased inputs through laboratory checks before use in the potu. It describes controls; it does not provide a current independent audit or establish another temple’s inspection authority.

For Puri, keep four questions separate: **what is made, where it is made, what ritual status it has, and who can legitimately represent its producers and custodians.** A maker outside the temple and a ritual-service holder cannot be treated as interchangeable without evidence. The [saved Mahaprasad record](mahaprasad.md) and indexed2019 dry-food account provide cultural and historical leads; they do not establish all modern recipe origins.

## Differences and technical claims retained for clarification

- **Weight:** the2008 text on pp.42 and52 gives small laddus170–180g; TTD’s10November2022 statement gives160–180g. Preserve source dates and product descriptions. No formal amendment or complete product-type reconciliation has been recovered.
- **Process temperature:** p.52 describes ghee as boiling hot at about80°C. This wording was visually confirmed and is held for technical clarification, not reused as a cooking instruction. No corrected temperature is invented.
- **Shelf life and economics:** the journal’s sensory-duration claim is not validated storage advice. Historical daily output, prices and staffing are not current output, annual turnover or GI-caused economic gains.
- **Origin:** the applicant’s temple narratives, reputation argument and religious accounts have different evidentiary roles. Temple antiquity alone does not date the invention of a modern laddu or a Puri dish.
