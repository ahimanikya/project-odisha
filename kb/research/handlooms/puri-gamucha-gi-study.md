# Puri gamucha identity and the Assam GI comparison

**Puri gamucha is still a candidate identity to define.** Alangdia’s reported “Machhi Gamucha” is a useful lead, not yet a verified match for the user’s broader Puri gamucha proposal. Existing GI-C004 is enhanced here; no second candidate or filing is created.

This study supports the Odisha GI opportunity programme. The [programme](../economy/gi-opportunity-programme.md) separates candidate discovery from registered status and producer-led filing readiness.

## Local evidence and its limits

The Odisha Handloom Department’s [cluster list](../sources/gi-puri-clusters.md), read 5 October 2026, identifies Pipli under Puri. Its separate [product-range record](../sources/handloom-product-range.md), row H, includes Puri in a multi-district coarse-cotton group up to 40s. These are broad textile-geography records. Neither establishes the yarn, weave or boundary of a particular Puri gamucha. The grouped production-potential figure cannot be assigned to Puri or treated as actual sales.

A [14 September 2026 local report](../sources/gi-alangdia-report2026.md) names Alangdia’s Machhi Gamucha and Puri Sadar Tantubaya Cooperative Society. Its account of low returns and younger people leaving weaving is an adverse livelihood signal to investigate. The report’s historical and temple associations need dated original records. No society registration, membership list, consent or technical specification has been obtained. The word “Machhi” alone does not establish a fish motif. This lead does not establish that all Puri gamuchas are the same textile.

## What Assam’s original documents can teach the dossier

Application **594** has two journal editions. The Registry also has a separate withdrawn application **682** for Assamese Gamocha: those IDs must not be conflated. The current saved registration check for 594 and its older journal text answer different questions.

| Field | Journal 124, 24 October 2019 | Journal 161, 12 August 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| Published name | Gamosa | Gamosa of Assam |
| Applicant | Institute of Handicraft Development | Directorate of Handloom & Textiles, Assam |
| Goods class | 25, Clothing | 24, Gamosa/Gamocha |
| Type headings | Nine separately listed types | Eight headings; Uka/Xadharon and Tioni/Pani combined, Gosain included |
| Defined geography | Assam | Assam |

Sources: [2019 journal](../sources/gi-gamosa-journal2019.md), pp.14–18; [2022 journal](../sources/gi-gamosa-journal2022.md), pp.7–11. The later advertised edition is the reference for this comparison. The reason or legal instrument for each change has not been established; neither edition is erased or counted as independent corroboration.

The 2022 specification describes plain weave, white or cream ground, red borders or motifs, and different forms and uses. It includes cotton and silk/art-silk variants; a GI textile need not imply a single fibre. Pages11–13 describe country and fly-shuttle looms and patterning arrangements. Page15 names producer, government and technical representation in an inspection structure. These are applicant descriptions, not proof of present inspection performance. No Assam design, boundary or origin narrative is proposed for adoption in Puri.

**A measurement remains conflicted:** Anakata’s length is 150–200cm in the prose on p.9, but 175–200cm in tables on pp.12 and15. Both readings are retained. We do not publish one as the definitive manufacturing standard without clarification.
