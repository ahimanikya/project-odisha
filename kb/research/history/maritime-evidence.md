# Maritime Odisha: the evidence structure

Odisha’s maritime story can be connected through objects, sites, commemorations and modern trade observations. Those links support an outward-looking commercial identity. They do not supply an ancient export ledger or a continuous company history.

## A chronology with visible uncertainty

Read each site’s dated record separately. The 2022 Manikapatna paper’s archaeological phases and the 2026 conference abstract’s activity window are not identical. Neither should be converted into “uninterrupted global trade for 2,000 years.” Object dates, occupation dates and voyage dates are different variables.

## Geographic discipline

Use Utkal · Odisha as the campaign identity. In historical records, retain the source’s Kalinga, Utkal or Odra terminology and time-dependent geography. Do not assign every port of the wider historical east-coast network to today’s Odisha. Treat cultural transmission as a specific, testable proposition: which practice or object, at which place and date, with what evidence of direction?
