# Researching district business opportunities

## Evidence before recommendations

1. Search the research map, district statistics and repository-native places/industries before browsing. Give each opportunity a stable identity at district + activity level; relate multi-district chains without duplicating them.
2. State the hypothesis, customer and decision. Evidence of a local asset is not evidence of demand. Record geographic resolution and contradictory evidence.
3. Use district administrations, statistical publishers, DIC/MSME, IDCO/GO PLUS, sector regulators and original scheme instruments. Attribute seller/operator statements. Record source edition, locator, retrieval, period, unit, publication date and provenance family.
4. Establish permissions for the exact activity, scale and site. A generic registry list is discovery, not an exhaustive legal checklist. Check amendments, local authority, fee and validity separately. Mark unknown applicability as unknown.
5. Treat premises as options: leased space, existing home, shared facility and land purchase/allotment are different paths. Check rights, restrictions, hazard exposure, utilities, permitted use and project-specific approvals. Public map availability is only a lead.
6. Build people and organisation links from public professional records. Do not label a listed person available, willing or endorsed. Record capacity, date and consent where required; no unsolicited outreach is part of automated research.
7. Date each price/quote. Distinguish source facts, attributed quotes, calculated values and author assumptions. Use consistent units and nominal price dates. Model tax, repayment and subsidy timing explicitly; do not assume an unreceived subsidy finances startup cash.
8. Complete one useful bounded dossier before expanding. Record gaps, retry dates and next action. Hold publication of current applicant instructions if original rules conflict or cannot be read; publish a labelled research question instead.

## Readiness states

- **hypothesis:** place/activity connection and open business question.
- **desk_researched:** original-source scope captured; demand, land and people gaps visible.
- **locally_checked:** named and dated permitted local checks actually exist, with their limited scope.
- **plan_draft:** user-specific choices, assumptions and finances assembled.
- **reviewed_plan:** documented review appropriate to the selected plan; still no financing or success guarantee.

Track source access, factual confidence, legal currentness and publication approval separately. A rich page or passing validator does not move a business to locally_checked.

## Reusable calculation definitions

Contribution per unit = selling price minus variable cost per unit. Break-even units = monthly fixed operating cost divided by positive contribution per unit, with units and rounding disclosed. This simplified operating break-even excludes debt service, tax and capital recovery unless expressly included. If contribution is zero or negative, show “no finite break-even under these assumptions.” Compare required sales with practical capacity and seasonal demand. Cash runway uses the lowest monthly cash balance across the chosen horizon, including investment, working capital, delayed collections and repayments. Report scenario outputs as estimates, never observed profits.

## Quality and release checks

Check canonical links, source locators, rule versions, stale fields, cash-flow arithmetic, double counting, consumer-friendly wording, Odia review status and export pagination. Public downloads contain only approved public records; personal notes remain private. Versions remain recoverable and exports retain evidence dates. Commercial associations cannot purchase a favourable opportunity rating. No aggregate “best district” score is proposed without a documented, tested method.
