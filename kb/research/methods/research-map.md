## Before any further web research

1. Search the [research map](../references/data/research-map.json) and the source knowledge index. Inspect the queue task’s reuse pointers.
2. Read the corresponding project-native record and its sources. Existing editorial approval is not a fresh fact check, but it is work to preserve.
3. Write the missing question explicitly: absent field, source conflict, stale operating detail, new release or genuinely new subject.
4. Reuse or append to the existing entity/observation. Keep publication editions, original works, proposal stages and estimate vintages distinct.
5. Add a new record only when its identity or scope differs; explain its relationship to the existing record and update this map.

The map is a dated inventory, not a substitute for source review. It includes bibliographic source pages and project/product records, not just factual encyclopedia articles. Similar titles or URLs are candidate links, not proof of equivalent claims.
