# Pala artists and the work of keeping a tradition alive

Pala survives through people who learn its texts, rehearse its music, answer one another on stage and make space for the next performer. This collection brings together **20 selected artists**, from nationally recognised exponents to regional teachers, women festival performers and artists heard through recorded performances. It is a sourced starting list, not a ranking of fame or a directory of present availability.

Historical careers and dated reports are kept in their own time. “Gayak Samrat”, “Gayak Guru” and similar titles may express community recognition; they are not automatically government awards. The entries describe documented contributions and identify where cultural continuity is our interpretation of those contributions. Lead singers depend on bayaks and palias: those ensemble members need fuller named coverage too.

## National recognition, teaching and writing

### 1. Jagannath Behera · ଜଗନ୍ନାଥ ବେହେରା

**Why included:** Sangeet Natak Akademi Award 2009 for Pala; citation also records state award 2007.

**Work:** Quoted literary classics across Odia, Bengali, Hindi, Sanskrit and English; citation records two books and poetry without book titles.

**How the culture carries forward:** Combined performance, writing and cultural organisation; citation records past leadership of All Orissa Pala Gayak Parishad and association with Jogi Mahapurusha Sanskrit Vidyalaya. Direct institutional account; offices are historical citation statements.

**Learning route:** Harekrushna Nath, Bhagaban Singh, Harekrushna Swain. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** 2009 award citation. Birth year conflict: citation 1926, Odisha Sambad 2018 profile 1925. No resolved birth date, current office or living-status assertion.

Sources: [Jagannath Behera — Akademi Award: Pala (Orissa)](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/awardees/docs/Jagannath_Behera.pdf).

### 2. Laxmidhar Rout · ଲକ୍ଷ୍ମୀଧର ରାଉତ

**Why included:** Sangeet Natak Akademi Award 2016, Pala; PIB uses Laxmidhar Raut.

**Work:** Authored Udaharana Sataka, according to citation. A 16 September 2024 report credits his Odia verse translation of Adhyatma Ramayana, using ragas and poetic ornament.

**How the culture carries forward:** Citation explicitly credits training younger students and teaching through Pala Pratishthan, Shree Jagannath Pala Research Foundation and Shree Nilachala Nikhila Utkala Pala Parishad, Kendrapara. Direct institutional teaching evidence; later publication is a secondary report.

**Learning route:** Damodar Sarangi, Baishnaba Charan Pradhan. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** 2016 citation; January 2018 programme; September 2024 book report. Distinct from Laxman Charan Rout. No current office or student count established; no ISBN/publisher or full book inspection.

Sources: [Laxmidhar Rout — Sangeet Natak Akademi Award 2016](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/awardees/docs/Laxmidhar_Rout.pdf), [2016 Sangeet Natak Akademi awards: January 2018 ceremony notice](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1516849), [Sangeet Natak Akademi Annual Report 2017–18: Laxmidhar Rout](https://www.sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/reports/164275835898857.pdf), [Laxmidhar Rout’s Odia verse Adhyatma Ramayana launch](https://odishasambad.in/launching-of-adhyatma-ramayana-book-odia-version-by-laxmidhar-rout/).

## Celebrated literary and performance legacies

### 3. Ghanashyam Nayak · ଘନଶ୍ୟାମ ନାୟକ

**Why included:** Remembered as Gayak Bhushan in a 2019 profile.

**Work:** Combined dancing, Dakhini, Padi, Athatali, Nadia Kirtan and mandira playing; wrote Pala poetry, bhajans and jananas.

**How the culture carries forward:** Profile describes disciples and continued performance of his compositions by later singers. Secondary historical account, not a verified pupil census.

**Learning route:** Bhimsingh Ray, Dasharathi Dash, Bhaskar Chandra Panda. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** Historical career; retrospective 22 September 2019. Profile reports death in 1986. Named composition editions and pupil list remain unverified; no present activity implied.

Sources: [Ghanashyam Nayak — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/famous-pala-singer-ghanashyam-nayak/).

### 4. Arjun Dash · ଅର୍ଜୁନ ଦାଶ

**Why included:** Known as Gayak Bani Bibhushan; profile attributes that title to Ravenshaw College in 1975.

**Work:** Repertoire included Harishchandra, Kumarasambhava, Naishadha, Subhadra Parinaya and Nimai Sannyasa; noted for shringara and karuna expression.

**How the culture carries forward:** Profile describes training students and participating in Badi Pala with several senior exponents. Dated secondary retrospective; named students still needed.

**Learning route:** Ramchandra Dash, Krishnaprasad Basu, Prabodh Chandra Mishra. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** Retrospective 30 August 2020. Profile reports death 25 August 2012. State award year and pupil identities not independently established.

Sources: [Arjun Dash — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/pala-singer-bani-bibhusan-arjun-dash/).

### 5. Abhaya Charan Swain · ଅଭୟ ଚରଣ ସ୍ୱାଇଁ

**Why included:** Sahityacharya and Sanskrit teacher profiled as a distinguished Pala exponent.

**Work:** Known for Sanskrit kavya and drama interpretation, dramatic delivery and psychological reading of characters. Profile recalls a student performance drawn from Kalidasa’s Meghaduta.

**How the culture carries forward:** Connected formal Sanskrit learning with accessible performed interpretation. Interpretation of documented practice; no measured audience learning outcome.

**Learning route:** Gopal Panda. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** Profile 1 October 2018. Reported retired in that profile. Do not describe as presently touring. No independently verified national award claimed.

Sources: [Abhaya Charan Swain — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/famous-pala-gayak-pt-abhaya-charan-swain/).

### 6. Amareswar Das / Amar Das · ଅମରେଶ୍ୱର ଦାସ

**Why included:** Remembered as Malika Samrat in a 2018 retrospective.

**Work:** Associated with Malika and Panchasakha literature and quick responses in Badi Pala.

**How the culture carries forward:** Agadhu Beura’s separate profile identifies him as the guru from whom Beura received the chamara. Two profiles from one publisher family; a reported transmission link, not independent institutional corroboration.

**Evidence date and limits:** 2018 retrospective; 2020 pupil profile. Profile reports death in April 2018. Claim that he was the first to introduce Malika into Pala is not adopted; prophecy is literary/religious belief, not verified prediction.

Sources: [Amareswar Das — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/pala-singer-malika-samrat-acharya-amareswar-das/), [Agadhu Beura — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/famous-pala-gayak-agadhu-beura/).

### 7. Prahallad Sahoo · ପ୍ରହଲ୍ଲାଦ ସାହୁ

**Why included:** Profiled as Gayak Samrat in September 2018; honorific, not a national award label.

**Work:** Reported repertoire includes Raghuvamsha, Shishupala Vadha, Rukmini Parinaya and Naishadha Charita; draws on Odia, Sanskrit and Hindi poets.

**How the culture carries forward:** Profile describes learning as a bayak with Udayanath Dash, then receiving his books and performance implements. Secondary account of apprenticeship and material transmission.

**Learning route:** Udayanath Dash. These are source-specific credits, not a complete or exclusive lineage.

**Evidence date and limits:** Profile 23 September 2018. No current touring, performance-night totals or pupil count adopted. Foundation biography names Biswanath Pattajoshi; this may describe another teacher, not an exclusive lineage.

Sources: [Prahallad Sahoo — Odisha Sambad profile](https://odishasambad.in/gayak-samrat-prahllad-sahoo/).

## What these lives show

**Apprenticeship is more than memorising an episode.** The sources connect Sanskrit study, musical coaching, dance and akhada rehearsal. Laxmidhar Rout’s citation explicitly describes pedagogy; Chandrasekhar Padhi’s account shows how a household and several teachers could become an entrance into performance. Passing on books and the chamara in Prahallad Sahoo’s profile provides a particularly tangible account of transmission.

**The library lives on the stage and on the page.** Bhanja literature, Sanskrit kavya, Odissi song forms and epic episodes become spoken explanation and sung narrative. Rout’s writing and reported 2024 translation show another route: preparing texts that readers and performers can return to. This does not establish that every older composition is still performed today.

**Organisation and hosting matter.** Behera’s citation and Beura’s profile describe councils and institutional work. The six 2012 programme credits show state festival support. The evidence does not measure whether funding was adequate, present livelihoods improved or those offices still exist in the same form.

**Women belong in the core account.** Santilata Sahu, Manorama Panda and Kumudini Patel have named repertoire and institutional programme evidence. Their shorter entries reflect gaps in our research, not lesser importance. We still need dated biographies, their own accounts of apprenticeship, students and current recordings.

**Recording expands the routes into Pala.** UKS TV carries named performances and an akhada-labelled clip. That makes recorded work discoverable; views do not establish unique listeners, income, apprenticeship or successful preservation. No full recording analysis, rights clearance or media republication was performed in this pass.

## What needs deeper work

- **Ensemble credits:** identify the bayak, sri palia and other palias for particular performances instead of treating the gayak as the whole troupe.
- **Women’s biographies:** verify schools, students, repertoire and current activity through dated institutional or performer-authored records.
- **Additional legacy artists:** Markandeya Bahinipati, Kashinath Jena and Niranjan Kar have discovery leads. The foundation’s documentary notices alone do not establish full biographies or film contents.
- **Editions and recordings:** inspect title pages, original authors and publishers; identify performance occasions and recording rights.
- **Conflicts:** Behera’s birth year is unresolved; one Beura profile’s detailed youth chronology is quarantined. Neither issue is silently “corrected” by guessing.
- **Livelihoods:** travel, payment, hosting and access to teaching require fresh evidence. No interviews or human specialist review are claimed.
