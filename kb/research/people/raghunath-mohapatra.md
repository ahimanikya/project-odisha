# Raghunath Mohapatra

A sculptor from Puri district, Mohapatra served in Odisha’s Handicraft Training and Designing Centre. He was nominated to Rajya Sabha in July 2018 and died on 9 May 2021, according to its obituary record.

The President’s Secretariat credits him with the [Sun God in Parliament](../works/mohapatra-sun-god-parliament.md) and a [wooden Buddha in Paris](../works/mohapatra-wooden-buddha-paris.md). These are historical attributions; present display needs confirmation.
