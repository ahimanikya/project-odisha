Barunei is a hill and shrine landscape in Khordha. Its temple is associated with two goddesses, Barunei and Karunei, whose worship connects the place with the former Khordha kingdom.[^barik-2006-barunei-karunei]

## Shrine and royal memory

In a 2006 *Orissa Review* essay, Pabitra Mohan Barik places Rama Chandra Dev’s capital at the foot of the hill. He describes the paired deities as protectors of Khordha fort, worshipped by the Paikas.[^barik-2006-barunei-karunei]

## Remembering 1817

The hill is also associated with the Paika uprising of 1817. In a 2018 address at IIT Bhubaneswar, President Ram Nath Kovind recalled Bakshi Jagabandhu’s leadership while speaking at the hill’s foot. In December 2019, he visited Barunei to lay the foundation stone for a Paika Vidroh memorial.[^president-2018-barunei-paika]

## Planning a visit

Odisha Tourism includes Barunei among Khordha’s hill outings. Its district guide names Khordha Road Junction and Bhubaneswar’s airport as the wider gateways, and recommends October–March for the district. Check the final approach and shrine access locally.[^odisha-tourism-khordha-20261125]
