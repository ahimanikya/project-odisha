Belghar lies within Tumudibandha block in Kandhamal. Hills and forest give the locality its setting in this part of Odisha.[^belghar-tribal-atlas]

## Forest and a wooden landmark

The Balliguda Forest Division’s working plan describes dense forest at Belghar. It also identifies the area’s lower river-edge and persistently moist valleys among semi-evergreen forest habitats, where climbers and ferns form part of the vegetation. The Forest Department’s wooden bungalow was built before 1980, according to the plan.[^balliguda-working-plan-2021-2031]

## Kutia Kandha and Kui

The *Tribal Atlas of Odisha* locates the Kutia Kandha Development Agency at Belghar and dates its establishment to 1978–79. Its account identifies Kui as the Kutia Kandha community’s language.[^belghar-tribal-atlas] Ask before photographing people or entering homes.

## Planning a visit

The working plan places Belghar on a road approach from Balliguda.[^balliguda-working-plan-2021-2031] Kandhamal’s district page names Tumudibandha as the nearest bus stop.[^belghar-district] Before travelling, confirm the final route and any accommodation arrangements through the Odisha Forest Development Corporation’s official camp directory, which includes Belghar Nature Camp.[^belghar-ofdc-contact]
