Deomali is Odisha’s highest peak, in the hills of Koraput.[^tourism] Its open upper landscape gives the destination much of its character: grass-covered plateaus, exposed rock and steep escarpments.[^flora]

Below the open grassland, wooded stream valleys offer different habitats.[^butterfly]

## An open plateau, a living landscape

A 2025 Koraput Forest Division survey recorded 339 plant species across its Deomali study area, including 104 on the tableland. It describes seasonal-water depressions and streams connected to the Kolab basin, alongside grazing and forest-produce collection.[^flora] The open ground supports plants, water and working lives; it deserves attention beyond the search for a picnic spot.

## What the stream valleys reveal

One small butterfly offers a glimpse of the landscape below. On 18 March 2017, researchers recorded a male Tamil Treebrown beside a perennial stream with bamboo vegetation at Deomali. Their study, published in 2019, reported Odisha’s first record of this butterfly in 129 years; the earlier record was from Khurda in 1888.[^butterfly]

That was a gap in recorded observations, not evidence that the butterfly had disappeared from Odisha. Nor does the study promise a sighting on a visit. Its account of moist woodland, streamside vegetation and plateau grassland is a reason to notice the changes in habitat, instead of treating every green slope as the same scenery.[^butterfly]

## Planning the journey

Koraput is a useful starting point for planning rail and road connections into the district. Odisha Tourism also names Semiliguda and Jeypore, written as Jayapur on its Deomali page, among the road gateways.[^access][^tourism] Arrange the hill journey around your actual arrival and return connections; a regional place name does not establish a direct bus or a guaranteed journey time.

Odisha Tourism recommends **October to March for the wider Koraput region**.[^koraput] Treat this as a seasonal guide, and check the forecast close to departure. The Deomali survey describes winter fog, strong winds and a wet monsoon.[^flora] Leave enough daylight for the return, and be prepared to shorten the visit if visibility deteriorates.

**Confirm the authorized entrance and applicable charges before travelling.** A report dated 11 November 2026 describes the removal of an unauthorized fee gate while authorized collection points remained. It does not provide a visitor tariff.[^entry] Avoid planning around a blanket promise of free entry, fixed opening hours or unrestricted camping. Ask your local host or the district tourism office about the arrangements for your intended date.

For anyone who needs step-free access, confirm the surface and gradient between parking and the viewpoint, as well as toilet access. A road into the hills alone does not establish those facilities. Carry drinking water and bring back packaging and food waste. Plastic waste, fire and road expansion are among the pressures identified by the forest survey.[^flora] Keep vehicles to designated routes and leave plants undisturbed.

## Make room for Koraput

Deomali can be the focus of a hill excursion without having to fit every regional attraction into the same day. Odisha Tourism’s Koraput guide also points towards Kolab, Duduma and Gupteswar.[^koraput] Choose onward journeys according to the time available and current local advice.

The district’s stories continue beyond viewpoints. [Kotpad’s weaving traditions](/stories/kotpad/) offer another way into the region, through colour, materials and the people whose work gives cloth its character. Leave room for those encounters as well as the view from the hill.
