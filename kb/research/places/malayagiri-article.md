Malayagiri is a mountain range in Angul’s Pallahara subdivision. The district identifies Malayagiri, at 1,187 metres, as its highest point.[^angul-malayagiri-about-20261125] Khuludi waterfall lies within this range, near the village that shares its name.[^angul-khuludi-visitor-page-20261125]

## Hills and valleys

Malayagiri’s terrain includes hills and intervening valleys. In their 2012 study, Sudam Charan Sahu, N. K. Dhal and R. C. Mohanty describe lateritic soils on the hilltops and red clay or clay-loam soils in the valleys.[^sahu-dhal-mohanty-2012-malyagiri-trees]

Between March 2007 and December 2009, the team surveyed 60 plots covering 2.4 hectares of tropical dry deciduous forest. They recorded 57 tree species, with sal identified as the dominant species in that sample.[^sahu-dhal-mohanty-2012-malyagiri-trees]

## The water at Khuludi

Rajani3737’s photograph from February 2014 shows water separating around dark rock, with leafy branches overhanging the flow.
