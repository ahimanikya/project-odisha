Mandasaru is a wooded gorge in Kandhamal’s Eastern Ghats.[^board] Its conservation history connects the forest with the people who know and care for it.

## A landscape with local custodians

In 2019, Mandasaru became Odisha’s first Biodiversity Heritage Site. The declaration records proposals from the biodiversity management committees of Gumamaha and Sugadabadi gram panchayats, with Gram Sabha approval.[^declaration] Local institutions have a documented place in the story of its protection.

One plant brings that relationship into focus. The National Biodiversity Authority records that a fern known as Patharagirdhini, *Drynaria quercifolia*, is conserved and worshipped locally.[^fern]

## Visiting the gorge

Raikia is the nearest main town, according to Kandhamal district’s guide.[^district] Odisha Tourism recommends **October to March for Mandasaru** and names road connections from Daringbadi, Phulbani and Brahmapur. It identifies Brahmapur as a rail gateway and Bhubaneswar as the airport gateway.[^tourism]

Decide what kind of visit you want before arranging the last leg. Time at a viewpoint, a forest walk and an overnight stay need their own plans. Ask locally about access for your date, and confirm any guide or accommodation arrangements separately. For a walk, check that the path and gradient suit your party; leave enough time for the return journey.

Leave plants undisturbed and respect places used for worship.
