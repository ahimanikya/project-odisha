Panchalingeswar is a Shiva shrine on Devagiri hill in the Nilagiri area of Balasore. Its name comes from the five lingas beneath flowing water, which the district administration describes as perennial.[^panchalingeswar-balasore-district]

## Water and worship

The stream is central to worship here. Odisha Tourism describes devotees touching the lingas beneath the water.[^panchalingeswar-odisha-tourism] Follow the shrine’s directions and give others room to worship.

In Subhashish Panigrahi’s photograph above, water runs through a narrow opening between large rocks. Marigold garlands and red-and-gold cloth hang across the stone, with roots visible above.

## Planning the climb

A stairway leads up to the shrine.[^panchalingeswar-otdc-stair-approach] Anyone needing assistance should confirm the route and available support before setting out.

Odisha Tourism recommends **October to March**. Balasore, also written Baleshwar, is the rail gateway, roughly **30 kilometres away**, with the road approach through Nilagiri.[^panchalingeswar-odisha-tourism]

Check local access after heavy rain and arrange the return journey before beginning the climb. Allow time for the steps and pauses along the way. Carry drinking water and take packaging back with you.
