## 1. Business in one page

What will be sold, to whom, through which channel, and why here? State the strongest local evidence, the largest uncertainty, and the next inexpensive test. Include links to local culture, people and production that actually relate to this business.

## 2. Customer and market notebook

For each customer segment record problem, existing alternative, willingness-to-pay evidence, sample/transaction period, competing offers, seasonality and source. Planned interviews and sales tests are a to-do list until performed. Record lost sales and objections as well as interest.

## 3. Product, supply and operations

Specification, supplier identity, provenance, quote validity, minimum order, quality checks, storage, transport, spoilage, packaging, returns and waste. Separate a cultural association from a supply agreement. Include production or service capacity and the constraint that sets it.

## 4. Premises comparison

For each candidate: exact site ID/location; owner/allotting authority; lease/shared/buy route; listing date; area and units; rent/price/tenure evidence; access; electricity/water/waste requirements and verified capacity; permitted use; title/encumbrance/rights checks; hazard exposure; site visit status. No candidate selected: unknown.

## 5. Permissions and support

For every potentially applicable permission/scheme record activity trigger, authority, original rule and amendment, applicability decision, fee, time expectation and its source, documents, dependencies, inspection, renewal and checked date. Mark unresolved requirements. Record incentives as applied/approved/disbursed separately and maintain a cash scenario without them.

## 6. People and local enterprise

Jobs/skills required, compensation assumption, seasonal availability, training route and public institutional contacts. List prospective suppliers/cooperatives/partners with confirmation state; potential is not agreed. Include proposed local procurement and producer payment terms, without claiming jobs already created.

## 8. Investigation and setup plan

Sequence: test demand → confirm sourcing → compare premises → resolve permissions → obtain quotes → assess downside and funding → make proceed/revise/stop decision → implement approved steps. Each action has owner role, dependency, evidence, date/status and next decision. Timelines are personal planning assumptions until official or supplier evidence supports them.

## 9. Risks and unresolved questions

Record demand shortfall, seasonality, input price, access, supply reliability, community/rights issues, environmental constraints, staffing, finance and each case-specific risk. Attach mitigation and stop condition rather than a generic reassurance.

## 10. Evidence appendix and update trail

Include stable concept/source IDs, URLs, exact locators, edition/retrieval dates, calculation inputs and revision log. Keep a short public citation list in the main book and optional detailed evidence. Personal documents, bank details and negotiation notes are never part of the public research repository.
