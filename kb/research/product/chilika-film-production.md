# Chilika — a film grounded in real references

Creative direction: Ahimanikya Satapathy, Founder & Editor-in-Chief. Research and production preparation: AI assistant. The Founder asked whether publicly available references could support a realistic simulated movie. This is the practical continuation of the [immersive destination brief](immersive-destination-brief.md), under POD-WORK-015. The initial AI plan was not rendered; the later free real-footage trial below produced a local review cut. Nothing has been published.

## Approach

Create a 35–40 second conventional film from independently reviewed short shots. Use real photographs to anchor composition, local boats, water, vegetation and architecture. Keep camera movement restrained. This is an AI-imagined interpretation of Chilika, not documentary footage or a 360° recording. A photograph cannot establish the unseen geometry behind it.

Image-to-video is technically suitable: the reference establishes appearance and composition, while the text guides motion. [Runway’s official guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/48324313115155-Image-to-Video-Prompting-Guide) documents this workflow. It is evidence of capability, not a claim of guaranteed realism, free credits, or an available account. The recommendation to test one shot first is our production judgment.

## Proposed edit

Durations are editorial targets, adjustable to the selected service’s supported outputs. Separate cuts signal separate places; this is not a continuous route or a recommended itinerary.

| Time | Scene | Treatment and realism check |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 s | Open water and the blue boat | Pilot shot. Stable camera, gentle forward drift and small ripples; original overcast light. Watch hull, passengers, birds and reflection for distortion. |
| 7–14 s | Wetland life | Select a visually checked Mangalajodi reference with manageable motion. Avoid starting with hundreds of individually animated birds. No invented species identification. |
| 14–21 s | Kalijai | Preserve the reference architecture; slight palm movement is enough. No orbit exposing invented sides of the building and no simulated ritual presented as observed practice. |
| 21–27 s | A taste of the lagoon | Food close-up with a slow editorial move; use a permitted higher-resolution image if needed. Do not invent a cook, restaurant endorsement or cooking sequence. |
| 27–34 s | Evening water | Sunset candidate only after inspection; otherwise return to the approved lagoon shot with different framing. No false promise that every visit has spectacular weather. |
| 34–40 s | Closing invitation and credits | Project Odisha · Discover Odisha. Imagine the possibilities. Source credits and “AI-imagined from real references”; retain readable full credits below the player. |

## First render: one 5–8 second test

Input: the existing local `projects/site/public/images/chilika/chilika-lake-hellohappy.jpg`. Upload the actual photograph to the chosen generator; do not rely on a place-name prompt alone. The frame contains people and birds, so even this opening requires review.

Motion prompt:

> The camera holds a steady wide view. The blue boat drifts slowly forward across the lagoon. Small ripples spread naturally around the hull and soften its reflection. The distant birds move gently through the frame. Soft overcast daylight stays consistent. Continuous, restrained, natural motion.

Review the full shot at normal speed and frame by frame. Accept only if the hull stays rigid, passengers remain stable, birds keep plausible anatomy, water and reflections behave coherently, and the horizon and shoreline remain consistent. Reject sudden new objects, morphing boats, invented structures, exaggerated speed and abrupt lighting changes. A prompt is a request, not proof that the output follows it. If a second simple attempt still fails, switch reference or use real permitted footage rather than stacking complicated instructions.

## Sound and delivery

Use quiet water and breeze, with a sparse original instrumental bed. Keep fabricated ambient sound distinct in the credits from location recordings. Avoid specific bird calls without an appropriate verified recording. No imitation of a local singer, sampled famous song or claim of authentic traditional music. No narration in the initial cut; short captions can carry the invitation.

Target a 16:9 1080p web master where the source material and generation quality support it, with poster image, accessible descriptive text, playback controls and sound off initially. Preserve original generated files and generation settings separately from compressed website exports. A portrait edit can follow after the main composition works.

Record each shot’s reference, source rights, prompt, service/model, date, cost, output, corrections and review result. Founder review precedes release. A small player label and end credits identify the AI interpretation without crowding the page.

Following the Founder’s “ok”, expanded the real-footage trial into [a three-scene film with sound](../../collections/chilika-film/index.md): Mangalajodi channels, an open-water boat ride, and the sunset return. The local review files remain outside the public website. Final aesthetic/listening review and any subsequent publication are separate from this preparation.
