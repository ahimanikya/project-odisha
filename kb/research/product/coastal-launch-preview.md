## What the reader receives

Five connected stories — Bhubaneswar, Dhauli, Konark, Puri and Raghurajpur — sit beside coastal meal and stay-area guides. Nine supporting pages provide the homepage, Explore, My journey, journey starters, purpose, Founder, contributions, usage guidance and 404 response. Explore therefore contains seven guides, while the complete website contains sixteen HTML pages.

The focused edition offers ten saveable ideas and one eight-idea “City, craft and coast” starter. The reading sequence does not imply verified driving times or a fixed itinerary. Its new homepage introduces the collection with a labelled concept illustration, real story photographs, an onward invitation and quiet end credits.

## One boundary across the whole delivered site

The technical route/ID configuration lives in `projects/site/editions/coast.json`, outside the KB. Editorial framing lives in [coastal-edition-copy.json](../destinations/coastal-edition-copy.json). The full research files remain unchanged by these edition-specific copy choices.

Filtering covers page links, discovery, correction subjects, save controls, starter choices and browser journey data. The selected catalogue is embedded by the server; the browser no longer imports all the draft content. Bhubaneswar’s held fresco card, language/literary reading collections and unavailable destination connections are absent from this edition. Konark’s existing inline experience descriptions and cultural narrative remain; this is not a fresh comprehensive factual or rights clearance.

The build renders into a temporary staging directory, validates the sixteen retained pages and reachable resources, then removes unused generated pages and assets. Only a successful candidate replaces `dist-coast`; a failing staging check leaves the previous good preview intact. Source assets, the KB and the separate 72-page `dist` draft are preserved. The output contains **53 files totalling 6,730,589 bytes (6.73 MB)**. This is the complete bundle size, not a measured first-page transfer size.

Older saved choices retain their stable IDs, dates and notes. Choices unavailable in this edition remain identifiable as unavailable items in backups and portable books. The coastal and full local previews have different origins, so browser storage is separate; this work did not automatically transfer or clear user journeys.
