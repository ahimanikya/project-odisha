## What changed and why

The five pages already contained useful material, but their endings did not sustain the proposed collection. Bhubaneswar’s principal onward destination was Cuttack; Dhauli had competing groups of related links; Konark’s connections used another pattern. A visitor could discover each page without recognizing the shared thread.

The collection now has one consistent ending: **Bhubaneswar → Dhauli → Konark → Puri → Raghurajpur**. Each shows the current chapter, a short invitation and a clear primary link to the next story. A photograph introduces the next place, with its existing provenance in an end disclosure. The final chapter invites the reader to arrange their own journey and offers meal/base research. The complete chapter list remains available, so the order never traps the reader.

This is an editorial reading sequence, not a verified driving route or a prescribed number of days. No distances, journey times, availability, new literary quotations or new factual claims were added. Connective prose is original Utkal framing drawn from the existing guides.

Useful former connections remain under “More connections to explore”; source material and stable save IDs remain intact. The old `connect-visit` anchors on the affected detail pages remain valid. The newer collection anchor is `collection-onward`. Unrelated detail pages keep their previous layout.

The canonical packet is [coastal-reading-collection.json](../destinations/coastal-reading-collection.json). The UTP site adapter uses the existing design-system typography, colours and button styles. The shared design package stays at rc.5; no adoption by other projects is inferred.
