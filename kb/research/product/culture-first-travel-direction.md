# Culture-first Odisha travel planning

**Direction endorsed by Ahimanikya Satapathy. Recorded 29 September 2026 ODC.**

The Founder responded **“I liked your recommendation”** to the proposed combined travel model and pilot sequence. This records endorsement of the direction below. It does not mark the detailed information dictionary, every PRD acceptance criterion or the current draft website as approved for publication.

## Promise

Help people **understand a place, choose meaningful experiences and turn those choices into a practical journey**. The Utkal knowledge base connects a story to an experience: literature, landscape, local people, food and cultural meaning.

## Visitor journey

1. **Discover:** enter through a place, interest, photograph, food, festival, poem or person.
2. **Understand:** explore history, local stories, ecology, people and cultural connections.
3. **Choose:** compare experiences, food, local hosts and stays using useful, verified information and honest limitations.
4. **Plan:** arrange choices around starting point, season, available days and travel preferences, allowing for transfers, meals and rest. Distinguish checked itineraries from suggestions.
5. **Carry:** take a personal tour book containing daily choices, practical details, cultural notes and compact credits. Start with printable/PDF output.
6. **Contribute:** propose corrections, photographs and discoveries for human editorial review before publication.

## Three connected information layers

- **Enduring knowledge:** history, culture, literature, ecology and documented connections; evidence and editorial review remain essential.
- **Visitor information:** access, opening times, costs, facilities, hosts and availability; checked dates and explicit scope matter because details change.
- **Personal plans:** the visitor's saved choices, ordering and notes. These do not belong in the public Git knowledge base.

Editorial recommendations remain independent of Odisha Collective's commercial relationships. A saved plan is not a booking. Proposed contributions do not become facts automatically.

## Delivery sequence

**First: Chilika and Konark.** Prove the model against nature and heritage destinations. Chilika already has a visitor-guide candidate; Konark currently has a sourced research note. Develop the missing content and practical evidence, keeping gaps explicit.

**Next: saved choices and a printable tour book.** Begin with local browser storage and portable file export/import as described in the existing addendum. Accounts or a paid database are not prerequisites.

**Later: broader coverage and participation.** Expand after the pilot is useful; build a reviewed contribution flow and richer planning capabilities in bounded batches.

The next concrete work is to prepare the Konark pilot and align both destination presentations with this journey. Actual implementation, review and release states remain tracked separately; this direction grants no automatic merge, deployment, spending or commercial commitment.
