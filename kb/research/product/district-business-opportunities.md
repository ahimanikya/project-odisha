# Build a business in Odisha: district opportunity programme

**User-directed scope, 2 October 2026. Research programme established; website features and completed feasibility books are not implemented by this change.**

Utkal should help a resident, returning Odia or entrepreneur from elsewhere answer: **What could I build here, who would buy it, and what must I establish before investing?** Every district is in scope. Tourism sits alongside food processing, farming services, craft, repair, logistics, professional services, health and fitness, and industrial supply chains.

## The intended reader journey

Choose a district → explore connected local assets → compare specific business ideas → inspect evidence and missing information → select a business model and premises → assemble a personal project book → carry it into site visits and discussions with local partners and professional advisers.

The public district page stays concise. Each opportunity card states the proposed product/service, likely customer, local connection, evidence date and largest unanswered question. Detailed evidence, permissions and calculations sit behind the card. Search filters can cover sector, location, capital estimate availability, seasonality, premises type and research readiness. Unknown capital is searchable as unknown; we will not invent small/medium budget bands.

## What an opportunity dossier contains

| Reader question | Required research and output |
|---|---|
| Why this place? | Canonical district/place IDs, distinctive assets and dated production/service evidence; neighbourhood/block precision where available |
| Who pays? | Buyer segment, existing alternatives, seasonal demand, dated transactions or scoped survey evidence; footfall is not spending |
| What do I sell? | Product/service specification, quality, capacity and a small validation experiment with a stop/go decision |
| Where can I operate? | Existing home, leased shop/shed, shared facility, industrial estate or other site route; plot identity, tenure, current listing, permitted use, utilities, hazard and rights checks kept separate |
| Who can help or work with me? | Public institutional roles, DIC/skill institutions, producer groups, cooperatives, suppliers and qualified advisers; skill availability, willingness and commercial relationship separately confirmed |
| What permissions apply? | Activity/scale/site/entity-specific decision tree; original rule and amendments, authority, applicability, fee, process, document list, inspection and renewal, each with a checked date |
| What will it cost and earn? | Dated local quotations; fixed/variable costs, working capital, seasonality, capacity use, wastage, taxes and finance; cash flow and downside/base/upside assumptions |
| What support is accessible? | Scheme version, eligibility, residency/ownership conditions, budget window, application status and disbursement evidence; approval and cash receipt remain different stages |
| What does the district gain? | Sourced baseline and explicitly proposed local procurement, paid jobs, apprenticeships, producer payments and resource use; measure realised outcomes later |
| What do I do next? | Sequenced investigation and setup milestones, dependency, responsible role, evidence needed and unanswered questions |

**Opportunity is a testable hypothesis.** A local tradition or producing district supplies a reason to investigate, not proof of an unmet market. Keep evidence against an idea alongside evidence supporting it. A useful outcome can be “do not invest until this is resolved.”

## From a guide to a project book

The [starter-book template](business-project-book-template.md) defines the reusable chapters. First deliver a public research guide with gaps visible. A future personal book adds the entrepreneur's chosen location, quotes, budget, scenarios and visit notes. Reuse existing repository journey-book print/export patterns after code and privacy review; do not build a second source of place information.

Exported PDF/HTML and portable structured data need an edition ID, generation date, each volatile claim's check date, sources, unresolved issues and an update link. A research draft, completed research guide and entrepreneur-specific project plan are different document states. Calling a plan “bank-ready” requires the actual lender's requirements and the necessary financial/technical review; generation alone does not establish acceptance.

Personal capital, identity documents, credit information, private contacts and negotiations stay outside public Git. Local-only saved plans with explicit export are the proposed first implementation. No paid backend, lender application, recruitment, partnership or outreach is activated here.
