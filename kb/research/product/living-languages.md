# Languages and Living Voices

Direction and concept: **Ahimanikya Satapathy**, Founder & Editor-in-Chief. Prepared with AI assistance under POD-DEC-107 / POD-WORK-114. This batch is a review candidate, not publication authorization.

## What a reader can do

Choose Odia or one of seven additional introductions: Santali, Kui, Kuvi, Saora / Sora, Ho, Juang and Koya. Follow a language into its places, publications and cultural context, open an institutional learning resource, save a reading idea in My Odisha Journey, or prepare a public contribution proposal. Kui and Kuvi remain separate; a script, language, community and district remain separate concepts.

The directory uses the existing manuscript photograph, the credited Ol Chiki specimen and typographic panels. The specimen spells the script’s name; it is not labelled as a translation of Santali. No generic village portrait stands in for a whole language. Nila Sagara, Mankada Pathara, existing type and spacing tokens provide continuity with the rest of the site.

## Evidence gained

- Seven original SCSTRTI-hosted trilingual booklets: PDF page 3 for publication credits and PDF page 10 for a sample comparison table. All record November 2018 first editions. Compilers, publisher and resource link remain together. Complete texts have not been reviewed or republished.
- CIIL–NCERT Kui Primer: PDF page 5 (printed ii) gives the 9 March 2024 first edition and intended learners; PDF page 10 (printed vii) credits the development team. No vocabulary or illustrations reproduced.
- Sahitya Akademi annual report 2022–2023: PDF page 145 (printed 144) verifies the 21 May 2022 Baripada symposium and participating Santali scholars. The event is historical, not a current booking opportunity.
- Sahitya Akademi’s indexed award register identifies Gobinda Chandra Majhi’s Nalha as the 2016 Santali award winner. Direct retrieval timed out; the book itself has not been read. The award date is not presented as a publication date.
- Direct institutional language overviews, catalogue entries and available community profiles rechecked. The Koya profile remained available through indexed text while its direct URL returned 404. Conflicting festival descriptions were removed. The unavailable Murmu birthplace biography was replaced in this candidate with the directly inspected Baripada report.

[Source inspection and file hashes](../../records/evidence/living-languages/source-inspection.json) record the exact downloaded originals and selected pages. Original PDFs and their page images are research intermediates outside the repository. Public pages link to the institutions.

## Contribution and review

The public GitHub proposal asks for preferred name, local variety, broad locality, source or firsthand context, chosen credit, transcript, separate translation, reviewer and rights. A link prepares an editable draft; it sends nothing automatically. No contributor has been contacted or appointed. No issue was posted during verification.

A recording proposal is distinct from a published recording. Consent documents and contact details must stay outside public GitHub. The site has no recording upload or private-consent storage service. Speaker and rights-holder permission, context, transcript and language review are required before publishing audio.

These are bounded **English introductions**. Native-speaker and specialist review remains outstanding, including preferred self-names and local writing conventions. No newly authored original-language text, pronunciation guide, translation, synthetic community voice or full literary text is offered. Existing institutional romanizations are attributed to their sources. Founder acceptance of this candidate does not imply specialist endorsement.
