Each content introduction needs traceable claim sources, honest inspection limits, a relevant licensed visual or an explicitly typographic identity, useful reading or planning links and inclusion in discovery. No invented quotations, portraits, facilities or source certainty. Local language review remains an explicit human follow-up.

Visitor tools must preserve existing plan IDs, notes and storage versions, handle limits and errors, work by keyboard, and expose no private notes through public links. No paid services, accounts, bookings or background analytics are added.

Each completed item will be recorded with concrete evidence. Public release remains a separate Founder decision. Existing source photographs and editorial qualifications must survive the batch.
