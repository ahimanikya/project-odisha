# Balasore and Mayurbhanj regional guides

Founder direction: Ahimanikya Satapathy, “keep going”. The assistant selected the next destination-coverage increment following the cultural trails and personal journey work. This prepares local review candidates, not publication approval.

## What a reader can do

Explore two illustrated, wide-screen guides; find a seasonal starting point, arrival or gateway guidance, places, food, cultural activities and areas to research for a stay. Save each region or its individual choices to the active personal journey: thirteen additional entries, forty-one in the full planner catalogue. Explore search, cultural trails, Fakir Mohan’s profile and the Santali page link into the guides.

Balasore connects Chandipur, Balaramgadi, a coastal meal and a literary reading idea. Mayurbhanj connects Similipal, Chhau, sabai craft, Mudhi Mansa and Ol Chiki. Neither guide treats a community as a visitor attraction with assumed access.

## Content and evidence model

[Canonical regional records](../destinations/regions.json) drive both page content and planner summaries. Each region has a stable ID, slug, English and Odia names, orientation, lead image, facts, individually saveable choices, narrative sections, source references and a next-region connection. Original suggestions are distinct from sourced summaries. The current records extend the existing destination approach for regional orientation; they do not replace the Chilika/Konark destination or detailed-place schemas.

Sources are official tourism, reserve, district, cultural and publisher records. Future opening dates are conditional: Similipal’s general season is not a confirmed calendar for the reader’s trip. Chandipur is described as a tidal landscape, not a swimming recommendation. No exact transit time, room price, performance schedule, host availability, invented quote, poem or oral tradition is supplied.

The two new real photographs total about 195 KB. The Chandipur sunset is a Wikimedia 1280px rendition; Barehipani retains the supplied 960×720 original. Their 2012/2013 dates are visible, with source, creator, CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, layout-crop disclosure and SHA-256 hashes retained. Existing bust and Ol Chiki assets preserve prior provenance. Guide credits sit in an accessible disclosure at the end of the page.
