## What changed for visitors

Readers can search inside their saved ideas, including their own notes, without putting those notes in a URL. Discovery and journey search now share a normalization rule that supports pasted whitespace, Latin accents and Odia vowel signs. Search results have clearer names, reset behavior and keyboard focus after an explicit search.

Saved journeys detect a newer stored copy before writing, warn about unsaved work and keep recoverable exports available when storage fails or another tab changes the plan. This is a best-effort browser-storage safeguard, not a synchronized account or an atomic database. A browser test also exposed and fixed a separate problem: changing the preparation checklist could leave note and day controls pointing at an obsolete copy. Checklist, note and day edits now survive reload together.

The portable book has day navigation, readable dated filenames, a plain-text itinerary and a choice to omit personal notes from books and print. Saved plans and JSON backups remain complete. Photo books report preparation progress and offer cancellation. Local image paths, streamed bytes, embedded data size and total photo budgets are checked before use.

Contribution fields explain their limits and retain help when an error appears. Editing a draft invalidates its prepared public-issue link; changing a selection does too. Clearing a draft requires confirmation, unfinished work prompts before leaving, and stale clipboard responses cannot claim that a newer draft was copied. The browser review used a synthetic local draft and made no public submission.

Shared pages have canonical and social metadata, a quiet footer share control with a copyable-link fallback, a no-script back-to-top link and Escape handling for the mobile menu. Checkbox sizing in the website adapter was separated from text-field sizing after the visual pass exposed a conflict. The larger clickable label remains.

They trace existing claims, distinguish belief and invitation from fact, check practical wording, inspect reading connections and provenance, and record unresolved questions. This does not close the need for local service verification, fluent Odia proofreading, independent historical review or rights judgment.
