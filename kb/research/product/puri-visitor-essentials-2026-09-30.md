## What the sources established

[Odisha Tourism’s food overview](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/the-taste-of-odisha.html) establishes Mahaprasad’s offering context. Its miraculous cooking account is not adopted as fact. [Incredible India’s Puri guide](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/odisha/puri) supports the named regional dishes and seasonal suggestion. Neither establishes a live menu or meal availability.

[Odisha Tourism’s beach page](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/discover/attractions/beaches/puri-beach.html) describes morning walks, the evening market and consulting Nulia lifeguards. Promotional general safety claims are not a guarantee for a visitor’s date. [Puri district](https://puri.odisha.gov.in/en/tourism/tourist-places/shree-jagannath-temple) identifies the railway station and airport. No fare or travel-time estimate was added.
