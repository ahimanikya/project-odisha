## Preparing for the visit

Each of seven guides now has six short questions inside its visit notebook: Bhubaneswar, Dhauli, Konark, Puri, Raghurajpur, coastal meals and coastal bases. They invite the reader to ask about access, return travel, comfort, food, respectful encounters and alternatives. Workshop questions respect the maker’s work and household; stay questions ask about a specific property without implying inspection. The food guide retains its existing saved identity rather than creating new dish records.

These are original editorial drafts, prepared with AI assistance under Ahimanikya Satapathy’s direction. No new field observation, quotation, song, image, permission or factual verification is claimed. Existing sources and quiet end-of-page credit remain. The questions are stored in the [project KB](../destinations/visit-questions.json), then used by the guides, planner and portable outputs.

Before you go now holds personal reminders and questions related to saved guides. Readers can write a reminder, copy a useful question, edit its wording, tick it, remove it and undo that removal. Each journey permits 40 reminders of up to 500 string units. Summary counts show unfinished personal work and ideas still unarranged. They do not rank the visitor’s safety or establish confirmed arrangements.

## Carrying one day

The download controls offer the whole journey, one numbered day or Ideas for later. A selected-day book retains the original day number and calendar date. Trip-wide preparation and reminders remain available; the saved original is unchanged. Photo preparation collects only the selected copy’s images.

The personal-notes choice now also governs reminder wording and ticks. Excluding notes removes the personal reminder list, item notes and private day writing from HTML, text and print. Public guide questions remain. Journey name, dates and chosen places remain visible, as the interface explains. JSON backups retain everything. A journey containing only reminders can also be exported.

## Bringing back a backup

| Choice | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Add as a new journey | Preserve all valid source details in an independent trip. |
| Add new ideas to selected journey | Keep existing destination writing and arrangements; add absent ideas to Ideas for later with their item notes. |
| Select from a collection | Choose one source journey for either operation above. |
| Replace all journeys | Separate acknowledgement before replacing the browser’s collection. |

The merge preview names its destination, counts new and duplicate ideas and explains that source day notes, reminders, date and checklist are excluded. It refuses an oversized combined plan. If the destination changes after preview, the first click refreshes the preview for review rather than applying stale information. An independent import remains the option for retaining all source context.
