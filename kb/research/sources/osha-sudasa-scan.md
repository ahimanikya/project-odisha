# Damayantinkara Sudasha Brata

An undated printed booklet from A. Pustaka Bhandar, Cuttack, survives in an eleven-page scan linked by [OdiaBooks](https://www.odiabooks.com/sudasha-brata-katha-pdf-ebook.html). Printed pp.1–12 contain the narrative; later pages contain worship material.

The cover and opening name Damayanti, who appears as the queen in the story. This is not an established author credit. The earlier catalogue note treating that name as attribution has been corrected; the author and edition date remain unknown.

All narrative pages have now been visually inspected. The reading establishes the Nala–Damayanti plot and selected food/object passages, not a diplomatic transcription or a critical edition. The scan’s digital timestamps do not date the printed text.
