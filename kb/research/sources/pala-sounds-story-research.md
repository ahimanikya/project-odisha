### Source criticism

Patra2017’s role list does not reconcile its performer counts. Its later award reference says Sahitya Akademi, conflicting with the original Sangeet Natak Akademi citation. Neither error is copied. Patra2021 repeats some earlier interpretations; it is not independent corroboration. Its broader historical chronology is not adopted. Mahapatra’s historical causal sequence and manuscript inventory require separate checks; the article’s existence does not establish an origin date. The Narthaki report is an unsigned contemporary account with promotional praise; credits are used, audience response is not asserted as our observation.

Dash2011 remains unread in full: direct secure retrieval failed on an expired certificate; full-issue web retrieval failed. Security verification was not bypassed. Indexed forms are preserved as historical leads, while the revised public form explanation uses the recovered Patra2017 text. Mahapatra’s PDF text was available and its printed p.33 inspected after web screenshot retrieval failed. No new playback, interviews, translations, current venue enquiry or recording permission was claimed.
