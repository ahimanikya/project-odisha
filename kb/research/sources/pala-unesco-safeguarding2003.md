# Convention for the Safeguarding of the Intangible Cultural Heritage

Official text adopted17October2003; Articles2,13–15 read19October2026. Safeguarding includes transmission and education; community participation and customary access matter. Applied as general principles, not evidence of Pala inscription, current decline or an implemented programme.
