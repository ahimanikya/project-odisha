## Field and editorial follow-up

- A practitioner should check Odia spelling, preferred local names, performance technique and distinctions between ritual blowing and staged Jodi Sankha.
- Record place, occasion, performer, teacher and permission; ask whether recording or replay is inappropriate in any setting.
- Ask how performers describe the instrument, shell sourcing, care, ensemble and livelihood today. Do not infer Odisha marine origin from coastal symbolism.
- Seek a public performance with confirmed date before offering a bookable experience. No troupe approached, endorsement or relationship inferred.
- Verify literary originals before adding a famous poem, song or quotation. None is invented for this article.
- A useful first audio pair would be a context-labelled single conch phrase and a Jodi Sankha ensemble passage, only after rights and consent. Synthetic audio is not field documentation.

## Proposed collection record

Store local name and variants; instrument or vocal technique; community and place; ritual/performance/work setting; documented period; origin claims and their evidence; narrative scene; practitioner testimony; image/audio provenance and consent; visitor context; related stories; source locators; review status. Never use state identity to flatten community distinctions.
