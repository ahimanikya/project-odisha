# Odisha coal: growth with the full timeline

**Odisha produced 269.363 million tonnes of raw coal in 2024–25—25.71% of India’s total.** Output rose **88.3% over five years**, from 143.016 million tonnes in 2019–20. These Coal Directory figures cover the state, not just Mahanadi Coalfields.

The path was uneven: output fell **0.90% in 2019–20**, while national share slipped from **24.52% to 23.99% in 2023–24** even as output rose. [Ministry of Coal, Table 3.9, pp.59–60](https://coal.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-03/28-11-2025a-stc.pdf).

## Resources, output and local value

The same directory records geological coal resources at **1 April 2025**, separately for Ib-River and Talcher and for proved, indicated and inferred classes. These are not automatically mineable reserves or annual output.

Six selected block records retain company labels and dated production. Dulanga was flat across the three recorded years; Jamkhani declined in the last year. Blank earlier cells remain unknown.

OCPL locates Manoharpur in Hemgir, Sundargarh. Its [rehabilitation page](https://ocpl.org.in/RandR.asp) includes October 2019 status tables and an objective to double displaced families’ incomes within five years. That objective is not evidence that incomes doubled. Its land figures also differ between narrative and table; reconciliation remains open.
