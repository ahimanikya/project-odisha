## How to read the matrix

- **District measure:** at least one district aggregate, with its own date and definition. All livelihoods cells include banking; this is financial context, not income or employment evidence.
- **Local evidence:** a hospital, university, city, mine or community case. Follow the underlying record for dates, adverse findings and unknown operating status.
- **Guide context:** a saved destination guide; current access and operations still need checks.
- **Scheme area:** a place in the dated homestay portal configuration. It does not establish operating stays or bookings.
- **Not mapped:** no eligible evidence mapped in the declared inputs. This is an evidence gap, never a zero value.
