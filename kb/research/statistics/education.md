# Education

The 2024–25 UDISE report records 61,565 schools, 7,644,052 enrolments and 344,116 teachers in Odisha. These counts describe the scale of the education system; the facility and schooling measures below add context without claiming learning outcomes.[^people-udise2025]

## What these measures explain

What scale of schooling and facilities is recorded? Enrolment, teacher and infrastructure counts help describe the system; attendance, learning and equitable access need their own measures.

## Secondary-school retention needs attention · 2025–26

**Odisha’s reported secondary dropout rate is 13.7%**, compared with 9.5% nationally in the same UDISE+ table. Both rates cover classes 9–10 and both sexes. This is a cohort-flow measure, not the share of every child who is outside school.[^people-udise2026]

| Existing-structure stage | Odisha dropout rate, 2025–26 |
|---|---:|
| Primary, classes 1–5 | 0.2% |
| Upper primary, classes 6–8 | 2.2% |
| Secondary, classes 9–10 | 13.7% |

The report was released on 7 July 2026; these observations are newly captured here, not newly released this week. The older 2024–25 system counts remain dated above. No trend or causal explanation is inferred, and these stages must not be mixed with NEP classes 9–12.
