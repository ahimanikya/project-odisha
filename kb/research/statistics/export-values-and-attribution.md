# Export values and attribution

This topic links the export observations in the statistical atlas to their canonical explanations. It does not create a separate series.

- [Annual merchandise exports](../trade/merchandise-exports.md) retains sector values, periods and the held 2021–22 subtotal discrepancy.
- [Export destinations](../trade/export-destinations.md) retains country shares and denominator limits.
- [Recovery story](../stories/exports-recovery-and-concentration.md) explains the matched 2023–24 comparison.
- [District business questions](../trade/export-business-questions.md) connects evidence to questions that require further local verification.

State attribution is exporter-reported; it is not proof of production location. Goods, software/electronics and combined totals remain separate, and country shares are not joined to individual products or districts.
