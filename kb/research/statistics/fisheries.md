# Fisheries

## What these measures explain

How does fish move through production and export measures? Freshwater, brackish-water and marine output explain the production base; export volume and value describe a different market outcome.

## Latest recorded period: 2025–26

**Odisha reported 12.70 lakh tonnes of fish production in 2025–26**, compared with 11.92 lakh tonnes in 2024–25: approximately **6.5% growth**, calculated from rounded reported totals. This is production, not exports or fisher income.[^weekly-fisheries-jul2026][^nature-fish-pib]

The new release also reports seafood exports of **1,00,897 metric tonnes, valued at ₹5,428.67 crore**, for 2025–26. Export attribution has not been harmonised with the earlier survey series, so no export growth rate is claimed.[^weekly-fisheries-jul2026]
