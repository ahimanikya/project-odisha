This pass downloaded the full public [BDK edition](https://bdkamerica.org/download/1865), *The Great Tang Dynasty Record of the Western Regions*,1996. Local inspection copy: `/private/tmp/odisha-buddhist-research/xuanzang-li1996.pdf` (416PDF pages). Printed269–270 are PDF285–286 and were directly read.

Printed269 describes Uda, its inhabitants' interest in learning, more than100 monasteries and over10000 monks; then introduces Pushpagiri separately. This establishes the regional grammatical scope of the figures. It does not establish enrolment at Pushpagiri or any named excavated institution. Printed269–270 contains accounts of miraculous light and a canopy adhering to a stupa; these remain attributed religious testimony. The narrative subsequently moves to Caritra and Kongoda, but the connected article does not assign their descriptions to a modern port or make an unsupported direct maritime route.

Do not equate “monks” with “students,” or the historical country of Uda exactly with current Odisha's administrative boundary. The public draft paraphrases the limited relevant passage rather than quoting at length.

## Synthesis claims and limits

| Connection | Evidence | Limit retained |
|---|---|---|
| Names in clay | Ratnagiri IAR1958–59p36; Udayagiri2007pp1,123,141;2012p201; Lalitgiri2016pp111,245 | Institution/site distinction; Udayagiri has two names; Chandraditya belongs to a particular monastery. |
| Related architecture | Ratnagiri paired monasteries; Udayagiri ambulatory and surrounding settlement; Lalitgiri apsidal hall and multiple monasteries | Related forms do not establish one administration or one founding date. |
| Different relic/text deposits | Lalitgiri2016pp44,51; Ratnagiri IAR1957–58p40 and1958–59p36; Udayagiri2012pp13,214 | Do not transfer Lalitgiri's nested caskets to another site or identify their remains as Buddha's. |
| Learning and practical life | Religious texts plus residential architecture, drains and probable kitchen | No curriculum, degree, ranking or enrolment invented. |
| Layered chronology | Udayagiri2007p41 versus expanded2012pp15–24; Lalitgiri disturbed deposits | An object's date is not automatically a monastery's foundation date. |
| Conservation | Ratnagiri IAR1960–61pp30,81; Lalitgiri2016p54 | Reassembled architecture/portable objects are not necessarily untouched ancient arrangements. |
| Pushpagiri debate | Udayagiri2007pp12–17 and original traveller passage | Historical identification proposals remain attributed; no single three-campus university asserted. |

The synthesis intentionally avoids a simplistic sequence in which each site represents exactly one Buddhist school, or all three changed schools simultaneously. That would flatten material spanning centuries into a promotional diagram.
