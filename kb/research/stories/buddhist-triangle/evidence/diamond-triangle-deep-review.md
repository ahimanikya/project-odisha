- Udayagiri's main monastery range is not extended to all earlier remains. The synthesis explains palaeographic, stylistic and structural dating rather than assigning a single shared founding date.

The passage first describes the historical country Uda and gives more than one hundred monasteries and over ten thousand monks, then introduces Pushpagiri. The article correctly keeps the figures regional, calls them monks rather than students, and does not equate the ancient territory precisely with current Odisha. Miraculous descriptions remain attributed religious testimony.
- Pushpagiri/Langudi identification remains a scholarly question. The article does not claim that recovered institutional names alone resolve every historical identification.
- UNESCO status is expressly tentative, not World Heritage inscription. Current district split is correct.
