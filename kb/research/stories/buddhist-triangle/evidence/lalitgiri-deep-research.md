## Internal disagreements and production decisions

1. **Apsidal hall:** p.38 associates development with the fourth century; p.80 proposes sixth–seventh-century construction of a phase and later use. Discussions mix earlier stupa, enlargement and hall. Public draft does not impose one precise construction year.
2. **Monastery 2:** p.40 places occupation in the eleventh–thirteenth centuries; p.104 says post-thirteenth. Article uses “later” without a precise terminal century.
3. **Sealings:** p.111 summarises eighth–ninth-century examples and earlier “Sri Chandra…” fragments. Epigraphy p.245 dates Reg.282 to the tenth century and Reg.281 to the seventh. UNESCO's ninth–tenth-century summary does not resolve this. Article gives the secure institutional name without one blanket sealing date.
4. **Dimensions:** “sq.m” sometimes appears to mean a square with that side length. Monastery 1's explicit 36×36m on p.95 resolves p.90's ambiguous wording. Avoid other fragile measurements and the mahastupa dimension discrepancy with older IAR material.
5. **Seasons:** title is 1985–91; some monastery chapters mention 1990–92. Cite the report title/main programme without insisting all fieldwork ended in 1991.
6. **Carved head:** p.86 describes a large Buddha head within a stupa, interpreted as a consecrated object. Do not confuse carved stone with bodily relics, or use a tentative identification as laboratory evidence.
7. **Patronage and decline:** broad Bhaumakara context does not identify a donor for each building. Damage and robbing do not identify historical attackers. No unsupported religious-conflict narrative.

## Pushpagiri and present status

The ASI foreword and pp.12,15 explain the Pushpagiri question and Chandraditya sealing result. The recovered monastery name is stronger than asserting a single Pushpagiri University embracing all three sites. Langudi identification remains a separate question.

[UNESCO tentative entry 6926](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6926/) groups Lalitgiri (Cuttack), Ratnagiri and Udayagiri (Jajpur), submitted 22 December 2025. This is Tentative List status, not World Heritage inscription. Descriptions are India's submission; none validates a Buddha-relic identity.
