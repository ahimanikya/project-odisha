# Editorial review of all 27 articles

Completed 25 November 2026. The revised files are linked in the [publication index](index.md).

## Overall findings

The original drafts had useful subjects and generally cautious sourcing, but many repeated the same structure: describe a place, discuss what evidence cannot prove, then ask for fuller reporting. That made distinct subjects sound alike and used space that could carry actual history, process or local detail. Several numerical, chronological and geographical claims also needed tighter boundaries.

All 27 have been rewritten and copy-edited. Independent research supplied specific material about cultivation, food-making, inscription texts, dance movements, archaeological finds, water systems and recent local reporting. Conclusions were shortened and varied. Material uncertainty remains beside the relevant claim; routine access problems and research decisions are collected here rather than repeated in the articles.

The weaker pieces received the largest changes. Dhemsa now describes movement and music. Patrapada includes documented botanical and bee research. Sakhigopal follows the coconut economy through Fani and later trader accounts. Galigabdar includes residents’ 2026 water-use concerns. Gundichaghagi remains a short, bounded place note because the available evidence does not justify a padded long feature.

## Publication scope

These are researched documentary articles, not original field reportage, hotel reviews, live travel advisories or comprehensive academic histories. Quotations and testimony reported by newspapers remain attributed to those publications and speakers. Operator statements are identified as such. Distinct documents from one government body are not counted as independent corroboration merely because they have different URLs.

No creator captions, transcripts, distinctive headlines, anecdotes, photographs or video frames are used. The discovery record stays in the earlier internal inventory. The publication copy credits the independent evidence it actually uses.

The assessment is that all 27 are suitable for publication in the formats identified in the index. This does not imply that every historical document was downloaded in full or that every external link is continuously available. The source register records those access limits. No unresolved assertion was left as a placeholder or disguised as an established fact.

## Verification performed

- Read all 27 original drafts and all 27 revised articles; checked subject coverage against the earlier collection.
- Rechecked numerical and chronological claims against the cited records; dated past proposals, observations, approvals and reporting totals.
- Removed unsupported superlatives, health promises, single-cause disaster claims and current-facility assumptions.
- Reviewed repeated themes and conclusions; checked exact repeated eight-word sequences across the final prose.
- Checked article headings, citations, local index targets and the absence of image embeds, draft placeholders and discovery-creator references in publication copy.
- Preserved the earlier drafts and research records. No external publishing action was taken.
