# Bali Jatra and the seasonal knowledge of sailors

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

The small boats floated at Kartika Purnima turn a maritime memory into a household act. Boita Bandana recalls departure and the uncertainty of a voyage; Cuttack’s Bali Jatra expands that memory into a public fair. The festival’s name means a voyage to Bali, placing Odisha’s coast within a wider Indian Ocean world. [^1]

Behind that remembrance lies a practical skill: knowing when to sail. In a study published in *Current Science*, maritime archaeologist Sila Tripati and L. N. Raut connect voyages from Odisha towards Southeast Asia with the northeast monsoon, and return journeys with the southwest monsoon. Seasonal wind and current patterns helped determine opportunities for passage. [^2]

This gives the calendar a material importance. A sailing voyage involved waiting for favourable conditions as well as building a vessel and assembling cargo. Distance across the sea was experienced through wind, current and the sequence of seasons. The festival preserves an association between a time of year and departure, even though it cannot supply the itinerary of every historical ship.

Archaeological research supplies a wider map. Tripati’s study of Kalinga’s maritime contacts discusses ports including Palur, Khalkatapatna and Manikapatna, drawing on excavated material, inscriptions and literary evidence. Its geography extends beyond a single destination in Bali to links across the Bay of Bengal and the Indian Ocean. Trade travelled through networks of ports, rather than one simple line between two modern places. [^3]

Contemporary Bali Jatra has its own network of exchange. A Ministry of Culture record lists visiting performance traditions at the 2022–24 editions, including Bihu, Purulia Chhau, Kathak and Odissi. The fair’s cultural programme brings artists from different regions into a festival rooted in Odisha’s overseas memory. [^4]

The present fair and the old trading world need not resemble each other in every detail. What joins them is the act of gathering around movement: people travelling, goods changing hands and knowledge passing between places. The miniature boat gives that large history a manageable form.

The remembered voyage required timing and observation as well as courage. A lamp on the water honours departure; the monsoon explains the seasonal knowledge that helped make departure possible.
