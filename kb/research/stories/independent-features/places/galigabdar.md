# Galigabdar beyond the waterfall view

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Galigabdar Rathabali, near Pottangi in Koraput district, is distinguished by water descending a rock face in successive steps. A December 2019 report placed it about 11 kilometres from Pottangi, in the foothills of Deomali, and described its multiple drops as visible from a distance. [^1]

The name also belongs to a village. Koraput’s administrative listing places Galigabdar in Nuagam panchayat within Pottangi block. That location gives the destination a setting in an inhabited landscape, beyond the frame of a waterfall photograph. [^2]

Tourism brought a serious concern into public view in 2019. The newspaper recorded seven accidents in the preceding fortnight, including one death. The district tourism officer said two guards had been engaged and funds sought for railings. Climbing the wet rock and approaching drops for photographs were among the dangers described. These are dated findings, not a statement of the facilities operating today. [^3]

Water in the wider panchayat has also been a subject of local political concern. In January 2026, *The New Indian Express* reported that residents of Galigabdar and several neighbouring villages joined a protest over proposed industrial use of the Nuagaon stream. Protesters feared effects on their community and environment. The sub-collector confirmed that the planned Gram Sabha could not be held and that another date would be arranged. The report concerns the Nuagaon stream; it does not establish that this is the stream feeding the waterfall. [^4]

The two episodes concern different uses of the area’s water landscape. One asks how visitors approach a scenic site; the other concerns residents’ participation in decisions about a local resource. Keeping both in view gives the place a social dimension that a destination photograph cannot provide.

The stepped falls give Galigabdar its most recognisable image. The village meetings reveal another part of life here: residents organising around water and decisions about its use.
