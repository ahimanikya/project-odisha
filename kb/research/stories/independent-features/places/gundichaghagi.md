# Gundichaghagi and Ghatagaon’s river landscape

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Gundichaghagi is a cascade on the Musala in Kendujhar district, close to Ghatagaon. The district administration places it about 12 kilometres from the town and describes a drop of roughly 50 feet through a wooded setting. Its appeal is concentrated in the meeting of flowing water, exposed rock and surrounding forest. [^1]

The site’s name appears in several forms, including Gundicha Ghagi and Gundicha Ghai. Odisha Tourism uses these names for the same Musala waterfall and places it near the Maa Tarini temple at Ghatagaon. That proximity gives the area two distinct attractions: a place of pilgrimage and a river landscape. [^2]

The district’s Ghatagaon tahasil profile places Gundichaghagi, Maa Tarini and Sitabinji within the same administrative area. Together they suggest the variety of places gathered around the town: a waterfall, a living place of worship and a site of ancient remains. The journey through the area can move between natural scenery and cultural history without treating them as interchangeable attractions. [^3]

Ghatagaon provides a point of orientation for these different journeys. At the temple, attention gathers around worship; at Gundichaghagi, it turns towards the Musala and the wooded rock through which it falls.
