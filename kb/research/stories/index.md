# Stories: choose a thread, follow it through Odisha

Begin with something that catches your attention: a meal, a cloth, a landscape or a question about people’s work. Then follow it into the evidence. These paths bring the collection’s story drafts together so a reader or editor can find the next chapter without searching separate research folders.

**This is the project’s research and editorial collection.** Inclusion here does not mean a story is published on the website, independently reviewed or ready to use as a travel itinerary. Each draft keeps its own sources, dates and unresolved questions. Journeys suggest connections to read; they do not confirm transport, access, opening hours or accommodation.

## Start with a taste

Read the kitchen collection first for a broad introduction. Then choose an ingredient, a cooking practice or the work behind the counter.

- [Taste Odisha: a collection of kitchens](taste-odisha.md) — meet a range of food stories before following a particular dish.
- [Cooking with fire](odisha-poda-country.md) — a reader story with poster and video-script concepts; visual and campaign treatments keep their separate review needs.
- [Rice kept for taste, seed saved for tomorrow](rice-kept-for-taste.md) — connect food preferences with named seed-keeping research.
- [From milk to market](sweets-from-milk-to-market.md) — follow the work behind a sweet through a documented Pahala case, with its sample limits visible.

## Follow the cloth

Start with making, then ask how a piece reaches a buyer. The two stories offer different lenses on the same curiosity.

- [Woven in Odisha: meet the making](woven-odisha.md) — a craft-led introduction with onward reading about cloth and creators.
- [Seventy years, from loom to market](handlooms-from-loom-to-market.md) — a dated institutional story; sales and organisational history do not establish a typical weaver’s earnings.

## Read a region through connected stories

These are reading journeys, not booked routes. Choose one to bring landscape, literature, food, art or historical evidence into a single flow.

- [Koraput through a book, a cup and a cloth](koraput.md) — follow connections between literature, food and making.
- [Western Odisha, beyond the familiar refrain](western-odisha.md) — give harvest, literature and landscape their own chapter.
- [Coast, food and making](coast-and-culture.md) — read coastal places through stone, painting, food and cloth.
- [A name in clay across Odisha’s Buddhist circuit](buddhist-odisha.md) — follow a source-led historical question across places; interpretation remains distinct from proof.

## Ask what a livelihood number means

Move from the crop to the payment, then to the costs. Read the forest stories together to distinguish enterprise reporting from worker support.

- [More rice from each hectare](rice-productivity.md) — a historical crop trend that keeps annual setbacks visible.
- [The money moving through paddy procurement](rice-procurement-payments.md) — identify what a reported payment covers before treating it as farmer income.
- [What remains after growing the rice?](rice-income-after-costs.md) — a historical case about costs and returns; it does not price farming today.
- [Forest products, local enterprise](van-dhan-enterprises.md) — named historical processing leads and the question of who receives value.
- [Forest work and the support behind the trade](forest-worker-support.md) — distinguish assistance categories from wages, sales and household prosperity.

## Look beyond the headline

A growth total, an industrial sale or an education ranking can open a story. The next step is to identify its entity, period and denominator.

- [Growing Odisha: numbers with a human setting](growing-odisha.md) — read economic measures with the people and questions they leave out.
- [Beyond the metal: Odisha’s small suppliers](minerals-and-local-suppliers.md) — a company procurement case; purchases do not measure supplier profit or jobs.
- [Odisha’s ferrochrome markets](odisha-ferrochrome-markets.md) — a company trade example with its reporting year and scope retained.
- [Bhubaneswar beyond the rank](odisha-university-evidence.md) — separate dated ranking scores and positions from graduate-cohort outcomes.
- [Institution building, one stage at a time](institution-building-evidence.md) — keep a university’s accounts and tuition-support figures within their named institutional boundaries.
