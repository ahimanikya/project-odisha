# Bhubaneswar beyond the rank: reading the 2025 university evidence

A city can live in the imagination through its stone, its food and the journeys people make to it. A university adds another question: what might someone become here? For Bhubaneswar, a pair of entries in a national ranking offers a doorway into that question. The numbers invite attention; reading them carefully gives the story its substance.

## Two names, one dated comparison

In the **2025 NIRF University category**, Siksha O Anusandhan (SOA) ranked **15**, and Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) ranked **17**. Both are listed in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. These are 2025 category results, not a claim about the latest ranking or every institution in the city. [NIRF 2025 University table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/UniversityRanking.html).

The small surprise appears when we put two editions beside each other.

| Institution | 2024 score | 2024 rank | 2025 score | 2025 rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SOA | 62.61 | 14 | 63.14 | 15 |
| KIIT | 62.53 | 15 | 62.87 | 17 |

**Both scores rose; both rank positions slipped.** A rank tells us a position in that edition’s comparison. A published score is a separate figure. Comparing two editions does not hold the ranking pool or method constant, or measure an individual student’s learning. The table cannot establish a causal improvement or decline in teaching. [NIRF 2024 University table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2024/UniversityRanking.html) · [2025 table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/UniversityRanking.html).

That is a useful habit to carry beyond this story: ask what the number describes before deciding what it means.

## Follow a graduating class

Now turn from the ranking year to a different clock: **the academic year in which a cohort graduated**. The institutions’ 2025 submissions contain earlier placement and higher-study tables. For their **2023–24 four-year undergraduate programmes**, the reported figures are:

| Institution | Graduated in minimum stipulated time | Placed | Selected for higher studies | Median annual salary among placed graduates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SOA | 2,471 | 2,218 | 237 | ₹6.00 lakh |
| KIIT | 3,611 | 3,513 | 98 | ₹9.70 lakh |

These are institution-submitted figures across four-year UG programmes, not engineering-only outcomes, an independent employment audit or pay for all graduates. The salary column is a **median among those placed**, not an average or a promise to a future applicant. The graduation column counts students finishing in the minimum stipulated time; it is not an entering-class count. [SOA submission, PDF p.2](https://www.nirfindia.org/nirfpdfcdn/2025/pdf/University/IR-O-U-0363.pdf) · [KIIT submission, PDF p.2](https://www.nirfindia.org/nirfpdfcdn/2025/pdf/University/IR-O-U-0356.pdf).

For KIIT, the same submission reports 4,021 placed graduates for 2021–22 and 3,513 for 2023–24, while the minimum-time graduation counts fall from 4,162 to 3,611. The reported median rises from ₹9.00 lakh to ₹9.70 lakh across those different cohorts. A salary trend and a headcount trend can move in different directions; neither tracks the same people over time. [KIIT submission, PDF p.2](https://www.nirfindia.org/nirfpdfcdn/2025/pdf/University/IR-O-U-0356.pdf).

The people behind these rows deserve richer accounts. What did they study? Which work did they find meaningful? What happened after the first offer? Those are reporting questions, not interviews we have conducted.

## Read the city with curiosity

This evidence gives a reader a reason to explore Bhubaneswar’s learning institutions alongside its other stories. It does not choose a course for them. A useful next conversation with a student or graduate would begin with the programme, graduating year and actual experience, rather than a city-wide claim drawn from two institutions.

Before making an education decision, seek current programme recognition, costs and admissions information, and the scope behind any placement claim. We have not verified those here. The [universities and learning collection](../culture/universities-and-learning.md) explains institutional categories and wider research; it also retains the unresolved intake/admission scope issue in KIIT’s submission, which prevents treating these rows as completion or utilisation rates.

For a future visual treatment, keep the two clocks apart: paired 2024/2025 score markers with their rank labels, followed by a separately labelled 2023–24 cohort panel. The story’s connection is from comparison to people. A campus image would need a verified location and usable rights; no photograph or imagined student testimony is supplied by this draft.
