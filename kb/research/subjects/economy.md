**MSME advances outstanding: 120,396.58 INR crore** — 2026-06-30; Odisha.[^macro-slbc-jun2026]

Reported balance at date, not lending disbursed during the year. Sector balances overlap; do not sum into investment. State utilised-credit scope differs from in-state sanction scope.

**Internet subscriptions: 30.56 million subscriptions** — 2026-03-31; Odisha.[^weekly-trai-mar2026]

All Odisha state-based internet subscriptions, broadband plus narrowband, wired plus wireless; density uses projected population. Subscriptions are not unique users; density may exceed 100. Rounded components sum to 30.55 million versus reported 30.56. Prior March 2025 source carried some operator data forward; no unqualified growth comparison made.
