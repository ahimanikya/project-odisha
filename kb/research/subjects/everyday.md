**Schools: 61,565 schools** — Academic year 2024–25; Odisha.[^people-udise2025]

All school management types in the reporting system. Administrative count for a dated academic year; infrastructure reporting does not establish daily reliability, service quality or learning outcomes.

**Households with health insurance/financing coverage: 82.7 percent of households** — NFHS-6 (2023–24); Odisha fieldwork 18 July–28 November 2024; Odisha.[^people-nfhs6]

At least one usual household member covered by a health insurance or financing scheme. Coverage of any member does not establish coverage of everyone, claims paid, service quality or protection from all out-of-pocket spending.
