# What Odisha exports—and what entrepreneurs still need to know

Export research has three separate jobs: describe recorded sales abroad, identify the producing places and firms, and establish whether a new business can serve a buyer profitably. The first does not settle the other two.

## Which products appear in the evidence?

The 20 December 2024 Parliament answer lists FY2023–24 exports attributed to Odisha in USD million. Aluminium/products: **4,490.54**; iron and steel: **2,551.39**; iron ore: **2,541.93**. Smaller entries include marine products **466.44**, non-basmati rice **22.18**, raw cotton including waste **50.65**, and cotton ready-made garments/accessories **24.74**. Principal-commodity categories have no HS crosswalk in this annex. Cotton garments are not handloom sarees; non-basmati rice is not proof of Kalajeera shipments.

The full twenty-product table and its USD11,859.04 million subtotal are in the structured register. Displayed rows sum USD11,859.05 million: a 0.01 precision discrepancy remains visible. The reply’s all-merchandise total is USD11,931 million. Its other annex deliberately selects growing products and must not be used as a balanced picture of all exports.

## Who benefits?

Export value measures recorded cross-border sales, not workers’ wages, local value added or household benefit. Processing can create supplier and employment links, but these tables do not measure their size or location. Follow invoices, procurement, payroll and producer payments before making a local-benefit claim.

## District research leads, not investment recommendations

| Place or production connection already documented | Business question to investigate | Evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Rourkela, Sundargarh: steel production chain | Can a local firm supply maintenance, testing, packaging or traceability services? | Actual procurement categories, vendor qualification, open orders, payment terms and local supplier spending |
| Damanjodi, Koraput: alumina chain | Which service contracts are available to local enterprises? | Plant-specific contracts and operating requirements; national-company exports cannot prove local demand |
| Bargarh: handloom production and cooperative routes | Can accurate catalogues and quality documentation help makers serve buyers? | Maker consent, stock, quality, verified buyer enquiries and margins; broad textile exports do not measure handloom demand |
| Koraput: coffee production and existing retail research | Is a traceable domestic or export order commercially viable? | Supplier lot, origin, cupping/specification, buyer quote, costs and destination requirements; statewide agriculture/forest total is not coffee export evidence |
| Odisha rice-producing areas | Which variety, milling specification and buyer contract can be served? | District/variety-linked shipments, buyer requirements, current permissions and landed costs |

Regulations and incentives must be checked for the actual product, destination and application date. The 2024 Parliament policy descriptions are historical context, not a current eligibility checklist. No contacts, orders, land availability or consent have been invented.
