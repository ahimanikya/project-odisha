# Exploring Odisha through seasonal food

An occasion can give a visitor a reason to look for a particular food and learn the story behind it. Start with [osha, brata and seasonal food](../culture/osha-brata-seasonal-food.md), then check the date and the place serving it before planning a visit.

## What the first guide can offer

For Raja, explore poda pitha and arisa. For Prathamastami, explore enduri and its turmeric-leaf preparation. The osha and brata entries currently include provisional historical associations; they are reading leads, not confirmed events or tasting offers.

A useful future card would say: **occasion and local date → food and variant → its meaning → where it is available → when that availability was checked**. Readers should be able to distinguish “learn about this food” from “confirmed for these dates”. No serving venue or visit date is confirmed in this first collection.

## What a serving record needs

Each offer needs a named cook, shop, event or host; locality and address; the actual dish and variant; serving dates and hours; a dated menu or direct confirmation; price and portion; any advance-order requirement; ingredients and provider-supplied dietary information. Record public access and host permission separately. A public sale can be documented without implying access to a private observance.

For a hosted demonstration, record the host's agreement, group size, language, compensation, photography choices and whether ritual participation is offered at all. These fields are questions for a future consented visit, not completed arrangements.
