# Patent Medicine

The title promises a cure; the story delivers an uncomfortable domestic joke. Chandramani’s evasions and intoxication confront Sulochana’s authority. The later sequence joins punishment to remorse, nursing and renewed companionship.

Watch how speech establishes power. Commands and excuses move the comedy forward; the aftermath brings injury and shame into view. Our interpretation asks how the expectation of a devoted wife coexists with her attempts to control a husband’s conduct. The fictional reform does not establish an effective treatment or endorse domestic violence.

Reading scope: opening and final confrontation, remorse and shared-reading passages inspected in the Odia transcription. A complete facsimile reading, publication chronology and language-specialist review remain pending.
