# Stuti Chintamani: prayer, compassion and English reading routes

**Start with the poetry, and keep the translator beside the poet.** *Stuti Chintamani* is an Odia devotional work by Bhima Bhoi. NBT’s catalogue describes its concern with Mahima Alekha, suffering and compassion. It describes 100 cantos of 20 stanzas each; that is the publisher’s account of the work, not a count independently checked against manuscripts.
