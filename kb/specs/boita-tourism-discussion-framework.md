# Boita tourism policy and entrepreneurship discussion framework

**For Ahimanikya Satapathy and prospective reviewers · 9 October 2026.** Creator and accountable human: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Prepared with AI assistance for the Project Odisha knowledge bundle.

Boita could help Odisha turn cultural knowledge into worthwhile visits and fair local earning opportunities. Its contribution would be to connect two kinds of work: helping public institutions resolve evidenced service problems, and helping willing local enterprises test better visitor experiences. The first useful result should be a small, documented improvement that a host and visitor can recognize.

Begin with the existing tourism research and a bounded learning pilot. Boita remains a proposed volunteer initiative: no team, legal body, government mandate or institutional partnership is established here. This five-part framework prepares the discussion; the later field-based five-page proposal under [WORK173](tourism-experience-pilot.md) still needs observed barriers and trial evidence. No external approach or spending is authorized by this draft.

## 1 Purpose and a bounded first offer

The proposed offer is a repeatable way to move from a cultural story to a clearly described experience, then from a delivery problem to a supported recommendation. For a maker, this could mean an agreed demonstration fee independent of a sale. For a museum, it could mean clearer information about what a group can arrange. Neither example describes a confirmed service today.

Use the Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Puri research already assembled. The [desk pack](../research/collections/tourism-pilot-desk-review.md) contains 22 leads and recommends six first enquiries or visits. These are a selected sample for learning, not a representative survey of Odisha. The [pilot brief](tourism-experience-pilot.md) proposes 20 confirmed records and three itineraries as later targets. Those targets are not current results.

A useful first offer to prospective collaborators would be: review a common experience record, identify which questions each participant can answer, and consider one suitably scoped trial after the necessary agreements exist. Ask for a discussion before asking for endorsement. Expansion to smaller towns should follow local interest and evidence that the approach is useful, rather than requiring them to reproduce a capital-city model.

## 3 Two work tracks with testable proposals

### Governance and public service

**Make the information path easier to follow.** If an authorized enquiry confirms that the applicable process is difficult to locate or interpret, propose a concise public process note maintained by the responsible institution. It could identify the activity covered, the appropriate enquiry route, information needed, applicable source and review date. Boita could prepare a draft for that institution to verify. Measure whether people can find the correct next step; do not promise faster approvals without evidence.

**Test a common experience information record.** Start with stable identifiers, source and check dates, activity description, evidence state, access details, permitted public contact route and a correction owner. UTP already has a record model to reuse. A small interoperable record could be an early Odisha Stack discussion topic, if the Founder later chooses that direction. It is not a state platform, government integration or new database of residents. Keep personal correspondence, consent forms and participant needs in a separately agreed restricted home before collecting them.

**Escalate only the problem that the evidence supports.** Distinguish an information correction, an operator delivery change, an institutional process issue and a possible policy issue. Record who has authority to address it before proposing an owner. A request for clearer guidance is different from a request to change a rule. Technical or administrative proposals need institutional review; this framework supplies no interpretation of legal entitlements.

### Entrepreneurship and local opportunity

**Help a willing host describe a small offer.** A proposed experience sheet should state what happens, group limits, language, duration, what is excluded, compensation, cancellation terms and whether purchases are optional. Use the [host confirmation checklist](tourism-fieldwork/host-confirmation.md) and test the actual agreed activity. Do not require free demonstrations as the price of being included in Utkal's editorial work.

**Build capability around a real delivery gap.** If evidence identifies a need, prepare a small support module such as explaining a craft to visitors, writing a clear offer or responding to an enquiry. The host should help choose the goal and assess whether the support helped. An attendance count is not proof of increased income or business capability.

**Keep commercial and editorial decisions separate.** Project Odisha owns its cultural research and corrections. A future Utkal Collective service would need its own authorization and disclosed commercial terms; it remains held. Boita's volunteer recommendations should disclose relevant interests. Paying for membership, a trial or training must not buy favourable encyclopedia coverage, certification or government access.

## 4 A barrier record and a learning plan

Use the following record only when there is something concrete to investigate. Link to the original experience ID and existing evidence rather than creating a second provider register. Public summaries should omit private details and use restricted references only after an approved storage home exists.

| Field | What a reviewer needs |
| --- | --- |
| Question and scope | Exact activity, place, group and date; who is affected and what they were trying to do. |
| Evidence state | Desk question, reported obstacle, directly observed event, conflicting evidence or resolved. Identify the source and limits. |
| Occurrence | What happened, when, and under which conditions. Separate the respondent's account from direct observation. |
| Consequence | Delay, changed scope, additional effort, excluded activity or cost, with a basis; unknown is not zero. |
| Recurrence and alternatives | Number of separate cases and the selection method. Explain other possible causes; one case cannot establish prevalence. |
| Proposed change | Smallest useful remedy, why it may help and what could make it unsuitable. |
| Authority and interests | Who could implement or verify it, once confirmed; any financial, institutional or personal interest. |
| Trial and resources | Consent, responsible human, funding decision, effort, compensation and stop conditions. |
| Outcome and disposition | Actual result, participant and host feedback, unresolved harm or burden; retain, revise, defer or stop. |

For example, a desk question about a demonstration fee remains a question until a willing host supplies terms. If different answers later emerge, record their scope and date before calling them a conflict. Do not populate this template with invented respondents or illustrative numbers that could be mistaken for collected data.

Evaluate a trial using the following measures. Establish the baseline and method before starting; targets and dates remain to be agreed by the human owner.

| Measure | Calculation or evidence | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Information completeness | Records with adequate evidence for every required field divided by records actually assessed; show numerator and denominator. | Completeness is not quality certification. |
| Planning effort | Comparable start and end points for each enquiry, active effort and elapsed time, with unresolved cases retained. | A small selected sample cannot establish district-wide improvement or causation. |
| Host benefit | Agreed fees, actual payments and delivery costs recorded separately, with permitted feedback on willingness to repeat. | Gross payment is not profit or increased income; a quote is not earnings. |
| Visitor usefulness | Number invited to respond, number responding, question wording and reported problems or useful moments. | Publish response counts; do not generalize from non-response or satisfaction alone. |
| Adoption | Draft shared, acknowledgment, discussion, accepted change and implemented change logged separately. | A meeting or acknowledgment is not policy adoption. |

Use the [operator readiness pack](tourism-fieldwork/operator-readiness.md) for the actual trial decision. Continue only if the agreed activity is deliverable, the host is willing, relevant participant needs are addressed and the proposed cost is authorized. Revise or stop when those conditions fail. Keep a cancelled or shortened trial in the evidence rather than counting it as a successful full delivery.

## 5 People resources and proposed discussion requests

Ahimanikya remains the sole human decision owner. Future coordinator, practitioner reviewer and public-service liaison roles need named willing humans, scoped responsibilities and appointments. Current AI assistance can organize evidence and draft material; it cannot speak for an institution, appoint volunteers or supervise humans.

The existing six-experience research estimate is **₹59,340**, including a constructed 15% contingency, as set out in the [capacity plan](tourism-pilot-capacity.md). It is neither a supplier quote nor an approved budget, and it does not price a visitor trial, new software platform or government rollout. Re-cost the chosen scope before any commitment. Do not assume incentive reimbursement or unpaid human labour. A different trial needs its own cost and funding decision.

The next discussion could ask for three things, each subject to a later recipient-specific message:

1. **A process clarification:** ask the relevant institution to identify the applicable official enquiry or application route and clarify only the unresolved questions within its remit.
2. **A practitioner review:** invite a willing host to assess whether the proposed experience record and compensation questions reflect their actual work. Participation and recording permissions are separate choices.
3. **A bounded learning agreement:** if capacity and evidence permit, agree one trial question, the responsible humans, permitted observations and a review point. No publicity, endorsement or continuing participation should be assumed.

The [Founder TODO](founder-todo.md) already holds the deferred coordinator, shortlist, budget and introduction decisions; this framework creates no deadline or repeated approval request. The next desk contribution can improve the evidence record or prepare a clearly marked draft. Sending a proposal, arranging a visit, making a payment and publishing a host's information remain distinct actions.

When field evidence is available, replace hypotheses with supported findings and prepare the five-page WORK173 proposal with exact proposed recipients, requests, cost, local benefit and implementation responsibility. Preserve the original evidence and dissenting accounts. The measure of Boita's contribution should be a useful change that survives scrutiny, with clear credit to the people who made it possible.
