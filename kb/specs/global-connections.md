# Global Connections

Founder: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Creator of this model and initiative direction: Ahimanikya Satapathy, with AI-assisted research and implementation. Approved direction: a website section now; a separate initiative may follow later.

## Purpose

Help residents, diaspora communities, students, cultural organisations and willing institutions discover Odisha’s international relationships and build useful people-to-people collaborations. Begin at `/global-connections/`. The section includes documented links, a selective Indian-city comparison, practical lessons and a contribution invitation.

## Operating boundaries

This remains UTP work, not a separately formed organisation. The Founder retains final authority. No external institution has joined this section through being mentioned, and no city relationship belongs to Project Odisha. No AI persona appointments, external outreach, membership purchases, funding commitments or internship offers are created by this release.

A private body can research, convene and organise agreed activities. Formal municipal partnerships need the relevant city authorities and applicable state/central processes. Confirm the process with BMC and the Odisha government before proposing an official agreement. Public-benefit editorial work remains independent of any future commercial referrals through Odisha Collective. Do not imply government endorsement.

## Phases and proposed outcomes

1. **Now:** publish the evidence-led section and six-city comparison, separating formal relationships, reported proposals and our aspirations. Keep sources at the end of the page, with local reference links. Add discovery from home, Explore, About and footer.
2. **Next research:** seek a complete BMC register, verify the Bloemfontein lead, and extend the directory to other Odisha cities. Preparation is allowed; contacting people requires explicit authorization.
3. **Pilot proposal:** identify willing counterpart coordinators, jointly design one cultural/storytelling activity or supervised student research collaboration, agree consent, responsibilities and a modest budget before launch. American University is a potential context from the Founder’s family connection, not a partner or an agreed internship provider.
4. **Later:** consider a dedicated initiative only after useful pilots, demand and a Founder decision. Define legal form, charter, funding, reporting and actual human appointments then. No separate repository or organisation is created now.

Proposed first-year ambition: two completed collaborations if partners and resources permit. Record participant numbers, reciprocal contribution, tangible outputs, local beneficiaries, costs, feedback and follow-up. Count signed agreements separately from programmes delivered. Targets are proposals, not public promises or achieved results.

## Information model

For each connection retain Odisha place, counterpart place/country, relationship type, proposal/resolution/signature dates, documentary source and locator, verification date, operating body, latest evidenced activity, confirmed outputs, uncertainty and next research step. For Utkal-led proposals also record human owner, permission, counterpart consent and status. Do not publish personal contact details from informal introductions.

## Editorial and delivery controls

The selective comparison is not a national ranking or exhaustive current census. A historical agreement is not proof of activity. Unknown is not zero. Current website scope includes this section; search indexing remains held under the existing separate search-publication policy. Founder's approval of the direction does not claim an independent factual review.
