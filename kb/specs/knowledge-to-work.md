## Research-to-work programme

The following are proposed delivery streams, not additional active assignments. Each needs a bounded brief and next available work ID when selected. Ahimanikya Satapathy remains the accountable human and final internal gate. No delivery dates or external commitments have been made here.

| Stream | Research basis within the collection | First useful deliverable | Evidence needed for acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encyclopedia and information architecture | about/, product/, places/, culture/, cities/ | Reviewed taxonomy, audience journeys and a small sourced article set | Navigable categories, credited sources, uncertainty and editorial review |
| Food, craft and living heritage | food/, handlooms/, culture/ | One place-based cultural story with linked makers and traditions | Verified claims, respectful representation, image rights and contributor credit |
| Tourism and visitor usefulness | visitor-index/, facilities/, journeys/ | One practical destination guide with a facility-quality research checklist | Dated checks, clear limitations, accessible information and tested links |
| Lineage and global connections | history/, trade/, references/data/maritime-links.jsonld | Evidence-led index of places, movement and cultural connections | Qualified relationships, confidence and counter-evidence; no assumed ancestry |
| Odisha opportunity and statistical atlas | statistics/, economy/, journeys/, evidence/ | One explainable indicator story and an opportunity brief | Definitions, dates, comparable measures, arithmetic and review of source tables |
| Product and technical delivery | product/, technology/ | A prioritized website release brief linked to the PRD | Approved scope, cost boundaries, acceptance criteria and reviewable implementation |
| Community, internships and institutions | about/community-encyclopedia.md and founding directions | Draft contributor, internship and partnership briefs | Clear duties, human supervision, contribution credit and approved outreach scope |
| Blueprint learning and training | UTP operating records and reviews | A documented lesson from the first bounded project task | Actual evidence, proposed model change, Founder review and separate project adoption |

Preserved requirements include contribution through the site and approved Git contributors, analytics, a conversational knowledge assistant, and relationships with cultural and educational bodies. Their existence in the KB does not mean these facilities are implemented. Earlier deferrals and cost decisions remain in the PRD and decision records; AI chat and private backups must not be activated merely because they appear in this programme.

Utkal Collective and Utkal Unbound retain independent charters and lifecycles. Hospitality, farm properties and associated commercial offerings belong to their own scoped work. BAL and BAC remain for later. This programme does not create work or commitments on their behalf.

## How a stream becomes a commitment

Use the [Blueprint research-to-work brief](https://github.com/ahimanikya/utkal-blueprint/blob/main/templates/research-to-work.md). Link its sources, specify the audience, deliverable, dependencies, acceptance evidence and next action, then record the Founder's decision in the work register. Assign contributors within existing human supervision. Close the work with review, the applicable human gate and preserved lessons.

The research import preserves the historical title and wording inside original documents. Current identity is **Project Odisha — Discover Odisha. Imagine the possibilities.** Historical text is evidence of evolution, not an instruction to revert current branding.
