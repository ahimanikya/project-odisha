# Odisha tourism experience pilot

Convert Utkal's cultural knowledge into practical visitor experiences and evidence for tourism policy. This brief is for the Founder and future local collaborators. It connects the Odisha Travel Bazaar announcement to an achievable first pilot in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Puri.

**Creator and accountable human:** Ahimanikya Satapathy. Prepared with AI assistance. The Founder authorized actionable documentation; the pilot scope below is proposed. No host, institution or volunteer has been appointed or committed through this document.

## Why this work now

The Founder shared a newspaper clipping about the MICE incentive portal and asked us to make the opportunity actionable. MICE means meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions. The fuller report also describes a proposed 100-day mapping of food, storytellers, artisans, markets and festivals. Our response is to build on existing research and demonstrate a small set of experiences that local people can actually deliver.

The [source brief](../research/collections/tourism-policy-opportunity.md) separates the official launch announcement, reported proposals and unresolved operational details. A launch announcement does not establish eligibility, funding availability or a working application service.

## Proposed scope and outputs

Start with **20 locally verified experience records and three sample itineraries** across Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Puri. These are planning targets; the current number of field-verified pilot records is unknown. Paradip is part of the reported MICE corridor but is deferred from our first pilot to keep fieldwork manageable. Expand to smaller Odisha towns after assessing delivery and local benefit.

Each experience should explain its story, a practical visit and who benefits locally. At least one relevant, willing host or operating body must confirm the arrangements before we describe an experience as ready for group visits. If fewer than 20 meet that standard, publish the actual number and record the gaps.

Deliverables are a source register, a candidate inventory, verified experience records, three draft itineraries, an operator readiness assessment and a five-page discussion proposal. The [canonical work register](../registers/DASHBOARD.md) owns task status and dependencies. This brief owns scope and acceptance criteria; it is not a second task tracker.

## Selecting useful experiences

Prioritize distinctive local knowledge, demonstrable local benefit, a willing host, feasible travel times and an experience that works for a small group without disrupting everyday or sacred use. Assess opening arrangements, toilets, drinking water, heat and rain exposure, mobility access, language support, group capacity, photography permissions, price and cancellation terms. Unknown answers are research tasks, not evidence of poor quality.

Reuse the [Puri provider research](../research/visitor-index/puri-providers.md), [Puri craft area](../research/visitor-index/areas/puri-crafts.md), [existing destination model](../reference/destination-model.md) and [local confirmation model](../models/local-confirmation.md). Food, craft and heritage stories are discovery leads; none alone demonstrates a currently bookable service. Use the [experience record template](../models/tourism-experience-record.md) to add missing operating information.

## Three itinerary hypotheses

1. **Bhubaneswar heritage and a local meal:** a modest walking segment, a hosted cultural interpretation and food. Confirm entry restrictions and suitable group spaces; do not assume temple interiors permit all visitors or private events.
2. **Cuttack craft and river-city stories:** a maker visit, local food and a heritage stop. Pay for the maker's time where agreed, obtain photography permission and keep workshop capacity realistic.
3. **Puri and the craft corridor:** a hosted craft encounter and coastal or food experience selected for weather, travel time and host availability. Avoid promising access to religious sites or sensitive coastal activities.

These are research hypotheses, not saleable packages. Each draft needs exact stops, alternatives, realistic transport buffers, group size, provider-confirmed costs and date of verification. Keep a half-day and a full-day suitability note for conference visitors and accompanying families.

## Readiness and information quality

Use four experience states: **desk lead**, **locally confirmed**, **trial assessed**, **publicly listed after review**. Field confirmation and publication are separate dimensions. A confirmed host can still have unresolved accessibility or seasonal constraints. Never translate a desk score into a public certification.

Before recommending a visit, require a dated operating confirmation, permission for any non-public contact details, an honest access/seasonality note and sources for cultural claims. Before a group trial, confirm the actual group size, price, transport, cancellations, host consent and relevant venue permissions. Reconfirm variable details before each scheduled visit. Proposed routine review is every 90 days and after a reported change; stale operating information must be flagged rather than silently carried forward.

Personal contact details, consent documents and participant information do not belong in this public repository. Store only approved public business contacts and a restricted-record reference. Restricted storage is not configured by this plan; do not collect sensitive material until its owner and approved location are agreed.

## Measuring whether it helps

| Measure | Definition and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed experiences | Unique records meeting the checklist, counted separately from researched leads. |
| Local participation | Unique willing hosts or enterprises; distinguish contacts, consent and actual delivery. |
| Paid opportunities | Completed engagements and amounts actually paid to local providers, with permission; quotes are not income. |
| Visitor usefulness | Participant feedback, planning problems solved and unmet needs from a consented trial. |
| Policy barriers | Specific recurring obstacles, dated examples and the responsible authority where verified. |
| Government engagement | Sent proposals, acknowledged discussions, accepted recommendations and implemented changes tracked separately. |

There is no outcome baseline or revenue forecast yet. At each stage review, the human owner decides whether to continue, narrow or pause based on willing hosts, feasible costs and evidence. No willing host, unresolved permissions or unaffordable delivery is a reason to defer an experience.

## Budget and proposal package

Begin with existing Git records and desk research. No new subscription or spending is authorized. Before field activity, prepare an estimate for travel, coordinator time, fair host compensation, accessibility needs and contingency, identifying the payer and approval. Do not assume volunteer labour or government reimbursement.

The five-page proposal should cover: (1) the problem and bounded offer, (2) the verified experience sample and local value, (3) observed delivery barriers and proposed fixes, (4) roles, cost and measures, and (5) specific requests with evidence links. Initial requests could be a technical discussion, confirmation of applicable guidance and a nominated liaison. They remain proposed until the Founder reviews recipients and wording.
