# Dhauli visit brief

**POD-EXP-B01 · Bhubaneswar · Desk lead.** Proposed research visit; the host, date, duration, capacity and price remain unconfirmed.

## Story to explore

**Read two different moments in one landscape.** Keep the ancient edict site and modern peace pagoda distinct; ask how a visitor can encounter both without compressing their history into one event. This is a question-led editorial direction, not a promised visitor product.

## Who to approach

Responsible site authority and an appropriately authorized local guide. A named recipient and the exact message require Founder approval before contact. Verify their authority for the specific venue or activity; an introduction alone is not permission.

## Proposed visit

A short interpretive route chosen around the research team’s actual access needs. Start with a two-person research team, subject to the host’s agreement. Treat a 60–90 minute appointment as an internal scheduling allowance only; the host sets an appropriate duration. Agree the date and meeting point before routing the day.

## Questions for the host

Who manages each part of the proposed route? What permissions does each require? Which historical claims have sources, and which stories are local traditions?

## Practical checks

Steps and slopes, shade and rest, vehicle drop-off, weather exposure, guide fee and current venue rules. No show, event or route duration is assumed. Apply the [shared confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md), recording exact conditions and the respondent’s role and date. Reconfirm before travel.

## What to bring back

A route sketch with observed barriers, sourced interpretation prompts and guide/authority confirmation recorded separately. Save a short account of local benefit, one practical barrier and what the host wants corrected or omitted. Obtain permission separately for quotes, recording, photographs and publication; payment is not a rights transfer.

## If the visit cannot proceed

Shorten the route or choose a single accessible stop; do not promise access across the whole site.
