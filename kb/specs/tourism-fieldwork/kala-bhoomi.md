# Kala Bhoomi visit brief

**POD-EXP-B03 · Bhubaneswar · Desk lead.** Proposed research visit; the host, date, duration, capacity and price remain unconfirmed.

## Story to explore

**A material becomes a memory.** Choose one displayed textile or craft object and ask how its material, technique and maker can be described accurately. This is a question-led editorial direction, not a promised visitor product.

## Who to approach

Museum visitor services or the responsible museum officer. A named recipient and the exact message require Founder approval before contact. Verify their authority for the specific venue or activity; an introduction alone is not permission.

## Proposed visit

A staff-led introduction and a separately agreed demonstration, only if currently offered. Start with a two-person research team, subject to the host’s agreement. Treat a 60–90 minute appointment as an internal scheduling allowance only; the host sets an appropriate duration. Agree the date and meeting point before routing the day.

## Questions for the host

Which galleries and interpretive languages are available? Who may authorize a research conversation, photography and reproduction of labels or objects? Is a demonstration possible without interrupting normal work?

## Practical checks

Ticket/guide charges; level access, seating and toilets; time away from staff duties; whether maker compensation is separate from museum fees. Apply the [shared confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md), recording exact conditions and the respondent’s role and date. Reconfirm before travel.

## What to bring back

One object-based story outline, exact label notes in your own words and a dated visitor-access description. Save a short account of local benefit, one practical barrier and what the host wants corrected or omitted. Obtain permission separately for quotes, recording, photographs and publication; payment is not a rights transfer.

## If the visit cannot proceed

Use a public museum visit within its rules if no interview is available; defer any hosted-experience claim.
