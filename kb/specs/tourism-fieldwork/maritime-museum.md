# Odisha State Maritime Museum visit brief

**POD-EXP-C04 · Cuttack · Desk lead.** Proposed research visit; the host, date, duration, capacity and price remain unconfirmed.

## Story to explore

**An object that opens a sea route.** Ask staff to identify a currently displayed object that helps explain maritime knowledge or exchange. Let its label determine what can be claimed. This is a question-led editorial direction, not a promised visitor product.

## Who to approach

Museum management or responsible visitor-services officer. A named recipient and the exact message require Founder approval before contact. Verify their authority for the specific venue or activity; an introduction alone is not permission.

## Proposed visit

A museum research visit and a staff conversation if agreed. Start with a two-person research team, subject to the host’s agreement. Treat a 60–90 minute appointment as an internal scheduling allowance only; the host sets an appropriate duration. Agree the date and meeting point before routing the day.

## Questions for the host

Is the museum operating on the proposed date, and which galleries are open? Is interpretation available? What evidence supports the selected object’s story? What may we reproduce?

## Practical checks

Current admission and guide cost, group limit, gallery closures, approach, steps, seating, toilets and photography. Apply the [shared confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md), recording exact conditions and the respondent’s role and date. Reconfirm before travel.

## What to bring back

Current operating confirmation, object/source references and a visitor-readiness note; no reconstructed trade route without separate evidence. Save a short account of local benefit, one practical barrier and what the host wants corrected or omitted. Obtain permission separately for quotes, recording, photographs and publication; payment is not a rights transfer.

## If the visit cannot proceed

If unavailable, investigate the Netaji Birthplace Museum as the documented reserve; do not silently substitute it as confirmed.
