# From field notes to a bounded tourism trial

**Version 0.1 · Prepared 8 October 2026 · POD-WORK-196.** This pack turns the existing six visit briefs into a consistent way to assess an experience and prepare a small trial. It does not assess any operator today. All six remain desk leads; no host, appointment, participant group, quote or trial is confirmed by this document.

The aim is a visit that is worthwhile for the guest and worthwhile for the person receiving them. A maker should be able to explain a craft without an obligation to sell, a museum should be able to protect staff time, and a guest should know what the visit will actually involve.

Created for Ahimanikya Satapathy's Project Odisha direction with AI-assisted preparation. The Founder remains the human decision owner. This is a reusable preparation deliverable beneath [WORK172's pilot assessment](../tourism-experience-pilot.md); local confirmation and confirmed itinerary work remain open.

## How to use this pack

1. Start with the existing experience ID and visit brief. Use the [host confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md) for the actual enquiry or visit, once those actions are authorized.
2. Copy the assessment below into the agreed restricted working home. Record each answer's source, date, scope and limitations. No restricted home is configured by this document: do not put contacts, consent forms, participant needs or receipts into public Git.
3. Resolve critical unknowns or reduce the proposed scope. Then prepare the trial decision sheet for the Founder. A completed sheet is a proposal, not permission to book.
4. If a trial is later authorized and actually happens, record its outcome separately. An authorized or scheduled trial is still **not trialled** until it occurs. Publication is a further editorial decision.

Keep the [experience model](../../models/tourism-experience-record.md) as the canonical record. This pack supplies the reasoning and decision handoff; it is not a second roster of confirmed providers.

## Assess an activity, not an operator's reputation

Write the exact activity, proposed group and conditions first. “A small seated demonstration without tools” and “a hands-on workshop” need different evidence, even at the same address. Do not award a star rating or publish a league table from this assessment.

Use these states per requirement. Every state needs a note; favourable answers need dated evidence. Recheck variable details for the intended visit, even if an older answer was accurate.

| State | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unknown | No adequate, current answer for this scope. Silence or a blank is not a refusal or a failure. | Name the unanswered question and who could answer it; defer that element. |
| Supported for this scope | Evidence supports this particular activity, group and conditions. It is not a general certification. | Retain the evidence and reconfirm time-sensitive terms. |
| Gap evidenced | An observation or authorized answer shows that the stated requirement is not met. | Record the exact mismatch; adapt or exclude the activity without generalizing about the host. |
| Conflicting or stale | Answers disagree, or their date/scope no longer supports the proposed visit. | Preserve both accounts; resolve before relying on them. |
| Outside proposed scope | The activity genuinely does not require this element. | Explain why; re-open it if the scope changes. Never use this to hide a necessary permission or access need. |

Keep the proposed disposition separate: **continue desk work**, **seek a specific clarification when authorized**, **prepare for human decision**, **revise scope**, or **defer**. No numerical average can override a missing invitation, unresolved participant access need or unfunded commitment.

## Trial decision sheet — prepare after the necessary evidence exists

The smallest useful trial tests one clear question. For example: *Can the agreed activity give guests a meaningful encounter while respecting the host's time and the participants' needs?* This example sets no trial size, date or host commitment.

| Field | Required entry |
| --- | --- |
| Decision requested | Exact proposed activity, experience ID(s), purpose and exclusions; authorization scope sought. |
| People and responsibility | Named human coordinator once appointed; willing host and authority; private participant list and needs reference; responsible person for stopping/replanning. |
| Date and delivery | Agreed date/window, group size and duration; meeting/return arrangements, language, equipment and fallback. Each linked experience must have suitable evidence. |
| Permissions | Venue/activity agreement and any recording uses; rights-holder scope. No public imagery or quotation assumed. |
| Money | Payer, proposed maximum commitment, selected valid quotes, unpriced exclusions, contingency rationale, deposits and cancellation exposure; cite the restricted quote/expense record. |
| Stop or change conditions | Host withdraws; participant fit unresolved; scope/capacity changes; material cost changes; weather/transport fallback unavailable. Name who acts and how the group is informed. |
| Evidence to collect | Actual versus planned duration, guests and host feedback, observed access details, host benefit/payment status, costs and problems. Recording remains permission-specific. |
| Success for this trial | Concrete observations answering its question; include host willingness to repeat and participant experience. Do not treat spending or attendance alone as success. |
| Decision | Pending / approved for stated scope / revise / deferred / declined; actual human decision, date and record reference. No prefilled approval. |

The [₹59,340 six-visit allowance](../tourism-pilot-capacity.md) is a **constructed field-research estimate**, not a visitor-trial price, quote or approved ceiling. Prepare a separate scoped trial budget. In the [cost tracker](cost-tracker.md), distinguish alternatives, selected quotes, commitments and payments. A missing quote is unknown, not ₹0; selecting a quote does not authorize the obligation.

## After an actual trial

Use the [outcome-review pack](outcome-review.md) to agree the observation method before the trial and assemble results afterwards. It defines denominators, missing-data treatment and a blank decision handoff; no trial results are prefilled.

Keep **not trialled / completed / cancelled / stopped early** explicit. Save planned versus actual activity, reasons for changes, dated observations, feedback with permission, costs committed/paid/outstanding and any refund due. Link each unresolved barrier to an owner and next action. A cancelled or shortened trial is useful evidence and must not be rewritten as a successful full visit.

Recommend one next step: revise and reassess, consider another bounded trial, defer, or send the supported scope for editorial review. Host willingness, a paid fee or a successful visit does not itself approve a public listing. Public text should contain only supported facts and permitted material, with its check date and limitations.
