# What a tourism trial taught us

**Version 0.1 · Prepared 10 October 2026 · POD-WORK-215.** A useful trial should tell us what worked for the visitor, what it asked of the host, and what must change before another visit. Attendance, spending and attractive photographs cannot answer those questions on their own.

This is a blank preparation pack. No trial, respondent, baseline, payment or government response is recorded here. It makes the measures already proposed in the [tourism pilot](../tourism-experience-pilot.md) and [Boita discussion framework](../boita-tourism-discussion-framework.md) usable at a later, authorized trial. WORK172 and WORK175 remain open. The [operator readiness pack](operator-readiness.md) governs preparation for the actual trial decision; this document does not authorize one.

## Agree the question before collecting results

Copy the working sheet below into an approved restricted location when a human coordinator is appointed. Before any intake, agree the record owner, access, permitted uses and retention/review date. The public Git copy remains blank. Do not collect participant names, contact details, individual access needs, consent documents or financial receipts here. A coded record can still identify someone in a small group; removing a name is not sufficient permission to publish it.

Choose one trial question that can be answered within the agreed activity. For example: *Can the proposed craft encounter give visitors a useful introduction while respecting the maker's agreed time and compensation?* This is an example question, not a finding or selected visit.

Before the trial, record the activity and experience IDs, date/window, expected group and relevant inclusion needs by restricted reference, responsible humans, evidence collection method and success conditions. The Founder agrees the conditions with willing participants and the host as appropriate; no numeric target is set by this template. Define what would cause a stop, reduction or deferral separately from success.

Record the baseline for each comparison with its date, method and scope. If there is no comparable earlier observation, write **baseline unavailable**. A first trial may establish a starting point; it cannot show improvement against an invented zero. Do not change the question or threshold after seeing results without retaining the original and explaining the change.

## Record events, not just the successful finish

Use one trial ID, linked experience IDs and a dated event record. Keep the planned, started and completed stages distinct. Choose an overall disposition: **not trialled / completed as agreed / completed with changes / cancelled before starting / stopped after starting**. Record planned versus actual scope and the reason for any change. A shortened visit may have useful results without having delivered the agreed full experience.

Keep these four evidence streams separate:

| Stream | Record after an actual event | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery | Agreed and actual start/end, stops, group counts at relevant stages, transport or access problems, changes and dated observations. | The observer's account does not prove every participant had the same experience. |
| Visitor account | Number eligible and invited to answer, number responding to each question, the question wording and permitted feedback. | Silence is neither satisfaction nor dissatisfaction. A partial answer is not a completed survey. |
| Host account | Agreed scope/time, actual burden and time where supplied, payment status and willingness to repeat: yes / with changes / no / unanswered. | A positive visitor response cannot override an unwilling host or an unresolved payment. |
| Money and issues | Restricted quote/obligation/receipt references, outstanding amounts, problems, corrective action and responsible human once appointed. | Quotes, commitments and money paid are different; an issue is not closed merely because it has an owner. |

Give visitors an optional short prompt: **What was useful or memorable? What made participation difficult? What would you change?** Ask the host separately: **Did the visit match what we agreed? What work or cost did it create? Would you choose to repeat it, and under what changes?** Record the language and whether answers were written, spoken or translated. Keep paraphrases distinct from quotations; quoting or using a photograph requires its own permission. Declining feedback must not affect the agreed visit or compensation.

## Measures and their denominators

Use counts alongside any percentage, the observation window and the precise unit counted. Apply the definitions below consistently within a review. Record **unknown**, **not collected** or **not applicable with reason** rather than replacing missing evidence with zero.

| Measure | Definition to use in the review | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Experience evidence completeness | Unique experience records with adequate dated evidence for every applicable required field / unique records actually assessed. Keep the checklist version and justify each not-applicable field. | Unassessed leads are not assessed failures or confirmed experiences. This is not certification. |
| Delivery against agreement | Completed-as-agreed trials / trials that started, alongside separate counts of changed, stopped and pre-start cancellations. Also state how many trials were planned. | A cancellation disappears if only starters are reported; a changed visit is not full delivery. Do not aggregate different scopes without identifying them. |
| Planning effort | Active staff minutes per defined enquiry, plus elapsed time from the agreed start event to resolution. Show unresolved enquiries and age at the cut-off separately. | Active time is not elapsed time. Excluding unresolved cases can make a slow process look fast; unlike enquiries cannot establish a before/after gain. |
| Visitor response | Unique respondents to a specific question / eligible participants invited to that question. Report invitations against eligible participants too. For a rating or yes/no question, show the response categories and missing answers. | Responses to one question do not supply another question's denominator. A convenience sample does not represent all Odisha visitors. |
| Host participation and repeat interest | Distinct willing hosts, hosts who actually delivered, and hosts asked about repeating; report yes, with changes, no and unanswered separately. | A retail purchase is not agreement to host a group. One repeat-interest response is not continuing consent or a booking. |
| Host payment | Agreed amount due, net amount actually paid after refunds, and unpaid balance for each obligation; compare like currency and scope. Keep host-reported costs separate. | Gross payment is not profit, household income growth or net benefit. Do not subtract unknown costs as if they were zero. |
| Barrier resolution | Evidence-backed issues opened, issues with a verified remedy, issues still open and issues deferred, each with dates and scope. Preserve conflicting accounts. | A proposed fix, meeting or assigned action is not a resolved barrier. Repeated reports of the same event are not separate occurrences. |
| Engagement and adoption | Separate dated events for draft prepared, authorized message sent, acknowledgment, discussion, recommendation accepted and change implemented; attach evidence for each. | No stage implies the next. Count unique recommendations by ID when reporting adoption, not the number of emails or meetings. |

For a zero denominator, report **not calculable** with the reason; do not report 0% or 100%. Show small samples as counts first. Do not combine visitor and host responses into a single satisfaction score. An unresolved permission, exclusion or payment problem remains visible even when other measures look favourable.

## Blank review sheet

Populate only after the relevant evidence exists. Leave a date or person unassigned when unknown. Do not prefill successful outcomes or approval.

| Field | Entry to complete |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and authority | Trial ID; experience IDs; exact human authorization reference; question; exclusions; permitted observation and feedback uses. |
| Responsibility | Named human coordinator and reviewer once appointed; review independence and relevant interests; restricted record owner/location. |
| Pre-trial basis | Planned date/window, activity and counts; checklist version; baseline or unavailable reason; method; agreed success and stop conditions. |
| Actual disposition | Not trialled / completed as agreed / completed with changes / cancelled / stopped; date, actual scope, reason and supporting evidence. |
| Results | Each applicable measure; numerator and denominator or individual observations; cut-off date; missing data; baseline comparability. |
| Visitor and host voices | Optional response counts by question; useful moments; burden and concerns; repeat-interest categories; permission for any proposed quotation. |
| Money reconciliation | Agreed due, net paid, outstanding or disputed; restricted evidence references; payer and owner of follow-up. |
| Exceptions and dissent | Observations that contradict the preferred conclusion; stopped elements; unresolved access, permission, safety or payment issues, with handling restricted where needed. |
| Interpretation | What the evidence supports, what it does not, alternative explanations and whether the trial question was answered. |
| Proposed next step | Retain the supported approach / revise and reassess / consider another bounded trial / defer / stop. Changes, resources and dependencies; no implied booking. |
| Actual human decision | Pending until made; decision maker, date, exact permitted scope and decision reference. Keep the proposal and decision distinct. |
| Follow-through | Action, responsible human once appointed, agreed review date and completion evidence. Unassigned work remains unassigned. |

Reconcile money against the [cost tracker](cost-tracker.md), retaining deposits and later payments within the same obligation rather than double-counting them. A refund reduces net paid; an unexpected negative outstanding amount requires investigation. A blank cost log is not evidence of no spending.

## Turn the review into the next useful decision

Prepare a short internal summary: **what we tried; what actually happened; what visitors and hosts told us; what remains unresolved; what decision is requested.** Attach the detailed evidence rather than replacing it with a score. The human reviewer can judge that the activity was enjoyable but unsuitable to repeat under the same terms.

For WORK173's future policy brief, carry forward only supported barriers and their scope. One selected trial can suggest a question to investigate; it cannot establish a statewide failure, causal economic benefit or the effectiveness of an Odisha Stack intervention. Keep outreach drafts and proposed remedies separate from actual communications and adopted changes.

Before any public account, select permitted findings and review whether even aggregates, dates or place/role combinations could identify a participant. Exclude or generalize identifying detail where appropriate; if the approved use cannot be preserved, hold that material. Publish no personal intake, private financial evidence or consent record. Website engagement statistics remain engagement statistics and are not substituted for host benefit or visitor outcomes.

This pack reuses the pilot, Boita framework, readiness pack and cost-tracker definitions. It adds a working method and blank handoff, not new field research, a statistical study design, a government standard or evidence that partners have agreed to participate.
