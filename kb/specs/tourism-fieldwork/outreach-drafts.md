# Six host introduction drafts

These messages are **unsent drafts** for the Founder or a specifically authorized coordinator. Choose a verified recipient, confirm the sender and review the exact message before sending. They request a conversation, not a partnership or government commission. Replace bracketed fields; no recipient identity or address is invented.

## Kala Bhoomi

**Proposed recipient:** Museum visitor services or the responsible museum officer. **Subject:** A research conversation about Kala Bhoomi for Project Odisha

Dear [recipient name],

I am [sender name and role], writing for Project Odisha, an independent initiative documenting Odisha’s places, culture and people. We are exploring a small research visit to understand Kala Bhoomi and the practical conditions a future visitor should know.

Choose one displayed textile or craft object and ask how its material, technique and maker can be described accurately.

Would you be open to a brief conversation about whether this would be suitable? We would first agree your available time, any fees, the activity and permissions. Our initial team would be two researchers, subject to your agreement. Photography, recording and publication would each need separate permission. There is no obligation to host us, offer free work or sell anything.

Please let us know the appropriate person to speak with and any restrictions we should understand. We would be glad to arrange a time that does not disrupt your work.

Thank you,
[sender name]
[approved role and contact]

## Following up

If the Founder authorizes it, make one courteous follow-up after a reasonable interval agreed for that contact. A non-response does not establish consent. Record the result as no response and let the Founder decide whether to close or revisit the lead. Do not automatically send reminders.
