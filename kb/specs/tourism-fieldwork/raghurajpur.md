# Raghurajpur visit brief

**POD-EXP-P01 · Puri district · Desk lead.** Proposed research visit; the host, date, duration, capacity and price remain unconfirmed.

## Story to explore

**Read a picture with its maker.** Invite the artist to select a work and explain its subject, materials and meaning in their own terms. Separate their account from independently sourced history. This is a question-led editorial direction, not a promised visitor product.

## Who to approach

An individual willing artist or household workshop. A named recipient and the exact message require Founder approval before contact. Verify their authority for the specific venue or activity; an introduction alone is not permission.

## Proposed visit

A pre-arranged conversation or demonstration within the artist’s available time. Start with a two-person research team, subject to the host’s agreement. Treat a 60–90 minute appointment as an internal scheduling allowance only; the host sets an appropriate duration. Agree the date and meeting point before routing the day.

## Questions for the host

Who consents to the household/workspace visit? Which parts of the work can be shown publicly? How should names and language be recorded? What is the difference between a demonstration fee and a purchase?

## Practical checks

Fee and materials, no-purchase option, group capacity, toilets and steps, transport meeting point, photo permissions and weather fallback. Apply the [shared confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md), recording exact conditions and the respondent’s role and date. Reconfirm before travel.

## What to bring back

An attributed story outline approved for accuracy by the speaker, scoped image permission and dated visit terms. Save a short account of local benefit, one practical barrier and what the host wants corrected or omitted. Obtain permission separately for quotes, recording, photographs and publication; payment is not a rights transfer.

## If the visit cannot proceed

An audio-free, photo-free conversation may be enough; accept a refusal and avoid entering other homes uninvited.
