# Cuttack Tarakasi visit brief

**POD-EXP-C02 · Cuttack · Desk lead.** Proposed research visit; the host, date, duration, capacity and price remain unconfirmed.

## Story to explore

**Follow one line of silver.** Ask the maker to choose a piece and explain one decision that took skill or time. Their voice should guide the account. This is a question-led editorial direction, not a promised visitor product.

## Who to approach

A willing maker or workshop proprietor with authority over the visit. A named recipient and the exact message require Founder approval before contact. Verify their authority for the specific venue or activity; an introduction alone is not permission.

## Proposed visit

An agreed demonstration or work conversation, with no purchase obligation. Start with a two-person research team, subject to the host’s agreement. Treat a 60–90 minute appointment as an internal scheduling allowance only; the host sets an appropriate duration. Agree the date and meeting point before routing the day.

## Questions for the host

Who actually makes the work? What can visitors see without interrupting production? Which designs, people and processes may be photographed or described? How should the maker be credited?

## Practical checks

Demonstration fee versus purchase price; maximum group; tools/heat/workspace restrictions; seating and toilets; language; cancellation and unfinished work. Apply the [shared confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md), recording exact conditions and the respondent’s role and date. Reconfirm before travel.

## What to bring back

Maker-approved attribution wording, one process account, fee/inclusion quote and separate permissions for workspace, designs and people. Save a short account of local benefit, one practical barrier and what the host wants corrected or omitted. Obtain permission separately for quotes, recording, photographs and publication; payment is not a rights transfer.

## If the visit cannot proceed

Accept a conversation without demonstration or photographs; if declined, retain the refusal and seek another willing maker only with authorization.
