# POD-WORK-005 · Website and brand foundation

Version 0.1 · Review candidate · 28 September 2026 UTC

**Human owner:** Ahimanikya Satapathy, Founder & Editor-in-Chief. Implementation assistance: current AI assistant; this work does not extend the earlier persona appointments.

## Purpose and authorization

Founder direction: “Now we need to build the website - oh we need to decide on brand assets too”. Prepare a working website and a concrete brand choice. Preserve the approved name, tagline and material colour direction while keeping the final mark/type/artwork decision visible.

Serve Odisha residents, the diaspora, learners and curious visitors with a readable first encounter and a traceable route into the knowledge collection. The initial outcome is a reviewable foundation, not a claim to a complete encyclopedia or a launched contribution programme.

## This version

Implementation lives at `projects/site/`, beside the KB. Use the existing Astro dependency baseline and first entry, add a homepage, collection with search and topic navigation, five source-linked research notes, purpose and contribution pages, and a brand studio. All research notes remain editorial drafts. Their explicit build-time selection is separate from any later publication gate.

The homepage introduces identity and mission, ways to explore, a featured person, a small selection of notes and an invitation to contribute. Article pages prioritize reading, sources, uncertainty and credit. The contribution page explains the intended human review process and says that intake is not yet open.

The brand studio compares bilingual literary, bilingual contemporary and compact English lockups. It shows the palette, a small-size test, a reverse-background proof and the new illustration. These remain proposals.

## Completion evidence

- A successful static build and resolving local links, assets and article anchors.
- Search, empty results and query/topic navigation checked in the local browser; verification limits recorded honestly.
- No automatic whole-KB export, active submission endpoint, analytics or remote script loading.
- Real Odia fonts and their licences included; artwork prompts, source reference and hashes recorded.
- Project work, decision, review and history records updated without claiming human acceptance.

The acceptance evidence is in [the check record](../records/website-brand-checks.json). The brand proposal and open choices are in [the brand brief](../reference/brand-foundation.md).
