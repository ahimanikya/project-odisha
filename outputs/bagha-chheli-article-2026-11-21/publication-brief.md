## What this adds

The scoped corpus review found no dedicated article about this game. Chapter 37, **Growing Up in Odisha**, already names it among games in a school-framework discussion. This draft adds a focused explanation of play and a distinction between recorded versions; it does not claim the topic has never been mentioned.

The discovery page was Antaranga Kalinga’s [“ବାଘଛେଳି ଖେଳ”](https://antarangakalinga.org/ବାଘଛେଳି-ଖେଳ/), Shishir Sahu Manoj, 13 November 2020. The draft avoids its nostalgic-decline framing and unrelated second-game sequence.
