# Basanta Kumari Patnaik

Basanta Kumari Patnaik (1923–2012), according to AU Press’s contributor note, earned an MA in economics at Ravenshaw College, Cuttack. With her brother she established Shanti Nibas Bani Mandira, a publishing firm that operated from 1959 to 1962. Her novels include Amada bata, Chorabali and Alibha chita.
