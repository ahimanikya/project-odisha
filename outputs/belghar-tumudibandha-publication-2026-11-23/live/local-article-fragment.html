Belghar: Forest and a Wooden Bungalow

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Belghar lies within Tumudibandha block in Kandhamal. Hills and forest give the locality its setting in this part of Odisha.1

Forest and a wooden landmark

The Balliguda Forest Division’s working plan describes dense forest at Belghar. It also identifies the area’s lower river-edge and persistently moist valleys among semi-evergreen forest habitats, where climbers and ferns form part of the vegetation. The Forest Department’s wooden bungalow was built before 1980, according to the plan.2

Kutia Kandha and Kui

The Tribal Atlas of Odisha locates the Kutia Kandha Development Agency at Belghar and dates its establishment to 1978–79. Its account identifies Kui as the Kutia Kandha community’s language.1 Ask before photographing people or entering homes.

Planning a visit

The working plan places Belghar on a road approach from Balliguda.2 Kandhamal’s district page names Tumudibandha as the nearest bus stop.3 Before travelling, confirm the final route and any accommodation arrangements through the Odisha Forest Development Corporation’s official camp directory, which includes Belghar Nature Camp.4
