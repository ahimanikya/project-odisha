# Independent editorial audit · 30 October 2026

All 41 current narratives reviewed. Recommendations only; no articles changed. This is not exhaustive fact verification or human approval.

## Main assessment

The collection explains its method of noticing collaborators after showing collaborators. Repeated invitations, credit explanations and next-work conclusions make unrelated lives sound alike. Reduce interpretive reprises before cutting specific testimony, names or source limits.

Do not erase DAMaN criticisms, television labour insecurity, constrained access, gender barriers or missing-film uncertainty to create an upbeat narrative. Hope is strongest when a named contribution and an attainable next action are visible. Avoid making prestige or one genre the test of worth.
