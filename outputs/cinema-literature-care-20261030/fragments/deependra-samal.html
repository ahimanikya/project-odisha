Before ଦମନ (DAMaN) took shape, Deependra Samal encouraged its directors to make something distinctive. Vishal Mourya recalled that request as part of the film’s beginning.1

Samal produced DAMaN under JP Motion Pictures; the CBFC’s certification record identifies him by name.2

A producer in the conversation

The directors’ public recollections describe more than financial backing. In their interview with The Federal, they credited Samal with constructive feedback and an understanding of what the project needed.3 They remembered a producer engaged in developing the idea as well as backing it.

Recognition for a shared work

JP Motion Pictures is the credited producer of DAMaN, named Best Odia Film for 2022 at the 70th National Film Awards, announced in 2024.4

The DAMaN story follows the actors, local performers, healthcare context and questions of representation around the production.

The work continues

Samal’s public production credits extend to ପରବ (Parab). Its colourist Sriprasad Sahoo’s project announcement names him as producer, Chinmay Das as director and Anu Choudhury as co-producer, again under JP Motion Pictures.5 The production brought Samal together with another director and co-producer.
