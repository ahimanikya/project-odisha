Pranab Patnaik remembered learning Urdu from friends while an undergraduate at Aligarh Muslim University. Singing Urdu poetry and listening to ghazal singers helped him develop his style. Recalling that learning in 2017, he also spoke of sustained riyaz—practice.1

Behind the familiar playback voice was a student learning a language among friends, then returning to the discipline of practice.

A voice among composers

Patnaik said successive composers had explored his voice differently. Among them were Akshaya Mohanty and Prafulla Kar.1 He remembered being asked to do different things with a voice audiences already knew.

In ମମତା (Mamata), “Re Mita Aa Sangata” brought Pranab Kishore Patnaik and Sikandar Alam together, with Prafulla Kar’s music and Sibabrata Das’s words.2

“Hai Se Harini Akhi,” from ଯାଯାବର (Jajabara), carries Patnaik’s voice in a composition by Akshaya Mohanty.3 Kar and Mohanty are two of the composers in the wider Odia cinema-music story; here their songs meet in one singer’s career.

A wider musical life

Patnaik’s work extended beyond film playback into devotional, folk, Odissi, semi-classical and ghazal music. His collaborators included Vani Jairam, Usha Mangeshkar, S. Janaki and P. Susheela.4 Cinema was an important home for his voice within this wider musical life. The ghazal interest he remembered from his student years belongs alongside these other forms, rather than disappearing behind his film career.

An honoured career, an open door to listening

Patnaik received seven Odisha State Film Awards. His selection for the Jayadev Puraskar for 2016 was announced in November 2017, recognising his lifetime contribution to Odia cinema.45 He died on 21 August 2026, aged 88, after a musical career spanning more than five decades.4

A first encounter with that career need not be an enormous playlist. Begin with “Re Mita Aa Sangata” or “Hai Se Harini Akhi,” then follow whichever song holds your attention. There is room for a new listener in this musical company, as there was once room for the young singer learning from his friends.
