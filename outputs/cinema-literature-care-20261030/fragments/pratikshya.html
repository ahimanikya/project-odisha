A secure job can become more than employment. It can stand for a family’s hopes, the ability to repay a loan and relief from being dependent on someone else. ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା (Pratikshya) places that desire beside a father’s illness, until the son’s hope for security becomes entangled with the possibility of loss.

The Film Bazaar viewing-room synopsis locates the story in a middle-class Bhubaneswar household. Sanjay wants a government job; his father Bipin approaches retirement while the family carries wedding debt. Illness changes the meaning of their waiting.1

A writer, a director and a leading actor

The official IFFI account identifies a short story by Gourahari Das as the film’s inspiration. Anupam Patnaik directed it; Dipanwit Dashmohapatra, playing Sanjay, described this as his first lead role.2

Patnaik’s profile follows the work that led to the film. The Gourahari Das profile opens a path to the writer’s fiction beyond this adaptation.

Alongside Dipanwit Dashmohapatra, the ensemble includes Choudhury Jayprakash Das, Barsha Patnaik, Sreela Tripathy and Roopambika Nayak.3

The pressure inside a household

The Film Bazaar synopsis describes Sanjay learning of compassionate appointment after an employee’s death. The prospect distorts his hopes for his father.1

The discomfort of that premise gives viewers room to think about the relationship between affection and economic pressure. A household can contain care, resentment and dependence at the same time. The film’s premise holds those feelings together, making security a question about relationships as well as income.

A film’s development and its recognition

The Film Bazaar entry credits Roshan Bisoi as writer, placing screenwriting beside the source story in the film’s creative history.1

Deepak Kumar’s cinematography and Deven Mishra’s editing helped bring that writing to the screen. Barsha Patnaik contributed production and costume design; Ankesh Anand and Ashish Pradhan composed the music and background score, with Bivash Rath composing one song.4

Pratikshya later received Best Odia Film at the 69th National Film Awards, announced in August 2023 for the 2021 award year. The result names Amiya Patnaik Productions and Anupam Patnaik as producer and director recipients.5
