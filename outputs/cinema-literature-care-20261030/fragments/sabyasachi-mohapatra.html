In a 2022 interview, Sabyasachi Mohapatra described learning through discussions with his cinematographer son, even when their ideas about an image differed.1 A director rooted in western Odisha’s stories was still working out how to tell them with another generation.

Our article on ଭୂଖା (Bhukha) follows its performers’ livelihoods, musical world and international recognition. Here, the discussion with his son brings those collaborations close to home.

A film must also travel

In a 2022 interview, Mohapatra recalled Bhukha reaching audiences across western and coastal Odisha. He also described promotion as a separate expense.1 Making a film and bringing it to viewers are connected undertakings.

A completed film still needed publicity and places to be seen. Mohapatra’s recollection keeps that expense beside the work of making it.

Mohapatra also argued that art films and mainstream films could coexist.1 His account leaves room for different audiences and ambitions within the same film culture.

The names beside the director

ଆଦିମ ବିଚାର (Aadim Vichar) offers a later point of entry. Its Indian Panorama credits name Kapileswar Prasad Mohapatra for the story, Kumar C. Dev Mohapatra for cinematography, Rajendra Mohapatra for editing, and Pankaj Jal and Ghasiram Mishra for music. Sabyasachi wrote the screenplay and directed.2

What the national award recognises

Aadim Vichar received Best Odiya Film at the 62nd National Film Awards, announced on 24 March 2015 for films of 2014. The result names Mohapatra Movie Magic Pvt Ltd and Sabyasachi Mohapatra as producer and director recipients. It was recognition in the language-category film award.3

Our film companion considers its encounter between a community’s understanding of justice and outside authority. Its credited writer and performers give that discussion particular voices.

A regional world, with room for difference

The discussion with his son returns us to a work still taking shape. Regional rootedness can coexist with disagreement over an image—and with a filmmaker’s willingness to keep learning.
