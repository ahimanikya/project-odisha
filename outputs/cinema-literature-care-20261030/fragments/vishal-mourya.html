Mourya recalled leaving software work in Pune after studying engineering, taking a film course and moving to Mumbai. Originally from Gwalior, he met editor Debi Prasad Lenka on a Zoom television project in 2010.1

The meeting joined two working lives: Mourya’s move from engineering into filmmaking and Lenka’s work in editing. Their later films would carry the collaboration between languages and places.

Learning to work as two

Mourya recalled a period of financial difficulty and a YouTube channel whose earnings helped them make B for Bundelkhand. By 2017, the Hindi film appeared in NFDC’s Film Bazaar Viewing Room catalogue, with both men credited as writers and directors, and as producers alongside Vivek Maurya.12

The catalogue describes a farmer and his son facing drought and a strained relationship. It records a film seeking distribution, rather than an award or a confirmed theatrical release.

Their later Odia collaboration, ବିଜୁ ବାବୁ (Biju Babu), was released in 2019. Mentis Films’ own profile identifies Mourya as a director, writer and founder, and places this film alongside DAMaN and B for Bundelkhand.3

The DAMaN chapter follows the documentary encounter and local casting networks behind their best-known Odia collaboration. Mourya also spoke about the discussions between the two directors.

Discussion before the camera turns

Mourya described a partnership in which either director could challenge an idea. They discussed shots together and worked through roughly ten drafts of DAMaN before filming.1

Preparation could not remove every obstacle on location. Mourya recalled the crew leaving vehicles behind and carrying equipment for miles to reach some shooting sites, with rain and missing mobile coverage adding difficulties.1 The work also had to reach the place where a scene would be filmed.

Their DAMaN received Best Odia Film for 2022 at the 70th National Film Awards, announced in 2024.4

In Mourya’s recollection, the shared drafts and the equipment carried on foot belong to the same production. Discussion continued into the practical demands of the shoot.
