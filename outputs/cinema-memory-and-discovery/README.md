Create one original horizontal 3:2 poetic editorial illustration for an article 'Searching for Sita Bibaha', about a present-day search for a missing 1936 Odia film. Follow Human Natural Image Poetic Natural: restrained emotion and believable physical space, no fake archival evidence. View from an ordinary side aisle inside a modest small cultural screening room in Odisha today. A plain, slightly uneven off-white cloth screen at the far end is empty; soft indirect daylight enters one high side window. Five or six simple wooden chairs, not perfectly aligned; two anonymous adult audience members seen from behind sit apart, one slightly leaning forward. The emotional anchor is patient attention to the still-empty screen, a possibility yet to be seen. No projector beam, no mystical light, no dramatic ruin, no damaged-celluloid effect.
