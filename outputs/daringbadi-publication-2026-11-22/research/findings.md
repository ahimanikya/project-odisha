## Specific decisions and exclusions

- **Season:** “Odisha Tourism recommends October to February for Daringbadi” is accurately scoped. The garden/fall pages extend toMarch. A colder-month preference is not a weather guarantee; no invented temperature record or frost date.
- **Snow/frost:** Do not promise snowfall. Prefer “cold winter mornings” or attributed frost if necessary. No IMD Daringbadi frost/snow record was recovered in the bounded search. Do not pronounce “snow has never occurred” either.
- **Identity:** Use Midubanda in normal prose if matching established local usage, with source spellings Midhubandha/Midiubanda/Mudubandha retained in evidence. Mention Dasingbadi orientation only with F1. Do not copy inconsistent kilometre values or invent stairs/step counts from unofficial blogs.
- **Gateway:** Brahmapur/Berhampur is the rail gateway named by both district and tourism; Bhubaneswar airport is the air gateway. Road continuation requires actual onward planning. Do not claim direct bus frequency, a particular route time, taxi cost or guaranteed transport.
- **Agriculture:** Coffee/pepper shade structure is enough. Do not import Koraput coffee history/producer claims. Regional Kandhamal turmeric context does not establish that every field/product in Daringbadi is certified organic or GI-labelled.
- **People:** Do not treat living communities as attractions or claim a curated park display represents every present-day household. No interviews or named maker encounters occurred.
- **Amenities/accessibility:** Current path surface, gradients, stairs, toilets, wheelchair access, drinking water and park fees remain unverified. Mention only the practical need to check relevant details, without turning half the article into warnings.
- **Scope:** Mandasaru, Belghar, Putudi and wider district attractions are not town-centre stops or a verified same-day circuit. No need to enumerate them here.
- **Etymology:** British-officer Dering/Daring story appears in tourism/community blogs but no archival evidence located. Omit.
- **Activities:** Listings and “soon-to-launch” copy do not establish an operating guided walk. No current reservations/services tested.
