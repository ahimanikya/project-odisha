### D2 — Tamil Treebrown study (primary)

URL: https://threatenedtaxa.org/JoTT/article/download/4485/6597?inline=1

DOI: https://doi.org/10.11609/jott.4485.11.15.15047-15052

Anirban Mahata, Sudheer Kumar Jena and Sharat Kumar Palita. *First record in 129 years of the Tamil Treebrown Lethe drypetis todara Moore, 1881 … from Odisha, India by fruit-baiting*. Journal of Threatened Taxa 11(15), 15047–15052, published 26 December 2019. Full HTML inspected; methods and results sections are the relevant passages.

- Methods: research September2015–March2018; moist deciduous forest, semi-evergreen vegetation beside streams, bamboo, plateau grassland.
- Results: one male recorded 18 March2017 at 1,296m beside a perennial hill stream with bamboo. Earlier Odisha record was from Khurda in1888.
- The 129-year interval runs from1888 to2017, not publication in2019. It describes a gap in records, not demonstrated absence, a new species, or a guaranteed visitor sighting.

This supports a short paragraph connecting the summit landscape to less conspicuous valley habitats. Avoid instructions to bait/capture butterflies. Article states CC BY4.0; figure credit/licence and the actual image must still be inspected before reuse. Public derived prose budget: 200 words.
