## New contribution

The scoped research-map and corpus alias searches recorded in the handoff found no dedicated Dhumpa coverage. Adjacent project writing covers other performance traditions. This article’s subject is collective music-making around the instrument, grounded in a local ensemble account and named public credits. It does not turn an instrument name into a universal construction recipe, invent a performance scene or follow an origin-biography narrative.

The topic was discovered through Antaranga Kalinga’s [“ଢୁମ୍ଫା”](https://antarangakalinga.org/%E0%AC%A2%E0%AD%81%E0%AC%AE%E0%AD%8D%E0%AC%AB%E0%AC%BE/), dated 5 October 2020 and credited to Shishir Sahu Manoj and Santosh Pattanaik. The coordinator verified that discovery page. No prose, song text, image, narrative sequence or factual claim from it is reused as article evidence.
