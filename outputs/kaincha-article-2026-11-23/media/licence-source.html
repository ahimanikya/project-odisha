  About
This project on 'Creating Digital-learning Environment for Design' also called 'e-kalpa' is sponsored by the Ministry of Education, Government of India as part of the National Mission in Education through Information and Communication Technology (NMEICT) and now through National Initiative for Design Innovation (NIDI).
Read More....

  People
This initiative 'e-kalpa' will be collaboratively developed by the three institutions - IDC at IIT Bombay, NID at Bangalore and DOD at IIT Guwahati along with partnership with many other design institutions. The key people are Ravi Poovaiah from IDC, IIT Bombay, Dr. Baral from NID, Bangalore and Ravi Mokashi-Punekar from DOD, IIT Guwahati.
