Ganjam and Kandhamal

Kalinga Ghati: Through the Forested Pass

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Kalinga Ghati is a forested pass on the road between Ganjam and Kandhamal.1 Its natural history reaches beyond the road itself, into the trees, rocks and stream-side habitats of the hills.

Forest beside the road

On the Ganjam side, the Ghumsur North working plan for 2015–16 to 2024–25 describes sal and other trees at Durgaprasad Ghat, its name for this part of the pass. It links the weathering of local rock with sandy soil.2

In 2025, Pratyush P. Mohapatra and his coauthors described a rock-dwelling gecko, Hemidactylus kalinga, named after Kalinga Ghati. Their account places its local habitat in stream-side forest near NH 157.3

Travelling through the pass

Sharp bends, blind curves and fog shape the road journey. In December 2025, IIT Bhubaneswar reported installing two roadside warning units here to alert approaching traffic.4

On 22 November 2026, Sambad English reported that traffic had resumed after a precautionary closure.1 Check the latest district traffic information before planning this stretch.
