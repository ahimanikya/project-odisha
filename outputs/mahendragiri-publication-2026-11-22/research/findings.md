## Narrative and drafting outline

Recommended organising idea: a mountain where the visible shrines and less conspicuous forest habitats both deserve attention. Ground each transition in the evidence above, without a fictional climb, imagined conversation or scenic observation presented as firsthand.

A useful 450–600-word article can follow this order:

1. Brief identity and a clear reason to visit; let the actual selected photograph supply only visibly supported scene detail.
2. Protected temple names, followed by explicitly attributed pilgrimage associations. Keep belief separate from archaeological status.
3. Conservation designation and the different vegetation types. Avoid an inventory of unchecked numbers.
4. One short scientific example, tied to careful looking and leaving plants undisturbed. Do not invite collection or promise discovery.
5. Seasonal recommendation and broad gateway choices. Advise confirming the final approach, permissions, transport and return arrangements shortly before travel; frame this as planning advice, not a report of closures or hazards.

These are maximum allocations, not targets.
