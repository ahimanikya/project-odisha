# Bounded candidate screen · 26 November 2026

Dumuriput was screened and then stopped on the coordinator’s explicit direction in favour of Olasuni. No Dumuriput article was claimed or drafted. Official district search retrieval categorized Dumuriput as Religious, describing its Ram shrine and kneeling Hanuman along the Koraput–Sunabeda road. It did not establish a hill-station identity. District pages disagree on tallest versus second-tallest Hanuman rankings; neither should be adopted.

Inspected indexed sources: https://koraput.odisha.gov.in/or/tourism/tourist-places/dumuriput and https://koraput.odisha.gov.in/or/tourism/places-of-interest . No direct HTML capture, photographic rights review, or current visitor-service verification was performed. Root selected Olasuni; subsequent findings are in outputs/olasuni-publication-2026-11-26/research/.
