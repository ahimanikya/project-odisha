Hills and highland escapes

Explore Deomali and Putsil in Koraput, Daringbadi and Mandasaru in Kandhamal, Mahendragiri and Jirang–Chandragiri in Gajapati, Taptapani in Ganjam, Panchalingeswar in Balasore, Kapilash in Dhenkanal, and Gandhamardan across Bargarh and Balangir. This growing collection brings together hill stations, peaks, valleys and hill pilgrimages, with each place’s landscape and character explained in its own article.
