Find a place that draws you in.

ଦେଓମାଳୀ

Deomali ↗

Grasslands, mountain views and a journey through Koraput’s high country.

ଦାରିଙ୍ଗବାଡ଼ି

Daringbadi ↗

Coffee, shade and hill views in Kandhamal.

Putsil ↗

A valley landscape in Koraput’s hills.

Illustration

Mandasaru ↗

A gorge and its conservation story.

Mahendragiri ↗

Mountain shrines and forest life in Gajapati.

Taptapani ↗

A hot spring and a place of worship in Ganjam’s wooded hills.

Jirang–Chandragiri ↗

A monastery and Tibetan settlement history in Gajapati’s hills.

Panchalingeswar ↗

A hill shrine where flowing water covers five Shiva lingas.

ବାଲେଶ୍ୱର

Balasore ↗

Begin with the changing shore, then connect the river mouth with Balasore’s literary world. Keep the reading flexible and arrange the coastal visit around local conditions.

ମୟୂରଭଞ୍ଜ

Mayurbhanj ↗

Begin with the forest and its rivers, then give a performance, a book or a Baripada meal the time it needs. Shape the stops around confirmed access and invitations.

ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର

Bhubaneswar ↗

Choose between temple architecture, Dhauli, a craft collection and the city’s painted memories. Leave time for lunch.

ପୁରୀ

Puri ↗

Choose time in Puri and a separate visit to Raghurajpur. Leave room for the journey, a meal and a conversation.

କଟକ

Cuttack ↗

Choose among Barabati’s remains, silver filigree, two museums and a dahibara-aloodum stop.

ଚିଲିକା

Chilika ↗

A beginning for understanding the coast through water, wildlife and local knowledge.

କୋଣାର୍କ

Konark Sun Temple ↗

Stone, light and a remarkable architectural inheritance.
