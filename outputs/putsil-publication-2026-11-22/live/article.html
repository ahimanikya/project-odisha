Putsil: A Valley in Koraput’s Hills

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Putsil is a valley destination in Koraput’s Eastern Ghats.1 The name also belongs to a village: Koraput district’s administrative list places Putsil under Dudhari gram panchayat in Semiliguda block.2

Looking across the hills

In Checkright’s November 2023 photograph above, rounded hills stretch across the frame beneath white clouds. The open foreground is covered with short grass and scattered stones, leading towards a low ridge. Bands of light and shade pick out the folds of the hills, with darker vegetation gathered in a dip between slopes.

Odisha Tourism suggests walking in Putsil Valley.3 Before setting out, ask locally which paths visitors may use and whether their surfaces and gradients suit everyone in the group. Give the walk time of its own, with pauses to look across the hills.

Planning a visit

Odisha Tourism recommends October to March for the wider Koraput region.3 Check the forecast close to departure and keep some flexibility for a clearer part of the day.

Koraput’s regional rail and road connections provide a starting point for planning the journey.4 Arrange the final road journey to Putsil separately, agreeing the intended stopping place and return pickup with your local host or transport provider. Leave a comfortable margin for your onward connection.

Carry drinking water, take packaging back with you and ask before entering cultivated or residential space. A visit should leave paths and stopping places ready for the next person who uses them.
