Tensa's forested hills and iron-mining economy belong in the same story. Begin with its Sundargarh/Koira setting; explain its connection with the Barsua–Taldih–Kalta mining complex and Rourkela Steel Plant. Then describe the nature camp's local-community involvement as documented in2020–21 and its continuing official listing. Finish with useful, restrained approach information: Rourkela as a road/rail gateway, Jharsuguda airport, official camp link for current arrangements. A hill-town destination does not establish permission to enter mines. Do not call this an untouched wilderness or a mining-tour product.

About300–450words including compact notes may be sufficient.500–650words should not be filled by repeating scenery adjectives, generic packing lists, project bureaucracy or unverified services.
