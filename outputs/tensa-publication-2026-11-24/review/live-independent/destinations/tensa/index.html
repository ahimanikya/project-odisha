Tensa: Hills, Iron Ore and a Community Camp

By Ahimanikya Satapathy

Tensa sits in Sundargarh’s hill country, with its nature camp in Koira block.1 Forested slopes and iron ore give the destination its character. The Bonai working plan for 2021–22 to 2030–31 identifies Tensa as a hill station and proposes nature tourism involving forest-dependent communities.2

Hills and iron ore

Tensa’s connection with steelmaking is recorded in SAIL’s environmental statement for 2023–24. It gives Tensa as the address of the Barsua–Taldih–Kalta Iron Mines and places the open-cast operation under Rourkela Steel Plant. The document reports 1960 as the mining operation’s establishment year.3

The camp and its community

Sundargarh’s annual report for 2020–21 describes the nature camp’s development through the Odisha Forest Development Corporation, with District Mineral Foundation funding. It records local young people managing activities and residents forming an Ecotourism Group for forest conservation.1 This gives the camp a history of local participation alongside the region’s industrial story.

The current EcoTour Odisha listing describes a community-managed camp and offers short walks around it towards views of the hills and forest patches.4

Planning a visit

Rourkela is a road and rail gateway; Jharsuguda is an airport approach identified in the district report.1 EcoTour advises daytime travel on busy mining-area roads and carrying rain and warm layers for changing hill weather. Use its Tensa page to confirm current stay and arrival arrangements.4
