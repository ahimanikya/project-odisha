Keeping one’s own dignity

ଆଲୋ ସଖି, ଆପଣା ମହତ ଆପେ ରଖି

Link to this Dhaga

ନିଜ ଆଚରଣରେ ନିଜ ସମ୍ମାନ ରଖିବା।

self-respect

Literal meaning

Friend, preserve your own dignity yourself.

What it means

The friendly address makes this advice sound like one person speaking directly to another: preserve your dignity through your own conduct. It may counsel restraint when someone is tempted to join a humiliating quarrel or chase approval. It does not make a person responsible for mistreatment inflicted by others.

When you might use it

During an argument, a friend is about to answer an insult with another insult. Someone close to them uses the line to encourage a calm withdrawal. (New editorial example, not a collected quotation.)

Cultural context

The contemporary witness begins ଆଲୋ ସଖି. The1893 scan appears to begin ଆରେ ସଖି; that difference is retained as a provisional historical reading. The wording does not establish who first used the saying or confine its advice to one gender.
