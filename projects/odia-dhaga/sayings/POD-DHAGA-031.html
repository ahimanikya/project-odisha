SAYING 31 · idiomatic saying

Refusing to be polished into something else

ମୋତେ ଯେତେ ମାଠିବୁ ମାଠ ମୁହିଁ ସେଇ ଦରପଡ଼ା କାଠ

Link to this saying

ଯେତେ ବୁଝାଇଲେ ବା ବଦଳାଇବାକୁ ଚେଷ୍ଟା କଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ ନିଜେ ସେପରି ରହିବି ବୋଲି କହିବା।

resisting-change

Literal meaning

Polish me as much as you like; I remain that half-burnt wood.

What it means

The speaker declares that repeated efforts to change or improve them will not work. The blunt material image can make the refusal comic, defiant or self-deprecating. It voices a stance in a conversation, not a truth that people cannot learn or change.

When you might use it

After repeated advice to adopt a more fashionable manner, someone jokingly insists that they will remain as they are. (New editorial example, not a collected quotation.)

Cultural context

The wood image and the first-person voice are preserved. This is distinct from enduring pressure: here the emphasis falls on resisting another person’s attempts to remake the speaker. The source spells the word ଦରପଡ଼ା. “Half-burnt” is an editorial interpretation of that spelling, not a verified correction to the original; lexical and human review remain pending.
