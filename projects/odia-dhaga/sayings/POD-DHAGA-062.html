Remembering a rescue

ଭାସି ଯାଉଥିଲୁ ମଝିନଇରେ ଧରି ଆଣିଥିଲି ତତେ, ଠାକୁଆ ଗାଲରେ ମାଉଁଶ ଲାଗିଲା ଜବାବ ଦେଉଛୁ ମତେ

Link to this saying

ବିପଦରେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ ପାଇ ଅବସ୍ଥା ସୁଧୁରିବା ପରେ ସେହି ସାହାଯ୍ୟକାରୀଙ୍କୁ ଜବାବ ଦେବା ନେଇ ବକ୍ତା ଅଭିମାନ କରୁଛନ୍ତି।

gratitude-and-independence

Literal meaning

I pulled you from the river; now your hollow cheeks have filled out and you answer me back.

What it means

The speaker resents defiance from someone previously helped. The rescue creates the contrast between former dependence and present confidence.

When you might use it

A mentor complains when a former trainee openly challenges a decision. (New editorial example, not a collected quotation.)

Cultural context

Editorial interpretation of an unglossed witness. Receiving help does not remove a person’s right to disagree.
