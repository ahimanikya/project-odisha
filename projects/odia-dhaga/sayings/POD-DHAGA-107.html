SAYING 107 · proverbial saying

A small search uncovers something larger

ଆଳୁ ଖୋଳୁ ଖୋଳୁ ମହାଦେବ ବାହାରିଲେ

Link to this saying

ସାଧାରଣ କିଛି ଖୋଜୁ ଖୋଜୁ ଅପ୍ରତ୍ୟାଶିତ ଭାବେ ବଡ଼ କଥା ବାହାରି ପଡ଼ିବା। ଛୋଟ ଖୋଜାରୁ ବଡ଼ ଆବିଷ୍କାର—ଏହି ବିପରୀତତାରେ କଥାଟିର ମଜା।

languageinterpretation

Literal meaning

While digging for tubers, Mahadeva appeared.

What it means

An ordinary investigation unexpectedly brings a much larger matter to light. This explanation follows the contrast in scale; the dictionary does not provide an origin tale.

When you might use it

Checking one unexplained receipt reveals a much larger error in the accounts. (New editorial example, not a collected quotation.)

Cultural context

The source labels it ଢଗ under appearing or becoming visible. Mahadeva is Shiva; no particular excavated shrine, village or historical discovery is verified.
