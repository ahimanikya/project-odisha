A short, repeatable field sheet for the Bhubaneswar–Raghurajpur–Puri trail. Complete one record per claim. No bookings or outreach have been made.

HOW TO USE
1. Record what you observed separately from what a notice or person told you.
2. A past visit does not establish current opening, availability or policy. Record the date and scope.
3. Keep private contact details and permission documents out of public GitHub issues. Use only public evidence and agreed public credit.
4. Leave unknown answers blank. Note conflicting information rather than replacing the earlier evidence.
5. The Founder reviews proposed guide wording before publication; receiving a report does not make it verified.
