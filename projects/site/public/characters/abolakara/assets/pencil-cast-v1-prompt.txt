Use case: illustration-story. Asset: monochrome graphite character concept for Abolakara and his travelling learned master, Saanta, for an Odia children's heritage story. These are interpretations of traditional characters, not portraits of historical people. Landscape 3:2 working study on warm ivory paper. Main scene occupies two thirds: Abolakara, a lean late-teen/young adult South Asian male with warm expressive face, broad eyebrows, short untidy wavy black hair, no moustache, in a plain knee-length wrapped dhoti and a simple short-sleeved loose cotton upper garment, carrying a small rolled sleeping mat and cloth bundle, has stopped on a village path and turns with an urgent question. Saanta, a wiry older South Asian male with receding grey hair, a short small grey moustache, lightly lined face, ordinary long cotton dhoti and plain upper cloth across his shoulder, holds a folded traditional bamboo-handled cloth umbrella and turns back to listen with mild patient surprise. No glasses, crowns, jewellery, ornate fabrics or theatrical guru costume. Both wear simple sandals. Physical height close: the older man is slightly taller, this is not a small child and a giant adult. On the remaining third add two small unlabelled head studies, Abolakara impatiently inquisitive and Saanta considering an answer. Loose pencil construction lines, selective shading, clear expressive 2D cartoon proportions, full feet visible. Human Natural: believable weight of the rolled mat, off-centre figures engaged with each other, very spare roadside ground and a little tree shade, no landmark collage. No colour, lettering, speech bubbles, frame, signatures or watermark.
