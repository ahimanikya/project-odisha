SOURCE NOTES AND CREATIVE BOUNDARIES
17 October 2026

S1 — ABOLAKARA
Kailash Pattanaik, "Abolkara: The Odyssey of a Disobedient Hero", Lokaratna,
Volumes V & VI (2013), PDF pages 143–145.
https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/8920e957-babc-45d4-9981-6b8bbe821a5b/content#page=143
Supports: the inquisitive, disobedient companion; questions prompting a
travelling master's stories; later literary transformations, including stories
by Manoj Das. Do not reduce the character to a foolish servant.
This source informed the discussion before the present concept briefs.

S2 — COLLECTED ABOLAKARA STORIES
Black Eagle Books, Abolakara Kahani, Abhay Charan Mohanty.
https://www.blackeaglebooks.org/product/abolakara-kahani/
Supports: publisher's account of the Santa/master frame and Mohanty's
collection in three parts. A named compilation is distinct from oral tradition.
The user's Internet Archive/Odia Bibhaba availability claim was not verified
against a specific accessible item; do not fabricate a download link.

S3 — ODIA NAME AND TRANSLATION CONTEXT
Prateek Pattanaik, Abolakara translation project.
https://pattaprateek.com/abolakara/
Supports: Odia spelling ଅବୋଲକରା and an oral, humorous storytelling tradition.
No story wording or illustrations from this project are being reused.

S4 — DHARMAPADA LEGEND
Odisha Review, December 2015, "The Legend" in the Konark discussion, pp. 9–12.
https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/orissareview/2015/Dec/engpdf/9-12.pdf
Supports: the familiar account of the twelve-year-old son of Bisu Maharana,
the temple's final stone and the legend's tragic ending. The account is
explicitly presented as legend, not verified biography.

S5 — DHARAMA / DHARMAPADA VARIANTS
Odisha Review, September–October 2021, section D3, "Legend about Dharmapada".
https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/orissareview/2021/Sept-Oct/engpdf/entire_book.pdf
Supports: the names Dharama and Dharmapada and discussion of the tale as a
legend. It is not evidence for a precise historical appearance or costume.

OUR PROPOSALS
All new names, family relationships, ages, occupations, appearances, series
titles and story outlines in this folder are creative working choices. No
existing author's story is being copied, translated or relabelled. Broad
themes are recorded as inspiration rather than claimed as newly invented.
No claim is made that common names or story motifs are exclusive to this work.

LANGUAGE STATUS
Odia names and working titles are first drafts. English descriptions are
development prose, not final translations of a completed Odia story. Future
scripts will be written in Odia with a companion English version and a
separate review record. Independent fluent-reader review has not occurred.
