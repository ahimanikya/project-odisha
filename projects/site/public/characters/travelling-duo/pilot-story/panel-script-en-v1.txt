The Two Endings

1.
Sunday afternoon. A story gathering in the village courtyard.
Madhab Kaka: The gardener set off to find his missing hat…

2.
Madhab Kaka: Oh! The last page is missing!
Benu: What happened next?

3.
Madhab Kaka: Perhaps the hat was in a tree…
Benu: Is that what the book says?
Madhab Kaka: No. I can’t remember. I’m guessing.

4.
Madhab Kaka: How would you end the story?
Girl: A bird had made a nest in the hat!
Boy: In mine, the wind blew it onto a scarecrow!

5.
Benu: These are our two endings. We still don’t know the book’s!
Madhab Kaka: We’ll ask the person who lent me the book.

6.
Madhab Kaka: Our story grew a little today!
Benu: So did my list of questions!
