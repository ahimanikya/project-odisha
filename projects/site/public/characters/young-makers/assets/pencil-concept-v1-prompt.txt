Use case: illustration-story. Make a first GRAPHITE PENCIL-STYLE concept board for THREE new original characters in an Odia children's workshop story. Wide landscape 3:2, plain ivory background, clear expressive 2D cartoon construction, simple faces and searching pencil lines with sparse grey shading. Contemporary fictional coastal Odisha family and neighbour; no historical or ritual costumes, temple ornament, royal dress or imitation of existing characters.
Top two-thirds: three complete figures with clear relative heights and plenty of whitespace. DINU MAMU, man 39, centre: sturdy upright adult, wavy short black hair, modest small moustache, friendly broad face, everyday collared work shirt with rolled sleeves, loose work trousers, sandals, a folded wiping cloth tucked at his waist. Clear wheel/axle construction, rounded cardboard wheels and a flat rectangular tray body. No power tools, no lettering on the drawing board, no speech bubbles, names, labels, numbers, signature or watermark.
