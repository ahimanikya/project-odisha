# Heritage walks to look for in Odisha

Checked 19 October 2026. Times are Indian Standard Time. Prices and departures can change.

40 official Odisha Walks listings, 3 current Ekamra event types, 8 community chapters and 9 advertised private heritage services. Products overlap geographically. This is a researched directory, not a census or 60 guaranteed operating departures. Nature excursions are separately labelled.

## Start with your travel date

Khordha advertises a free 25 October walk with phone registration. Ekamra Living Temple City showed a ticket selector for 21 October. The checked 20 October Dhauli event was sold out. Reopen the event before arranging travel. These observations are not reservations.

### Best time to walk

Odisha Tourism recommends October–March broadly. For outdoor city walks prefer a cool morning; follow actual scheduled times for food, museums and working craft stops. Forest access is seasonal and must be checked independently.

### How to book

Open the provider link, select or confirm a date, resolve the specific notes, then ask for the final price and written inclusions. A listed price is not a seat reservation. Book Odisha pages displayed no tickets for their selected date during inspection; that does not establish availability on other dates.

### What to confirm

Confirm language, walking distance, steps, wheelchair or mobility access, toilets, drinking water, child policy, transport and return point, admissions, food, GST, minimum group, weather cancellation and refund terms. Unknown items are not included by default.

### On the walk

Wear comfortable shoes and modest clothing; carry water and sun protection. Temple rules apply independently of a tour ticket. Ask before photographing people, rituals or artisans. Avoid obstructing worship, funerals and residents.

### Coverage and unverified routes

No current guided departure was verified for Sonepur/Sonpur/Subarnapur. Keep the proposed Sonepur route separate from bookable products. Older Ekamra free-walk pages and past community events are historical references. Other private operators may exist; this list is not exhaustive.

### Upcoming winter programme

A 15 October Times of India report announced an Ekamra winter programme for 1 November 2026–28 February 2027. Extra themes in that announcement are not represented here as bookable without dated event pages.
