# What a stone anchor at Manikapatna tells us about Odisha and Gujarat

*By Ahimanikya Satapathy · 27 November 2026*

A broken stone anchor turned up beside a mosque at Manikapatna, on the shore of Chilika. Archaeologists found it during explorations in 2018–19, among stone fragments unearthed when workers dug foundations for a mausoleum. The anchor still had its two lower holes, one of them filled with sand. The upper section, where a rope would have passed through the stone, had broken away. What surprised the researchers was the rock from which someone had cut it.<sup id="find-note"><a href="#study-note" aria-label="Footnote 1">1</a></sup>

## Where did the stone come from?

The anchor was made of basalt, a rock formed when lava cools. Small cavities in the stone marked places where gas bubbles had been trapped. Sila Tripati and colleagues studied the rock under a microscope, measured its chemical elements and compared the ratios of strontium and neodymium isotopes with those in known rock formations. Their 2022 paper identified a lava flow near **Palitana**, in Gujarat’s Saurashtra region, as the most likely source.<sup id="rock-note"><a href="#study-note" aria-label="Footnote 1">1</a></sup>

Put Palitana and Manikapatna on a map and the scale of the finding becomes clear. The stone in this medieval anchor on India’s eastern coast most likely came from the west. Its chemistry helps explain where the material began its journey. The anchor bears no record of the ship that carried it, the ports where the crew called or the cargo they took aboard.<sup id="connection-note"><a href="#study-note" aria-label="Footnote 1">1</a></sup>

## What people remember on the river

On Kartika Purnima, families float small boats in the observance of Boita Bandana. In Cuttack, Bali Jatra commemorates voyages and exchanges with Southeast Asia. The festival keeps those journeys in public memory. The Manikapatna stone answers a different question about the seafarers’ world, tracing a material connection between two shores of India.<sup id="festival-note"><a href="#culture-note" aria-label="Footnote 2">2</a></sup>
