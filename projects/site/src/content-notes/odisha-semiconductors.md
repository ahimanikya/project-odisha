# Inside Odisha’s chip industry: three projects, three milestones

*Project Odisha · 27 November 2026*

A fast charger needs electronics that can switch power efficiently. A computer needs connections that let its chips work together. Two projects at Info Valley in Odisha’s Khordha district address those different problems. A third project, from RIR Power, is testing equipment for a step that comes before a finished power device reaches a customer.

## SiCSem has started civil work

The Union Cabinet approved SiCSem’s silicon-carbide project on 12 August 2025. Silicon carbide is a material used in devices that handle electrical power, including in vehicle chargers and solar inverters. The project would give manufacturers a domestic source for devices used in those systems. On 3 November 2026, SiCSem’s parent company, Archean Chemical Industries, reported that civil work had begun and the first consignments of equipment had started shipping from their points of dispatch. [Cabinet approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2155459&lang=1&reg=20) · [Archean earnings call](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ARCHEAN_06112026185750_Investor_Transcript_sd.pdf)

## 3D Glass is working on how chips connect

Advanced packaging brings semiconductor components together so signals and power can pass between them. The Heterogeneous Integration Packaging Solutions project, promoted by 3D Glass Solutions, reached groundbreaking on 19 April 2026. The electronics ministry put the investment at ₹1,943.53 crore and gave two target dates. Commercial production was targeted for August 2028, with full-volume production targeted for August 2030. The distinction matters because the start of production and the ability to supply at scale are separate milestones. [Ministry account of the groundbreaking](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2253549&lang=1&reg=3)

## RIR Power has installed reactors for wafer processing

RIR’s reactors are designed to grow a controlled layer of silicon carbide on a wafer. That layer matters to the electrical characteristics of the power devices subsequently made from it. On its 27 October 2026 earnings call, RIR reported that the reactors had been installed and were undergoing internal testing. Management said building and fire clearances were pending. The November or December start it discussed depended on those clearances. [RIR earnings call, pages 3, 5 and 12](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/RIRPOWER_03112026153038_Covering_letter_Transcript_of_Concall.pdf)

## What would progress look like for Odisha?

For a technician in Khordha, a working production line could mean a job maintaining equipment or testing wafers. For a local supplier, a repeat order would show that it can meet a plant’s quality and delivery requirements. As these projects advance, three questions will help readers judge their impact. Which lines have been commissioned? What products have customers qualified and received? How many people and local businesses are doing the work?
