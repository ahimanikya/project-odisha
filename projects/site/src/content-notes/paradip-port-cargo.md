# What 156 million tonnes at Paradip means for Odisha

*Project Odisha · 27 November 2026*

A ship can leave Paradip, unload at another Indian port and complete its journey without crossing an international border. Such journeys account for a substantial part of the work at Odisha’s largest cargo gateway. In FY2025–26, Paradip handled **156.45 million tonnes** of cargo. Of that total, **65.81 million tonnes** moved along the coast. That is roughly 42 tonnes in every 100. [Paradip Port Authority’s year-end report](https://paradipport.gov.in/all_news/paradip-port-registers-historic-high-cargo-handling-in-fy-25-26-best-ever-in-its-history/)

## How much more cargo moved through Paradip?

In FY2024–25 the port handled 150.41 million tonnes. A year later it handled an additional **6.04 million tonnes**, a rise of about **4.0%**. A longer view puts that increase in perspective. In FY2015–16, Paradip handled 76.39 million tonnes. The annual volume more than doubled over the following decade. [Port authority’s annual figures](https://paradipport.gov.in/major-achievements/) · [Shipping ministry’s historical figures](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1513070)

## Why it matters beyond the waterfront

Consider a power station receiving coal by sea. Its supply depends on several handovers: a train reaching the loading port, a berth becoming available, a vessel carrying the load and transport at the other end. A delay at any handover can leave a buyer waiting even when the port handles a record volume over the year. Coastal shipping gives producers and buyers another way to move heavy loads between Indian markets. Paradip’s figures show how much of that work already passes through Odisha.

## What would that mean for people in Odisha?

Tonnage tells us the weight of goods loaded and unloaded. A tonne of cargo passing through Paradip can serve a business elsewhere in India. To understand how much of the benefit stays in Odisha, we would need different evidence. Can a local transporter count on shorter waits? Are equipment maintenance contracts going to local firms? Are more workers finding steady, skilled jobs?

The cargo figures establish Odisha’s place in eastern India’s freight network. Following a shipment inland, or a maintenance contract back to the firm doing the work, would tell the next part of the story.
