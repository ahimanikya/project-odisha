A photographic memory · February 2009

Bhubaneswar’s
painted streets.

On these walls, Odisha remembers in public.

“I did capture a fraction of those paintings, and I am sure you will enjoy them...”

Hours on foot, a camera, and walls worth stopping for. Browse the photographs from Ahimanikya Satapathy’s February 2009 visit, from long street scenes to the smallest painted details. A leaf becomes a page; a wheel carries a memory of the sun; a wall gives a city something to think about.
