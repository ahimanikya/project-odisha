# Odisha Store working agreement

**Draft local agreement · follows Odisha Blueprint structure**

The Founder's Utkal conversation is the decision home. Human authority stays with Ahimanikya Satapathy. AI assists within the assigned task; humans never report to AI. Current assistance is the implementing assistant, not four automatically appointed personas.

Keep project knowledge in this KB, current work in its registers, and code/configuration outside the KB. Record decisions, sources, design iterations and actual checks. Reuse the organization/work/records pattern without importing another project's memberships or historical approvals. Formal version-specific adoption remains pending review.

Each product needs a reviewed sample and a filled vendor specification before being marked available. A render is not a supplier photo. The Founder approves supplier contact, purchase commitments, payments and launch. Catalogue information can live in Git; customer names, addresses, orders and payment data cannot. Treat the store's order flow and access separately from encyclopedia contributions. JML records are required if people, personas or vendors receive roles/access. No new member is created by a job description.
