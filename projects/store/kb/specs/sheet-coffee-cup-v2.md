# The Story Cup sample sheet v2

**ODS-CP-001 · v2 · Founder review candidate**

Base: Ivory body; sea-blue handle/interior - proposed.

Proposed product: 330 ml ceramic mug; straight-sided blank preferred for the story wrap. Supplier confirms actual capacity and dimensions.

Proposed artwork canvas: 200 × 85 mm.

Placement: 200 x 85 mm preliminary wrap: emblem panel x=10-85 mm, story panel x=110-190 mm. Leave the handle exclusion zone to the actual vendor template.

Process to quote: Ask for a ceramic transfer/decal proposal compatible with the chosen two-tone blank. No dishwasher/microwave claim without supporting evidence.

Supplier to confirm: Capacity, diameter/height, full printable template, handle clearance, glaze/decoration process, colour swatches and supported care/contact-use documentation.

Sample request: One decorated sample proposed showing both emblem and readable story. Check actual-size lettering and handle alignment.

Quote fields: supplier, blank SKU, MOQ, unit costs at 25/50/100, setup charges, sample cost, lead times, taxes, shipping and defect terms. These are quote bands, not orders.

See [sample pack context](sample-pack-v2.md).

## Superseded by graphical story direction

The Founder rejected the prose-on-cup approach: “That English does not make sense - We need use a graphical story”. [Sample pack v3](sample-pack-v3.md) is current. The rejected rendered/print candidate is retained privately in the KB history evidence, not served by the website.
