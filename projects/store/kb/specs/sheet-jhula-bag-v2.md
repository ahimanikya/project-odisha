# The Jhula Bag sample sheet v2

**ODS-BG-001 · v2 · Founder review candidate**

Base: Natural ecru - proposed.

Proposed product: Cotton canvas, 300 GSM; body 380 x 420 mm; 80 mm gusset; handles 650 mm total length. Proposed quote targets.

Proposed artwork canvas: 280 × 300 mm.

Placement: Centre front on the body. Top of print 60 mm below top hem; 50 mm side margins at the proposed body width. Reverse blank.

Process to quote: Two-colour screen-print proposal, sea blue and laterite. Ask for clean small gaps and a physical colour proof.

Supplier to confirm: Body/handle dimensions, fabric weight, handle attachment, seam finish, load-test method/result, print area and unit costs.

Sample request: One decorated sample proposed, with actual body/handle measurements and load-test evidence.

Quote fields: supplier, blank SKU, MOQ, unit costs at 25/50/100, setup charges, sample cost, lead times, taxes, shipping and defect terms. These are quote bands, not orders.

See [sample pack context](sample-pack-v2.md).

## Superseded by graphical story direction

The Founder rejected the prose-on-cup approach: “That English does not make sense - We need use a graphical story”. [Sample pack v3](sample-pack-v3.md) is current. The rejected rendered/print candidate is retained privately in the KB history evidence, not served by the website.
