# The Utkal T-shirt sample sheet v2

**ODS-TS-001 · v2 · Founder review candidate**

Base: Black - confirmed by Founder.

Proposed product: Relaxed crew neck; 100% combed cotton; 220 GSM; sizes S-XXL. These are quote targets, not selected stock.

Proposed artwork canvas: 240 × 260 mm.

Placement: Centre front, top of print 80 mm below the front neck seam on the proposed M sample. Confirm size grading and print clearance. Back blank.

Process to quote: Ask for a soft-hand screen-print sample and a short-run transfer alternative. Vendor to specify ivory underbase, registration and care.

Supplier to confirm: Blank SKU, composition/GSM evidence, size chart, shrinkage, wash/rub result, actual print box and unit costs.

Sample request: One M sample proposed, plus fabric and colour swatches. Founder to confirm size and spend.

Quote fields: supplier, blank SKU, MOQ, unit costs at 25/50/100, setup charges, sample cost, lead times, taxes, shipping and defect terms. These are quote bands, not orders.

See [sample pack context](sample-pack-v2.md).
