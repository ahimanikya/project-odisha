# The Utkal Water Bottle sample sheet v2

**ODS-WB-001 · v2 · Founder review candidate**

Base: Matte sea blue - proposed.

Proposed product: 750 ml, single-wall stainless steel, proposed grade 304; screw cap with replaceable seal. No insulation claim. Supplier confirms suitability.

Proposed artwork canvas: 70 × 150 mm.

Placement: 70 x 150 mm front-only print; centre on body, above base shoulder. Lower wave band stays within the front panel in this version; no unapproved wrap.

Process to quote: Ask for a multi-colour screen/transfer method compatible with the coating. Confirm smallest gaps, colour registration and abrasion durability.

Supplier to confirm: Material grade evidence, usable capacity, body/neck dimensions, coating, cap/seal, leak-test result, print box, care and unit costs.

Sample request: One decorated sample proposed. Inspect lid, seal, leak performance, finish and print alignment.

Quote fields: supplier, blank SKU, MOQ, unit costs at 25/50/100, setup charges, sample cost, lead times, taxes, shipping and defect terms. These are quote bands, not orders.

See [sample pack context](sample-pack-v2.md).

## Superseded by graphical story direction

The Founder rejected the prose-on-cup approach: “That English does not make sense - We need use a graphical story”. [Sample pack v3](sample-pack-v3.md) is current. The rejected rendered/print candidate is retained privately in the KB history evidence, not served by the website.
