# Sea & Stone — vendor sample brief v1

**Prepared for Founder review. Not sent to a vendor; not an order or production approval.**

Project: Odisha Store, under Odisha Collective. Creative direction and final decision: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Design development: AI-assisted, including built-in image generation. Initial sourcing is planned through Bangalore vendors; no supplier is appointed.

## Design intent and fixed choices

Exactly four Utkal-branded products. Only the T-shirt is black. Separate English and Odia treatments; do not combine both scripts in a single mark. The first collection proposal, **Sea & Stone**, uses a book-and-boat emblem and simple wave patterns. This title, placements, type assignments and other product colours are proposals.

| Item | Proposed finish and artwork | Proposed print canvas | Information required from supplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-shirt | Black; ivory Odia wordmark/sails, laterite hull, straw wave accent; centre front | 240 × 260 mm | Fabric composition and weight, fit/size chart, print process, underbase, wash/rub proof, printable area by size |
| Jhula bag | Natural ecru; sea emblem and English UTKAL, laterite sun/frame | 280 × 300 mm | Fabric/weight, bag and handle dimensions, seam construction, load testing, print area and reverse treatment |
| Coffee cup | Ivory with sea handle/interior; earth/sea emblem, English UTKAL, lower wave band | 200 × 85 mm | Actual mug material/capacity, two-tone blank availability, print template, handle exclusion zone, coating/process and supported care claims |
| Water bottle | Matte sea-blue; ivory Odia emblem/wordmark, straw lower wave band, laterite rule | 70 × 150 mm front study | Material/capacity, coating, lid/gasket, leak test, print circumference/template, whether wrap print is feasible and supporting care claims |

Dimensions are candidate artwork canvases, not measured product specifications. Do not scale to fit a blank without a revised proof. The bottle front artwork does not yet supply a full wrap dieline. The mug needs its actual manufacturer’s wrap template. Back views, packaging, garment labels and print separations remain to be developed after selection.

## Colour and files

Nila Sagara #1D4658; Mankada Pathara #91462F; straw #C1A263; ivory #F2E6D0. These RGB values are design references, not verified spot inks, CMYK formulas or physical material matches. Ask the supplier for available finishes and physical colour swatches. Confirm spot colours/underbase with a sample; do not invent Pantone equivalents.

SVG artwork contains outlined lettering shaped from Noto Serif Oriya Bold; no font installation is needed to render the design. Font licence is retained in the review pack. The generator and original font remain in the repository. Keep proportions and clear space; avoid stretching and do not recreate Odia lettering by eye. Outlined glyphs prevent missing-font substitution but do not replace the Founder’s language review.

Mockup photographs are AI-generated concept illustrations. They differ in symbol/letterform detail and geometry from the flat artwork studies; neither set is approved for manufacture. Review the preferred visual, reconcile it with one selected vector master and the actual supplier template, then approve a dated sample. Do not reproduce the generated lettering directly as final print artwork.

## Quote and sample request fields

For each item request supplier name, blank SKU, available colours/sizes, actual material and dimensions, MOQ, costs at 25/50/100 units, artwork/setup charges, sampling cost and lead time, production lead time, taxes/shipping, packaging, defect/reprint terms and care/testing evidence supporting advertised claims. Quantities are quote bands, not purchase commitments.

Sample review: compare mark proportions and Odia letter shapes against the approved proof; inspect colour, placement, print clarity and smallest gaps; check fit/feel or handling; request appropriate wash/rub/leak tests for the selected process. Founder records sample disposition, final unit economics and approved production version before any order.

## Handoff state

The downloadable review pack includes 13 brand vector variants, four merchandise vector studies, this brief and the original Noto font licence. Four rendered concept images are separately versioned in the Store assets. It is a design review pack, not a production-ready vendor release.
